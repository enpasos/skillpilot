Zusatzanweisung:
Create one mathematically proportional thin-layer chromatogram and one interpretation card. Draw exactly two lanes, “Probe” and “Referenz”. Put the baseline at 0,0 cm and the solvent front at 10,0 cm. Add a vertical ruler divided into ten equal intervals. Put exactly one spot in each lane at the sixth division, exactly 60 percent of the baseline-to-front height. The liquid solvent level must lie below the baseline. Label the measured spot distance “6,0 cm” and the measured front distance “10,0 cm”. Render exactly “Rf = 6,0 cm / 10,0 cm = 0,60”. State exactly: “Gleicher Rf-Wert unter gleichen Bedingungen: Hinweis auf denselben Stoff, aber kein Identitätsbeweis; weitere Bestätigung nötig.” No other spots, lanes, distances, substances, equality signs between substances, certainty symbols, decorative icons, or perspective distortion.
