Titel: Konfiguration und Konformation unterscheiden

Zusatzanweisung:
Contrast configuration and conformation with deliberately abstract, error-resistant schematics. Configuration: use a geometric tetrahedron icon whose four colored vertices are labelled A, B, C and D exactly once, with C* at the center, beside its mirrored tetrahedron. Connect the center to the four vertices only with ordinary thin lines of equal style. Do not use solid wedges, hashed wedges, dashed stereobonds, named molecules, hydrogen atoms or extra bonds. State „Umwandlung nur mit Bindungsbruch“. Conformation: use a simple C–C single-bond rotation icon with two positions and state „Drehung um die C–C-Einfachbindung, kein Bindungsbruch“. Do not draw Newman projections or count individual substituents. Summarize: configuration requires bond breaking; conformation changes by rotation without bond breaking.
