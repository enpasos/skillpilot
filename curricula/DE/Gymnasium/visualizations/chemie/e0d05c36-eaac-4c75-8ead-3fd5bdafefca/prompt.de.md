Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Konstantes Massenverhaeltnis`.
- Use one clean example only: magnesium oxide.
- Show the balanced equation `2 Mg + O2 -> 2 MgO`.
- Show the fixed mass ratio `Mg : O = 3 : 2`.
- Use continuous proportional bars, not repeated cubes, hexagons, beads, or countable pieces.

  - green magnesium bar and blue oxygen bar are separate before the arrow.
  - the product after the arrow is one joined bar labeled `MgO`, with a green section and a blue section in the same `3 : 2` length ratio.
  - Probe 2 bars are exactly twice as long as Probe 1 bars.
- Include one short rule label: `Gleiche Verbindung, gleiches Massenverhaeltnis`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not draw any countable individual pieces in the bars.
- Do not label a separate `O` on the product side.
- Do not show leftover oxygen.
- Do not write `MgO2`.
- Do not use any mass values other than `3 g`, `2 g`, `5 g`, `6 g`, `4 g`, `10 g`.
