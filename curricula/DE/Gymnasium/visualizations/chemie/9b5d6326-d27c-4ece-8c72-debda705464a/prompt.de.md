Bitte visualisiere das folgende Lernziel im einfachen Cartoon-Stil.

Titel: Dalton-Modell und Modellcharakter erläutern
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann Daltons Atomhypothese erläutern und den Modellcharakter gegenüber Gesetz und Hypothese fachlich abgrenzen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Use the attached reference image as layout reference only.
- Keep the same clean three-panel structure: `Elemente`, `Verbindung`, `Reaktion`.
- Draw only Dalton-style solid spheres; no orbit symbols, no electron shells, no nuclei, no protons, no neutrons.
- Critical correction:
  - A single blue sphere may be labeled `A`.
  - A single red sphere may be labeled `B`.
  - Never write `AB` inside an individual sphere.
  - Label `AB` only below or beside a bonded blue-red pair as a whole.
- In the reaction panel:
  - before side has exactly two blue `A` spheres and exactly two red `B` spheres.
  - after side has exactly two bonded blue-red pairs, each pair labeled `AB` outside the spheres.
  - counters remain `A:2, B:2` before and after.
- Keep the plain text strip with no icons:
  - `Gesetz: konstante Massenverhaeltnisse`
  - `Hypothese: Materie besteht aus Atomen`
  - `Modell: Kugeln als Hilfsmittel`
  - `vereinfacht, nicht massstabsgerecht`

Vermeiden:

- Do not put `AB` on a blue sphere or on a red sphere.
- Do not add a decorative atom icon.
- Do not include any second reaction example.
- Do not draw a reaction where atom numbers change.
