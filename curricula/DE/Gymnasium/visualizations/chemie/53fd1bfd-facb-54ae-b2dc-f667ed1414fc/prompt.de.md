Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.

Vermeiden:

- Do not show a saturated solution as cloudy mud.

- Do not imply unlimited solubility at fixed water amount and fixed temperature.
