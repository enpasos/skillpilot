Titel: Lithium-Ionen-Akkumulator einordnen
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann Aufbau und Funktionsweise eines Lithium-Ionen-Akkus in vereinfachter Form erläutern und Nutzungsmöglichkeiten nennen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Use case: scientific-educational
Split the visual into two explicit states using fixed labels “negative electrode” and “positive electrode”. Discharge: Li⁺ moves through electrolyte from negative graphite electrode to positive metal-oxide electrode; electrons move through the external circuit in the same overall direction and power a load. Charge: both directions reverse under an external source. Do not use fixed anode/cathode labels across both states. Show separator, electrolyte, energy storage and key safety/resource limits. No simultaneous contradictory arrows, logos or watermark.
