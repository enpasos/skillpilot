Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann den Plattenkondensator als Beispiel eines homogenen elektrischen Feldes beschreiben, die Feldstärke im Kondensator bestimmen und die Kapazität mit $C=\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r\frac{A}{d}$ berechnen sowie ihre Abhängigkeit von Geometrie und Dielektrikum erläutern.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.

- Make it visual-first: show a large parallel-plate capacitor as the main illustration.
- Left plate is positive and labelled `+`; right plate is negative and labelled `-`.
- Draw exactly five straight parallel electric field arrows between the plates, all pointing from the positive plate to the negative plate.
- Mark the plate separation with a neutral bracket labelled `d`, with no arrowheads.
- Mark plate area with a small label `A` on one plate.

- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `+`, `-`, `d`, `A`, `Dielektrikum εr`, `E = U / d`, and `C = ε0 εr · A / d`.

- Do not draw current arrows, electron-flow arrows, charging arrows, or curved field lines.

- Do not write the capacitance formula with `d` in the numerator or `A` in the denominator.
- Do not add explanatory cards, internal instructions, or production guidance.
