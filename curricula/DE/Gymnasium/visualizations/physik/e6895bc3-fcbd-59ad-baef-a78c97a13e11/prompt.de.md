Titel: Gedämpfte Schwingungen beschreiben
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann gedämpfte mechanische Schwingungen qualitativ beschreiben, den Einfluss der Dämpfung auf die Amplitude erläutern und die Dämpfung als Erweiterung der reibungsfreien Idealisierung einordnen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Gedaempfte Schwingung beschreiben`.
- Make it visual-first: show a spring-mass oscillator with a small damper or friction pad.
- Next to it, show a clean `t-s-Diagramm` with axes `t` and `s`, without arrowheads.
- Draw one oscillating curve whose peaks and troughs get smaller over time. The curve must cross the midline repeatedly and must have no arrowheads.
- Draw two smooth envelope curves around the oscillation, both approaching the midline, without arrowheads.
- Add the short note `Amplitude nimmt ab`.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `Daempfung`, `t-s-Diagramm`, `t`, `s`, and `Amplitude nimmt ab`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not draw amplitude growing over time.
- Do not draw a monotone exponential curve without oscillations.
- Do not draw force arrows, velocity arrows, acceleration arrows, energy-flow arrows, or heat arrows.
- Do not add formulas, explanatory cards, internal instructions, or production guidance.
