Titel: Elastische Stöße
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann elastische Stöße in einer Dimension mithilfe von Impuls- und kinetischer Energieerhaltung analysieren und sie fachlich von inelastischen Stößen abgrenzen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

Show a one-dimensional elastic collision with equal masses.

Title: `Elastischer Stoß`

Use two panels: `vorher` and `nachher`.

Physical situation:
- two identical carts on a straight horizontal track
- cart A and cart B each have `m = 1 kg`
- `vorher`: cart A moves right with `v_A = 2 m/s`; cart B is at rest with `v_B = 0`
- `nachher`: cart A is at rest with `v_A' = 0`; cart B moves right with `v_B' = 2 m/s`

Arrows:
- draw exactly two physical arrows in the whole image, both momentum arrows
- `vorher`: one arrow anchored at cart A, pointing right, labelled `p_A = 2 kg m/s`
- `nachher`: one arrow anchored at cart B, pointing right, labelled `p_B' = 2 kg m/s`
- do not draw force arrows

Energy and momentum check:
- `p_ges: 2 kg m/s -> 2 kg m/s`
- `E_kin: 2 J -> 2 J`
- `elastisch: Impuls und kinetische Energie bleiben erhalten`

Vermeiden:

Do not draw extra motion, force, or acceleration arrows.
Do not show the carts sticking together.
Do not show kinetic energy decreasing.
Do not make the before and after total momentum different.
Do not write negative momentum in this example.
Do not label the collision as inelastic.
Do not write audience or school labels such as `Gymnasium`, `Schule`, or `Klasse`.
