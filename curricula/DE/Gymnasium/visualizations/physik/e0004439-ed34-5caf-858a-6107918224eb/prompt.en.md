# Recording drop height and fall time

Wide landscape educational watercolor illustration for German upper-secondary physics, friendly loose colored pencil outlines on warm white paper, calm, minimal and physically realistic. A clear diptych with identical camera position, floor and vertical measuring stick planted on the floor in each panel. Left: a hand gently releases a small blue ball around waist height beside the measuring stick; just a narrow visible gap between fingers and ball, no downward push. Right: the same small blue ball at the moment its bottom first touches the floor beside the same measuring stick, identical scale, a resting hand entirely out of frame. In the slim right margin a simple, blank two-column ruled notebook hints at recording readings. Only unnumbered evenly spaced ticks on each ruler. No labels, words, numbers, arrows, formulas, clocks, trajectories, splash lines, stars or highlighted boxes. Leave the space between release and contact empty. Single ball per panel, two consecutive observations, floor at same level on both panels. The subjects are the measurement endpoints, not the model of free fall.
