Titel: Coulomb'sches Gesetz
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann die elektrische Kraft zwischen Punktladungen quantitativ mit dem Coulomb'schen Gesetz berechnen und den Zusammenhang zur Feldstärke begründen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Coulomb'sches Gesetz`.
- Make it visual-first: at least two thirds of the image area must be one clear illustration, not cards or text blocks.
- Show exactly two point charges on a horizontal line:
  - left charge: `+q1`
  - right charge: `-q2`
  - the distance between their centers is labelled `r` with a neutral dimension bracket, not an arrow.
- Draw exactly two force arrows:
  - one arrow starts at the left positive charge and points horizontally toward the right negative charge
  - one arrow starts at the right negative charge and points horizontally toward the left positive charge
  - both arrows have the same length and are labelled `F`
- Add one small formula label: `F = k · |q1 q2| / r²`.
- Add one small relation label: `E = F / q`.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `+q1`, `-q2`, `r`, `F`, `F = k · |q1 q2| / r²`, and `E = F / q`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not draw extra arrows, curved arrows, field-line fans, electron-flow arrows, current arrows, or motion trails.
- Do not show same-sign charges attracting or opposite-sign charges repelling.
- Do not use unequal force-arrow lengths.
- Do not write `r` instead of `r²` in the denominator.
- Do not add cards, notes, checklists, instructions, or production guidance.
