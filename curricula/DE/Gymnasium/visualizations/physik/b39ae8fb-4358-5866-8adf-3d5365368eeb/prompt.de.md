- Do not mix electron-current and positive-carrier conventions in one drawing.
- Do not reverse the Hall polarity for the stated positive-carrier model.
- Do not draw multiple voltmeters.
- Do not draw carrier-motion arrows, force arrows, curved arrows, decorative arrows, or application examples.
