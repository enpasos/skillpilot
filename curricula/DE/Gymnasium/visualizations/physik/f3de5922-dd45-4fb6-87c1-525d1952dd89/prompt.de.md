Titel: Erweiterte Feldlinienbilder deuten
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann Feldlinienbilder von Dipolfeld, Faraday-Käfig und Spitzeneffekt qualitativ deuten und mit Abschirmung beziehungsweise Feldverstärkung verbinden.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Feldlinienbilder deuten`.
- Use exactly three separate panels, not one causal chain.
- Panel 1 `Dipol`: show one `+` charge on the left and one `-` charge on the right. Draw exactly five electric field lines. Every field line must start at the `+` charge and end at the `-` charge. Every arrowhead must point toward the `-` charge. Field lines must not cross. Do not draw any open field lines outside the two charges.
- Panel 2 `Faraday-Käfig`: show a hollow conducting cage. Draw external field lines only outside the cage, bending around it from left to right. Inside the cage, show no field-line arrows and add `E innen = 0`.
- Panel 3 `Spitze`: show one positively charged conductor with a sharp tip labelled `+`. Draw only outward field arrows from the conductor surface. Near the sharp tip, the outward arrows must be visibly denser than on rounded parts.
- Use short labels only and keep the three panels visually separate.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `Dipol`, `+`, `-`, `Faraday-Käfig`, `E innen = 0`, `Spitze`, and `Feldlinien dichter`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not draw electrostatic field lines as closed loops.
- Do not draw field lines that start at `-` and end at `+`.
- Do not draw field lines leaving the `-` charge in the dipole panel.
- Do not draw open field lines in the dipole panel; all five lines must connect `+` to `-`.
- Do not let field lines cross.
- Do not draw field-line arrows inside the Faraday cage.
- Do not draw inward arrows into the sharp tip.
- Do not show the tip effect as a lightning strike to an unrelated object.
- Do not add long explanatory text, internal instructions, or production guidance.
