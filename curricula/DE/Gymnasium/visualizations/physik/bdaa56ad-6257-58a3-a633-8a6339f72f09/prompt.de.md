Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Innenohr: Resonanzmodell`.
- Show a simplified unrolled cochlea/basilar membrane from left to right, labeled `Basis` on the left and `Apex` on the right.
- Mark frequency-place mapping correctly:
  `hohe Frequenz` near `Basis`;
  `tiefe Frequenz` near `Apex`.
- Show three colored resonance envelopes on the membrane:
  high-frequency envelope peaking near base;
  medium-frequency envelope peaking in the middle;
  low-frequency envelope peaking near apex.
- Add a small graph card `Amplitude` versus `Ort`.
- Add a small model icon of three tuning forks or spring-mass resonators labeled `Resonanz`.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title and the labels listed above.

Vermeiden:

- Do not swap high and low frequency positions.
- Do not draw fluid-flow arrows through the cochlea.
- Do not show every point of the membrane vibrating equally.
- Do not imply that amplitude alone encodes pitch; pitch is place/frequency selective here.
- Do not make the image a text-only poster.
- Do not add internal instructions or production guidance.
