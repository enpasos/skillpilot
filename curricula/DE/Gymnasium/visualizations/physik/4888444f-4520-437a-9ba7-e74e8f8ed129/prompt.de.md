Titel: Überlagerungen unabhängiger Schwingungen qualitativ beschreiben
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann Überlagerungen unabhängiger Schwingungen qualitativ beschreiben und dabei Verstärkung, Abschwächung und Schwebungen fachlich einordnen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Schwingungen ueberlagern`.
- Use three equal graph panels with the same horizontal axis `t`.
- Panel `Verstaerkung`: draw two thin same-frequency input sine waves in phase; draw one thicker `Summe` sine wave in the same phase with about double amplitude.
- Panel `Ausloeschung`: draw two thin same-frequency input sine waves exactly opposite in phase; draw one thicker `Summe` line almost flat on zero.
- Panel `Schwebung`: draw only one smooth black `Summe` curve: a fast sine-like oscillation whose amplitude slowly grows and shrinks inside a pale dashed envelope. Do not draw input curves in this panel.
- Keep all graph curves smooth and continuous.
- Do not draw arrows between panels or inside graph panels.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, panel labels, `t`, `x1`, `x2`, `Summe`, and `Schwebung`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not draw separate stacked mini-axes for `x1`, `x2`, and `Summe`.
- Do not draw input waves in phase in the `Ausloeschung` panel.
- Do not draw the in-phase sum with smaller amplitude than the input curves.
- Do not draw the beating result as loops, bars, jagged spikes, vertical strokes, or an envelope arrow.
- Do not add propagation arrows, force arrows, decorative arrows, formulas, or long explanatory text.
