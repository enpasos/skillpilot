Zusatzanweisung:
Create a new, simpler German educational infographic. Show one coordinate system with voltage U horizontally and current I vertically. Draw exactly two realistic photovoltaic I-U curves, labelled 1000 W/m² and 600 W/m². Enforce this strict left-to-right order of their marked maximum-power points: MPP 600 W/m² lies left of MPP 1000 W/m² because its MPP voltage is lower. The 600-W/m² curve also has clearly lower short-circuit current and slightly lower open-circuit voltage. Label both MPP stars directly, not only one. Show P = U · I once with a small rectangle for the higher curve. Use no invented numeric current or voltage values, no duplicate axis captions and correct German spelling.
