Titel: Wärmeleitung, Konvektion und Wärmestrahlung unterscheiden
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann Wärmeleitung, Konvektion und Wärmestrahlung unterscheiden und passende Alltagsbeispiele fachlich zuordnen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

Show the three mechanisms of heat transfer in three equal columns.

Column 1: `Wärmeleitung`
- example: metal spoon with one end in hot tea
- one arrow inside the spoon from the hot tea end toward the cooler handle
- label on the arrow: `Energie durch den festen Stoff`

Column 2: `Konvektion`
- example: pot of water heated from below
- one simple circulation loop inside the water
- upward part labelled `warm steigt`
- downward part labelled `kühl sinkt`

Column 3: `Wärmestrahlung`
- example: sun or heat lamp warming a hand
- three wavy rays from the source to the hand
- label: `Energie ohne Stofftransport`

Vermeiden:

Do not swap the examples between the three mechanisms.
Do not draw conduction through empty space.
Do not draw convection without a fluid or gas.
Do not draw radiation as particle transport in air.
Every arrow or ray must start at the source and end at the receiver named in the label.
Do not write audience or subject labels such as `Gymnasium`, `Physik`, `Schule`, or `Klasse`.
