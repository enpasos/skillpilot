Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Allgemeine Relativitaet`.
- Use three simple panels.

- Panel `Lichtbahn gebogen`: show a light path as a smooth curved line passing near the mass and continuing onward. The light path must not hit the mass.
- Panel `Schwarzes Loch`: show a dark circular object with a ring labelled `Ereignishorizont`, with no object or light ray crossing it.
- Add two clock icons at different heights near a planet: the lower clock labelled `tiefer: langsamer`, the higher clock labelled `hoeher: schneller`.
- Do not draw force arrows, falling arrows, or orbit arrows. The light path should be a line without arrowhead.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `Masse kruemmt Raumzeit`, `Lichtbahn gebogen`, `Schwarzes Loch`, `Ereignishorizont`, `tiefer: langsamer`, and `hoeher: schneller`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not show gravity as a force arrow pulling objects down.
- Do not show light travelling into the star or planet in the bending panel.
- Do not show a black hole as an explosion.
- Do not show exact equations or unsupported numeric values.
- Do not add long explanatory text, internal instructions, or production guidance.
