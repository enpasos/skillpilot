```text
Titel: Gravitationskraft und Gravitationspotential
Beschreibung: Der Lernende versteht die Gravitationskraft als konservative Kraft, die aus einem Potential abgeleitet ist. Das wird im Rahmen der mathematischen Möglichkeiten der Oberstufe erklärt.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create a compact German infographic about gravitational force as a conservative force derived from a potential.
- Left panel: central mass `M`, object `m`, distance `r`.
- Draw exactly one physical force arrow: it starts at `m` and points toward `M`, labelled `F_G`.
- Show the potential formula: `U(r) = -G*M*m/r`.
- The minus sign in `U(r) = -G*M*m/r` is mandatory and must be clearly visible directly before `G`.
- Show the radial-force relation: `F_r = -dU/dr = -G*M*m/r^2`.
- The minus sign in `F_r = -G*M*m/r^2` is mandatory and must be clearly visible.
- Right panel: a simple potential graph with horizontal axis `r` and vertical axis `U(r)`. The curve is below zero and approaches `0` from below as `r` increases.
- In the potential graph, draw only the axes and the potential curve. Do not draw a red arrow or pointer inside the graph.
- Add note: `konservativ: Arbeit haengt nur von Anfangs- und Endpunkt ab`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not draw an outward gravitational force.
- Do not make the potential curve positive or crossing above `U=0`.
- Do not omit the minus sign in `U(r) = -G*M*m/r`.
- Do not label force as `+G*M*m/r^2` without the inward/radial negative sign.
- Do not add circular orbit arrows, velocity arrows, or decorative curved arrows.
- Do not imply that potential is the same as force.
- Do not use any arrow in the graph except ordinary axis arrowheads.
```
