Titel: Schwingungsgleichung lösen
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann die Differentialgleichung der ungedämpften Schwingung lösen und Lösungen interpretieren.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Schwingungsgleichung loesen`.
- Show a mass-spring oscillator at rest next to a clean graph.
- Graph axes: horizontal `t`, vertical `x(t)`.
- Draw a smooth sinusoidal curve centered on `0` with constant amplitude.
- Show exactly this differential equation: `x'' + omega^2*x = 0`.
- Show exactly this solution card: `x(t) = A*cos(omega*t + phi)`.
- Add a small interpretation row: `A: Amplitude`, `omega: Kreisfrequenz`, `phi: Phase`.
- The graph and formula are enough; keep the oscillator sketch direction-free.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, graph labels, equation, solution card, and interpretation row.

Vermeiden:

- Do not show damping, growing amplitude, or a curve drifting away from zero.
- Do not use a wrong sign in the differential equation.
- Keep the oscillator sketch direction-free and avoid decorative connectors.
- Do not add long explanatory text, internal instructions, or production guidance.
