Titel: Bahn- und Winkelgeschwindigkeit
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann Bahn- und Winkelgeschwindigkeit definieren, berechnen und anhand typischer Kreisbewegungen (z. B. Umlaufbewegungen) vergleichen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

Show the relation between angular speed and tangential speed for uniform circular motion.

Title: `Bahn- und Winkelgeschwindigkeit`

Main visual:
- draw one circle with center `M`
- draw one radius line from `M` to point `P`, labelled `r = 2 m`
- draw exactly one physical arrow: a tangent arrow anchored at point `P`, perpendicular to the radius, labelled `v = 6 m/s`
- show a small filled angle sector at the center labelled `Δphi = 3 rad`, without any arrowhead on the sector
- show `Δt = 1 s`
- the circular path must have no arrowhead anywhere

Formula box:
- `omega = Δphi / Δt`
- `omega = 3 rad/s`
- `v = omega * r = 6 m/s`

Vermeiden:

Do not draw a radial velocity arrow.
Do not draw a force arrow.
Do not draw an arrowhead on the circular path or angle sector.
Do not make the tangent arrow point toward or away from the center.
Do not make the radius label inconsistent with the calculation.
Do not write `v = omega / r`.
Do not confuse `omega` with `v`.
Do not write audience or school labels such as `Gymnasium`, `Schule`, or `Klasse`.
