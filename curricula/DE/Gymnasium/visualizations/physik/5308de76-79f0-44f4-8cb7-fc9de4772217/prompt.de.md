```text
Titel: Druck in Flüssigkeiten und Gasen qualitativ beschreiben
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann Druck als Kraft pro Fläche qualitativ beschreiben und Beispiele für Druck in Flüssigkeiten und Gasen fachlich zuordnen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

Show pressure qualitatively as force distributed over an area, with one liquid example and one gas example.
Use three panels.

Panel 1:
- title `Druck = Kraft pro Fläche`
- formula `p = F/A`
- two blocks with the same force `F`: one on a large contact area labelled `kleinerer Druck`, one on a small contact area labelled `größerer Druck`
- vertical force arrows point downward onto the contact areas

Panel 2:
- title `Flüssigkeit`
- water container with two depth points
- upper point labelled `geringerer Druck`
- lower point labelled `größerer Druck`
- no horizontal water-flow arrow

Panel 3:
- title `Gas`
- closed gas container with particles
- particles touch the walls
- label `Teilchenstöße an der Wand erzeugen Druck`
- no red arrows around the container
- optional tiny black contact ticks only at particle-wall contact points

Vermeiden:

Do not show pressure as a substance flowing through the liquid.
Do not show the upper point in water as having greater pressure than the lower point.
Do not draw force arrows upward in the contact-area panel.
Do not draw any external arrows around the gas container.
Do not draw red arrows on the gas container wall.
Do not use `p=F* A`; the correct formula is `p = F/A`.
Do not write audience or subject labels such as `Gymnasium`, `Physik`, `Schule`, or `Klasse`.
```
