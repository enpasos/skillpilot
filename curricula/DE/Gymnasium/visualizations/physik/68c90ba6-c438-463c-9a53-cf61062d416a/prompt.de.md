```text
Titel: Bezugssysteme und Superpositionsprinzip
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann geeignete Bezugssysteme für Bewegungen wählen und das Superpositionsprinzip für Bewegungs- und Kraftüberlagerungen qualitativ erklären.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

Show superposition of motions in a chosen reference frame using a vector triangle.

Title: `Bezugssysteme und Superposition`

Main visual:
- draw a simple river grid from above with a small boat, but keep the main statement in a vector triangle
- vector triangle:
  - blue arrow from point O to the right, labelled `v_Boot relativ Wasser`
  - green arrow starts at the blue arrow head and points upward, labelled `v_Wasser relativ Ufer`
  - red resultant arrow starts exactly at point O and ends exactly at the green arrow head, labelled `v_Boot relativ Ufer`
- the red resultant must close the vector triangle

Formula box:
- `v_gesamt = v_1 + v_2`
- `Bezugssystem festlegen: Ufer oder Wasser`
- `Superposition: Teilbewegungen addieren`

Vermeiden:

Do not draw extra arrows outside the vector triangle.
Do not start the resultant arrow at the wrong point.
Do not end the resultant arrow before or beyond the vector sum endpoint.
Do not make the resultant parallel to only one component.
Do not mix velocity arrows with force arrows.
Do not write audience or school labels such as `Gymnasium`, `Schule`, or `Klasse`.
```
