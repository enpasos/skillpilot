- Keine Drittanbieterlogos, keine Arbeitsblatt-Kopie, keine geschützten Figuren.

- Beschriftungen und mathematische Schreibweisen müssen fachlich korrekt und auch in kleiner Darstellung lesbar sein.

Titel: Gaußsches Verfahren anwenden
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann das Messverfahren von Gauß (Ablenkungsmethode in Hauptlage und Schwingungsmethode) zur Bestimmung der Horizontalintensität des Erdmagnetfeldes beschreiben und anwenden.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Gaußsches Verfahren anwenden`.
- Make it visual-first: show a tabletop experiment for measuring the horizontal component of Earth's magnetic field.
- Show a simple compass disk with a red-blue capsule needle, a bar magnet on a straight ruler in the main position, and a simple analog stopwatch or oscillation setup nearby.
- Compass and magnet needles must be plain red-blue capsule or rectangle shapes, not arrow-shaped. Every labelled needle must have exactly one `N` on its north end and exactly one `S` on its south end.
- The compass disk must be plain: no circular arrows, no white arrows, no field-line decorations, and no compass rose letters except the needle-end labels `N` and `S`.
- The stopwatch must be analog with simple hands or ticks only: no play button, no triangle icon, no arrow icon.
- Add exactly three short labels near the apparatus:
  - `Ablenkungsmethode`
  - `Schwingungsmethode`
  - `BH bestimmen`
- You may show a small angle arc at the compass labelled `φ`; the arc must have no arrowhead.
- Do not draw any standalone arrows: no field arrows, no force arrows, no torque arrows, no motion arrows, no direction arrows on or near the bar magnet, no callout arrows, and no arrowheads in small icons. The optional `φ` angle arc is allowed only as a plain arc without an arrowhead.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, the three listed labels, `φ`, and simple compass directions `N` and `S`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not draw force arrows, torque arrows, current arrows, field arrows, direction arrows, or curved rotation arrows.
- Do not draw a compass needle pointing inconsistently with its labelled `N` and `S` ends.
- Do not draw extra compass rose letters, duplicate `N` or `S` labels around one needle, Earth/globe icons, decorative field-line icons, circular arrows, or user-interface icons.
- Do not show multiple conflicting field directions.
- Do not add formulas, internal instructions, or production guidance.
