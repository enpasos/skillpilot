Create a three-part qualitative infographic about charge separation, voltage measurement, and a capacitor as charge storage.

- wires must have no arrowheads
- there must be no direct wire between the `+` plate and the `-` plate except through the voltmeter leads

Do not draw current arrows.
Do not draw electron arrows.
Do not draw sparks, lightning, radiation symbols, or explosive effects.
Do not show electrons crossing the empty space between capacitor plates.
Do not draw a short-circuit wire directly connecting the two capacitor plates.
Do not draw only one connection to the voltmeter; the voltmeter must have two leads.
Do not label the capacitor as a battery.
Do not mix `+` and `-` charges on the same capacitor plate.
