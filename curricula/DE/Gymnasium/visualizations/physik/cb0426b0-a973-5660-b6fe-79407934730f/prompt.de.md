Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Kernzerfall und Strahlung`.
- Use three clean panels: `Zerfall`, `Abschirmung`, `Halbwertszeit`.
- In `Zerfall`, show one unstable nucleus emitting exactly three labeled outgoing signals: `alpha`, `beta-`, and `gamma`. All arrows must start at the nucleus and point outward.
- Draw `alpha` as a small cluster of two proton-like and two neutron-like dots, `beta-` as one small electron-like dot, and `gamma` as a wavy ray. Do not use detailed isotope equations.
- In `Abschirmung`, show three horizontal rays from left to right: `alpha` stops at `Papier`, `beta-` passes paper and stops at `Alu`, `gamma` passes paper and aluminum and becomes weaker after `Blei`.
- In `Halbwertszeit`, do not use jars or loose piles. Show three simple rectangular count fields with exact dot grids for undecayed nuclei: first field `16` as a 4 by 4 grid, second field `8` as a 2 by 4 grid, third field `4` as a 2 by 2 grid. The visible dot count must exactly match the number below it.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, the three panel headings, `alpha`, `beta-`, `gamma`, `Papier`, `Alu`, `Blei`, `16`, `8`, `4`, and `t`, `2t`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not draw any arrow into the nucleus.
- Do not show `gamma` as a material particle ball.
- Do not fully stop `gamma` at lead; show attenuation only.
- Do not label beta radiation as `beta+` here.
- Do not draw a half-life picture if the dots cannot be counted exactly as 16, 8, and 4.
- Do not add long explanatory text, internal instructions, or production guidance.
