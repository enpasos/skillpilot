Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not imply that shielding increases dose.
- Do not draw curved or reflected radiation paths unless they are clearly absorbed at the shield.
- Do not use a fake numerical dose calculation with arbitrary numbers.
- Do not mix up `Gy` and `Sv`.

- Do not add long explanatory text, internal instructions, or production guidance.
