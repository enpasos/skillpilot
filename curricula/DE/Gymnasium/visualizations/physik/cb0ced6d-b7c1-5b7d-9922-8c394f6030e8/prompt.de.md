Titel: Harmonische Wellen und ihre Größen
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann harmonische Wellen erzeugen und beschreiben, charakteristische Größen wie Ausbreitungsgeschwindigkeit, Wellenlänge und Frequenz angeben und den Zusammenhang $v = \lambda \cdot f$ nutzen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Harmonische Wellen und Groessen`.
- Make it visual-first: show a horizontal rope or water wave with one clean sinusoidal wave shape.
- Draw exactly one propagation arrow above the wave pointing to the right, labelled `Ausbreitung v`.
- Mark the wavelength `lambda` with a neutral horizontal bracket from one crest to the next crest; the bracket must have no arrowheads.
- Mark the amplitude `A` with a neutral vertical bracket from the midline to a crest; the bracket must have no arrowheads.
- Show a small oscillator at the left labelled `Quelle f`.
- Add the formula `v = lambda * f`.
- Use axes or midline without arrowheads.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `Ausbreitung v`, `lambda`, `A`, `Quelle f`, and `v = lambda * f`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not mark wavelength from crest to trough or from zero crossing to crest.
- Do not mark amplitude peak-to-peak.
- Do not draw multiple propagation arrows or ambiguous diagonal arrows.
- Do not draw a damped wave, standing wave, shock wave, or square wave.
- Do not add explanatory cards, internal instructions, or production guidance.
