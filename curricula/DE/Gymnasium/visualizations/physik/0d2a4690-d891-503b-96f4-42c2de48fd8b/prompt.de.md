Titel: Physik der Musikinstrumente
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann erklären, wie die Randbedingungen die möglichen stehenden Wellenmoden in Saiten und Luftsäulen festlegen und dadurch die möglichen Töne bestimmen, und den Zusammenhang zwischen Instrumentenlänge, möglichen Wellenlängen und Frequenzen qualitativ ableiten.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Toene durch stehende Wellen`.
- Use two visual panels:
  - Panel `Saite`: show one guitar string fixed at both ends with exactly one smooth standing-wave loop; label it `L = lambda/2`.
  - Panel `Rohr`: show one long open organ pipe or flute tube with a smooth standing-wave curve inside; label it `langes Rohr: tief`.
- Add a small comparison with two simple drawings: a long string labelled `lang: tief` and a shorter string labelled `kurz: hoch`.
- Add the relation `f = v / lambda` once, outside the drawings.
- Use no physical direction arrows. The image should communicate stationary standing waves and length-frequency relation through shapes and labels.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `Saite`, `Rohr`, `L = lambda/2`, `langes Rohr: tief`, `lang: tief`, `kurz: hoch`, and `f = v / lambda`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not use the word `Luftsaeule`, `Luftsäule`, or any variant with the letter `æ`.
- Do not draw travelling wave arrows inside the instruments.
- Do not show nodes in the middle of the one-loop fixed string.
- Do not claim that a longer string gives a higher tone.
- Do not draw an air column as random zigzag or sound rays.
- Do not add explanatory cards, internal instructions, or production guidance.
