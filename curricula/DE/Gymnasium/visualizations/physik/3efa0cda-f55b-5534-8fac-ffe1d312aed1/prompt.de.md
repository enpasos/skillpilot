Titel: Erzwungene Schwingungen und Resonanz

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Erzwungene Schwingung und Resonanz`.
- Make it visual-first: show a spring-mass oscillator on the left connected to a small motor shaker labelled `Erreger`.
- Draw exactly one small driving arrow from the motor toward the spring, labelled `Anregung`; this is the only physical arrow in the apparatus.
- On the right, show a resonance graph with horizontal axis `f_Erreger` and vertical axis `Amplitude`, both without arrowheads.

- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `Erreger`, `Anregung`, `f_Erreger`, `Amplitude`, `Eigenfrequenz`, `geringe Daempfung`, and `starke Daempfung`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not draw the resonance peak at zero frequency.
- Do not draw amplitude decreasing monotonically with no resonance peak.
- Do not draw extra force arrows, velocity arrows, energy-flow arrows, circular arrows, or process arrows.
- Do not add formulas, explanatory cards, internal instructions, or production guidance.
