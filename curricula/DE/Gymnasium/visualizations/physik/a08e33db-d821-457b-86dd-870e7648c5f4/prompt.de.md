Titel: Einstein-Synchronisation erläutern
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann die Einstein-Synchronisation mit Lichtsignalen erläutern und ihre Bedeutung für Zeitmessung und Gleichzeitigkeit in der speziellen Relativitätstheorie beschreiben.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Einstein-Synchronisation`.
- Show two distant clocks labelled `Uhr A` and `Uhr B` at rest on one horizontal line.
- Show a light pulse sent from `A` to `B` and reflected back from `B` to `A`.
- Draw exactly two straight light-path arrows: one from `A` to `B`, one from `B` to `A`.
- Label the send event at A as `t1`, the return event at A as `t3`, and the arrival/reflection event at B as `tB`.
- Add one formula card exactly as: `tB = (t1 + t3)/2`.
- Add one small label exactly as: `gleiche Lichtlaufzeit hin und zurück`.
- Keep all arrows attached to the light pulse path between the clocks; no extra decorative arrows.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `Uhr A`, `Uhr B`, `Lichtsignal`, `t1`, `tB`, `t3`, `tB = (t1 + t3)/2`, and `gleiche Lichtlaufzeit hin und zurück`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not draw curved light paths.
- Do not draw more than the two light-path arrows.
- Do not place `tB` at the return event on clock A.
- Do not imply instant communication or sound signals.
- Do not write `tB = t3 - t1`.
- Do not add long explanatory text, internal instructions, or production guidance.
