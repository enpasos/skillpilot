Titel: Zeitentwicklung von Quantenzuständen einordnen
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann erklären, dass die zeitliche Entwicklung eines Quantenzustands im Modell deterministisch beschrieben wird, während Messergebnisse probabilistisch gedeutet werden.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Quantenzustand: Zeit und Messung`.
- Use two clearly separated panels.
- Left panel `Modell`: show a smooth wave-packet or state icon at `t0` and a related state icon at `t1`, connected by one clean time arrow labelled `eindeutige Entwicklung`.
- Right panel `Messung`: show one detector looking at the state and three possible outcome cards `A`, `B`, `C`.
- Under the outcome cards, show a small bar chart labelled `Wahrscheinlichkeit`; use three different bar heights, not three equal bars.
- Add one compact formula card exactly as: `psi(t0) -> psi(t1)`.
- Add one compact note exactly as: `Messwert: zufällig nach P`.
- Keep the scene visual-first: state evolution on the left, measurement distribution on the right.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `Modell`, `Messung`, `t0`, `t1`, `eindeutige Entwicklung`, `A`, `B`, `C`, `Wahrscheinlichkeit`, `psi(t0) -> psi(t1)`, and `Messwert: zufällig nach P`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not show a tiny particle following one definite classical path.
- Do not claim that the measurement result is determined before measurement.
- Do not show all outcomes as equally likely unless the bars are explicitly meant to be equal; here they must be unequal.
- Do not draw random arrows inside the left time-evolution panel.
- Do not add wavefunction-collapse formulas, long explanatory text, internal instructions, or production guidance.
