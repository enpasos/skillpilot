```text
Titel: Braunsche Röhre (Oszilloskop)
Beschreibung: Anwendung der Ablenkung von Elektronen im elektrischen Feld zur Funktionsweise des Oszilloskops; magnetische Ablenkung qualitativ einordnen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Braunsche Röhre`.
- Make it visual-first: show a simplified cathode-ray tube / oscilloscope tube from left to right.
- Show an electron beam entering between two deflection plates and then hitting a screen.
- Upper deflection plate is labelled `+`, lower deflection plate is labelled `-`.
- Draw exactly three straight vertical electric-field arrows between the deflection plates, all pointing downward from `+` to `-`.
- These three electric-field arrows are the only arrows in the entire image.
- The electron beam is labelled `Elektronen`. Its path bends upward while passing through the plates, because electrons are negative and the electric force is opposite to the field direction.
- Draw the beam path as one smooth continuous glowing line without arrowheads: straight and horizontal before the deflection plates, then smoothly curving upward while it passes through and after the plates.
- On the screen, show a bright spot above the center line, labelled `Leuchtpunkt`.
- Add one small label near the plates: `Ablenkung durch E-Feld`. Place this label directly next to the plates without a pointer arrow or callout arrow.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `+`, `-`, `Elektronen`, `Leuchtpunkt`, and `Ablenkung durch E-Feld`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not draw the electron beam bending downward for the shown plate polarity.
- Do not draw field arrows from minus to plus.
- Do not draw current arrows, magnetic-field arrows, Lorentz-force arrows, or extra beam arrows.
- Do not draw label pointer arrows, callout arrows, curved arrows, or decorative arrows.
- Do not draw the electron beam as a wave, sine curve, zigzag, oscillation, or repeated waveform.
- Do not add oscilloscope scales with incorrect axes or unexplained waveforms.
- Do not add explanatory cards, internal instructions, or production guidance.
```
