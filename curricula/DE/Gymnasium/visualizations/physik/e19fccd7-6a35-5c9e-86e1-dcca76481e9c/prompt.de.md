```text
Titel: Elektrische Dipolfelder mit dem EKG verknuepfen
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann elektrische Potentiale und Aequpotentiallinien eines Dipolfeldes experimentell oder simulativ untersuchen und den Zusammenhang zu EKG-Signalen fachlich beschreiben.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Dipolfeld und EKG`.
- Use two connected visual panels.
- Left panel `Dipolfeld`: show exactly two point electrodes, one labelled `+` and one labelled `-`. Show exactly five fully visible smooth electric field lines. Each field line must start on the positive electrode and end on the negative electrode. Every arrowhead on a field line must point toward `-`.
- Do not draw any field line segment on the outer right side of the negative electrode, and do not draw any arrowhead after a line has reached the negative electrode.
- Show dashed equipotential lines labelled `Aequipotentiallinien`; they must not cross each other.
- Right panel `Messung am Koerper`: show a simplified torso outline with two measuring electrodes labelled `V1` and `V2`, plus a small ECG waveform strip labelled `EKG`.
- Between the panels, show only the label `Potentialdifferenz`, without an arrow unless the arrow clearly goes from the two measuring points to the waveform display.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title and the labels listed above.

Vermeiden:

- Do not draw field-line arrows toward the positive electrode.
- Do not draw field lines that continue beyond the negative electrode.
- Do not draw partial outer field lines at the image edges; every visible field line must have both endpoints visible.
- Do not let field lines cross each other.
- Do not show random arrows through the body.
- Do not make the ECG signal look like a diagnosis chart with extra medical claims.
- Do not add current arrows, force arrows, or charge-motion arrows.
- Do not make the image a text-only poster.
- Do not add internal instructions or production guidance.
```
