```text
Titel: Realitaetsbegriff in der Quantenfach diskutieren
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann den Realitaetsbegriff in der Quantenfach anhand von Messung, Praeparation und Wahrscheinlichkeitsaussagen fachlich diskutieren.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Messung und Realität`.
- Use a three-step visual sequence: `Präparation`, `Wahrscheinlichkeit`, `Messung`.
- In `Präparation`, show a source preparing identical small wave packets, not a definite classical ball with a marked path.
- In `Wahrscheinlichkeit`, show a soft probability cloud or distribution curve with several possible detector positions.
- In `Messung`, show one detector click as a single bright dot, plus a small inset labeled `viele Messungen` with a histogram-like distribution.
- If process arrows are used, they may only connect `Präparation` to `Wahrscheinlichkeit` to `Messung`; do not use arrows to claim a definite hidden path before measurement.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `Präparation`, `Wahrscheinlichkeit`, `Messung`, `ein Klick`, and `viele Messungen`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not show a single definite particle trajectory through the whole setup before measurement.
- Do not show a particle splitting into literal smaller particles.
- Do not add formulas, long explanatory text, internal instructions, or production guidance.
```
