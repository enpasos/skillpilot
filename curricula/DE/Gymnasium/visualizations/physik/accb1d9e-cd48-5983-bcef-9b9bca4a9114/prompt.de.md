Pflichtinhalt:

Show applying centripetal force in a numerical uniform circular motion example.

Title: `Kreisbewegung anwenden`

Main visual:
- top-down view of a mass on a string moving in a circle
- center point labelled `M`
- mass at the right side of the circle labelled `m = 2 kg`
- radius line from `M` to the mass labelled `r = 1,5 m`
- draw exactly one physical arrow starting at the mass and pointing straight to center `M`, labelled `F_z = 12 N`
- do not draw a tangent velocity arrow in the main sketch
- do not draw an arrowhead on the circular path

Calculation box:

- `v = 3 m/s`
- `F_z = 2 kg * (3 m/s)^2 / 1,5 m`
- `F_z = 12 N`

Vermeiden:

Do not draw outward force arrows.
Do not draw force arrows tangent to the circle.
Do not draw extra motion or velocity arrows.
Do not draw a circular motion arrow on the path.
Do not make the force arrow miss the center.
Do not write `F_z = m * v / r`.
Do not make the numerical result different from `12 N`.
Do not write audience or school labels such as `Gymnasium`, `Schule`, or `Klasse`.
