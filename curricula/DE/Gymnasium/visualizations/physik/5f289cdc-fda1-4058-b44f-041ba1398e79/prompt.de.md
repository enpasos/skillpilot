```text
Titel: Grundgleichung der Mechanik anwenden
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann die Grundgleichung der Mechanik $F = m \cdot a$ in einfachen eindimensionalen Situationen anwenden und Kräftegleichgewichte von beschleunigten Bewegungen abgrenzen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

Show applying `F = m * a` in one-dimensional motion.

Title: `Grundgleichung der Mechanik anwenden`

Main scene:
- show one cart on a horizontal track
- label the cart `m = 2 kg`
- draw exactly one red force arrow anchored at the cart center, pointing to the right, labelled `F = 6 N`
- draw one blue acceleration arrow anchored at the cart center, pointing to the right, labelled `a = 3 m/s^2`
- the force arrow and acceleration arrow must point in the same direction

Calculation box:
- `F = m * a`
- `a = F / m`
- `a = 6 N / 2 kg = 3 m/s^2`

Comparison note:
- `Summe F = 0 -> keine Beschleunigung`
- `Summe F = 6 N nach rechts -> a nach rechts`

Vermeiden:

Do not draw force and acceleration arrows in opposite directions.
Do not draw more than one force arrow on the cart.
Do not draw the force arrow starting away from the cart.
Do not write `a = m / F` or `F = a / m`.
Do not show balanced forces in the main calculation case.
Do not write audience or school labels such as `Gymnasium`, `Schule`, or `Klasse`.
```
