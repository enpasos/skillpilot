```text
Titel: Energieentwertung vs. Energieverbrauch
Beschreibung: Abgrenzung alltagssprachlicher "Energieverbrauch" von physikalischer Energieerhaltung und Entropieerzeugung.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

Show the difference between everyday "energy consumption" and physical energy conservation.

Title: `Energieentwertung vs. Energieverbrauch`

Main visual:
- use two side-by-side energy-account bars labelled `vorher` and `nachher`
- both bars must have exactly the same total height and be labelled `E_ges = 100 J`
- `vorher`: `nutzbare Energie = 100 J`, `thermische Energie = 0 J`
- `nachher`: `nutzbare Energie = 30 J`, `thermische Energie = 70 J`
- show a simple device such as a lamp or motor between the labels, but do not use process arrows

Explanation box:
- `Energie wird nicht verbraucht`
- `sie wird weniger nutzbar`
- `Entropie nimmt zu`

Vermeiden:

Do not show total energy decreasing.
Do not write `E_ges = 30 J` after the process.
Do not claim that energy disappears.
Do not use an arrow that suggests energy leaves the system.
Do not confuse useful energy with total energy.
Do not make the thermal-energy part smaller after the process.
Do not write audience or school labels such as `Gymnasium`, `Schule`, or `Klasse`.
```
