Bitte visualisiere das folgende Lernziel im einfachen Cartoon-Stil.

Titel: Influenz bei Leitern untersuchen
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann Influenz bei Leitern experimentell beschreiben, die Ladungsverschiebung im äußeren elektrischen Feld erklären und das Ergebnis mit Feldlinienbildern verknüpfen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Influenz im Leiter`.
- Show two large source plates: a `+` plate on the left and a `-` plate on the right.
- Draw uniform external electric-field arrows from the left `+` plate to the right `-` plate, labelled `E_außen`.
- Place one neutral metal conductor between the plates.
- On the conductor, show negative charges gathered on the left side and positive induced charge on the right side.
- Add one electron-shift arrow inside the conductor labelled `Elektronenverschiebung`; it must point left, opposite to `E_außen`.
- Add one small label inside the conductor exactly as: `E innen = 0`.
- Keep field-line arrows outside the conductor; do not draw field-line arrows through the conductor interior.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `+`, `-`, `E_außen`, `Elektronenverschiebung`, and `E innen = 0`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not put negative induced charge on the right side for a left-to-right external field.
- Do not draw the electron-shift arrow in the same direction as `E_außen`.
- Do not draw external field arrows from `-` to `+`.
- Do not show a battery circuit or current loop.
- Do not show net charge being added to the conductor.
- Do not add long explanatory text, internal instructions, or production guidance.
