Titel: Widerstand und Kennlinien einfacher Bauteile untersuchen
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann den Zusammenhang zwischen Stromstärke, Spannung und Widerstand experimentell untersuchen, Kennlinien interpretieren und Material-, Längen- und Querschnittseinflüsse qualitativ beschreiben.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Widerstand und Kennlinie`.
- Use two clear parts: left an experiment, right a graph.
- Experiment: show a safe low-voltage source, an ammeter `A` in series with a test resistor, and a voltmeter `V` parallel across the two terminals of the test resistor. Label the test resistor `Bauteil`.
- The voltmeter must not be part of the main current loop. Its two leads attach only to the left and right ends of `Bauteil`.
- Omit all current arrows in the experiment. The wiring topology and the graph are the learning focus.
- Graph: draw axes with horizontal axis `U` and vertical axis `I`.
- In the graph, show one straight line through the origin labelled `ohmscher Widerstand`.

- Add one small comparison strip with four clearly separate wire sketches:

  - a long wire labelled `lang` with `größerer R`;
  - a thick wire labelled `dick` with `kleiner R`;
  - a thin wire labelled `dünn` with `größerer R`.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `A`, `V`, `Bauteil`, `U`, `I`, `ohmscher Widerstand`, `Lampe`, `kurz`, `lang`, `dick`, `dünn`, `kleiner R`, and `größerer R`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not draw a curved ohmic-resistor line.
- Do not draw the lamp line as a straight line if it is labelled `Lampe`.
- Do not put the voltmeter in series or the ammeter parallel to the component.
- Do not draw current arrows through the voltmeter branch or through any wire.
- Do not point `kleiner R` at the long or thin wire.
- Do not point `größerer R` at the short or thick wire.
- Do not add numerical values that could conflict with the drawn graph.
- Do not add long explanatory text, internal instructions, or production guidance.
