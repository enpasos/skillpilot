```text
Bitte visualisiere das folgende Lernziel im einfachen Cartoon-Stil.

Titel: Elektrische Anlagen, Gewitter und Haushaltsstromkreise sicher beurteilen
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann Gefahren elektrischer Anlagen erläutern, Verhalten bei Gewitter erklären und Haushaltsstromkreise sicher beurteilen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

Show a safety-focused qualitative infographic with three panels.

Panel 1: `Haushaltsstromkreis`
- show socket, fuse labelled `Sicherung`, and residual-current device labelled `FI/RCD`
- include a label `Schutz schaltet bei Fehler ab`
- no exposed live wire being touched

Panel 2: `Gefahr`
- show a damaged cable or wet hand near a socket with a clear red cross
- label `nicht berühren`
- label `Strom durch den Körper ist gefährlich`

Panel 3: `Gewitter`
- show safe behavior only: one person inside a building or inside a car
- show a lone tree and a metal fence or metal object crossed out, without any person standing next to them
- label `Schutz suchen`
- label `Abstand zu Baum und Metall`

Vermeiden:

Do not show a person touching live conductors.
Do not give step-by-step repair instructions.
Do not draw current arrows through a body.
Do not show a safe person standing under a single tree.
Do not show any person standing outside in the thunderstorm panel.
Do not show any person standing next to a metal fence, metal pole, or metal object.
Do not show lightning striking a person.
Do not use arrowheads except for a simple warning pointer if unavoidable; prefer icons and red crosses.
Do not use any arrowheads in the household circuit or protection-device panel.
Do not draw green, red, or black arrows on wires, fuses, sockets, or protection devices.
Do not write audience or subject labels such as `Gymnasium`, `Physik`, `Schule`, or `Klasse`.
```
