Titel: Sonnensystem und Kepler-Gesetze
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann Aufbau und Dynamik des Sonnensystems beschreiben, Kepler’sche Gesetze anwenden und Gezeiten qualitativ erklären.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Sonnensystem und Kepler-Gesetze`.
- Use four small panels.
- Panel `1. Gesetz`: show one elliptical orbit with the Sun at one focus, not at the center. Mark the empty second focus as `zweiter Fokus`.
- Panel `2. Gesetz`: use a circular special case to make equal areas unambiguous. Show the Sun at the center of a circular orbit and exactly two quarter-circle sectors of equal area, both labelled `gleiche Zeit, gleiche Flaeche`.
- Panel `3. Gesetz`: show a small table with two rows: `a=1, T=1, T^2/a^3=1` and `a=4, T=8, T^2/a^3=1`.
- Panel `Gezeiten`: show Earth, Moon, and two tidal bulges aligned along the Earth-Moon line.
- Do not draw orbit-direction arrows or motion arrows.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `1. Gesetz`, `2. Gesetz`, `3. Gesetz`, `Sonne im Fokus`, `zweiter Fokus`, `gleiche Zeit, gleiche Flaeche`, `a`, `T`, `T^2/a^3`, and `Gezeiten`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not place the Sun at the center of the elliptical orbit in the first-law panel.
- Do not make the two second-law sectors visibly different in area.
- Do not draw orbit-direction arrows.
- Do not use a wrong third-law table; both shown ratios must equal 1.
- Do not add long explanatory text, internal instructions, or production guidance.
