Titel: Elektromagnetischen Schwingkreis analysieren
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann einen einfachen LC-Schwingkreis beschreiben, charakteristische Größen wie Periodendauer, Frequenz und Amplituden angeben und die periodische Energieumwandlung qualitativ erklären.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Elektromagnetischer Schwingkreis`.
- The entire image must be arrow-free: no arrowheads, no vector arrows, no current arrows, no electric-field arrows, no magnetic-field arrows, and no direction marks anywhere.
- Make it visual-first: show a simple LC circuit with one capacitor labelled `Kondensator` and one coil labelled `Spule`.
- Show five time snapshots in a row labelled `1`, `2`, `3`, `4`, and `1 nach T`.
- Under each snapshot, show two energy bars labelled `E_C` and `E_L`. Use only colored bar heights; do not write words such as low, high, medium, empty, or full.
- Snapshot 1: capacitor visibly charged with `+` on the top plate and `-` on the bottom plate, `E_C` high and `E_L` low.
- Snapshot 2: capacitor partly discharged, coil glow visible, `E_C` medium and `E_L` medium or high.
- Snapshot 3: capacitor uncharged, coil glow strongest, `E_C` low and `E_L` high.
- Snapshot 4: capacitor charged with opposite polarity, `-` on the top plate and `+` on the bottom plate, `E_C` high and `E_L` low.
- Snapshot `1 nach T`: same state as snapshot 1 again, showing one full period.
- Add one neutral horizontal bracket with plain vertical end marks from snapshot `1` to snapshot `1 nach T`, labelled `T`. The bracket must have no arrowheads.
- Add the relation `f = 1 / T`.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `Kondensator`, `Spule`, `1`, `2`, `3`, `4`, `1 nach T`, `E_C`, `E_L`, `T`, `f = 1 / T`, `+`, and `-`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not draw blue vertical arrows inside the capacitor.
- Do not draw orange curved arrows or field-line arrows around the coil.
- Do not draw current-direction arrows or energy-flow arrows.
- Do not draw a q(t) graph.
- Do not make both energy bars maximum at the same snapshot.
- Do not draw a battery as an active permanent source in the oscillating circuit.
- Do not add English words, explanatory cards, internal instructions, or production guidance.
