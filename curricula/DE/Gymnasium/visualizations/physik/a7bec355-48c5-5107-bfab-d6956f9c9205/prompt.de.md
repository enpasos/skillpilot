```text
Bitte visualisiere das folgende Lernziel im einfachen Cartoon-Stil.

Rahmen:
- Stil und Anspruch: klar, anschaulich und fachlich präzise; keine Schulform-, Fach- oder Publikumshinweise als Bildtext.
- Erzeuge eine klare, gut lesbare Infografik im Querformat.

- Keine Drittanbieterlogos, keine Arbeitsblatt-Kopie, keine geschützten Figuren.

Titel: Sternspektren und Temperaturgesetze auswerten
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann Fraunhoferlinien als Absorptionslinien deuten und Oberflaechentemperaturen von Sternen mit Stefan-Boltzmann-Gesetz sowie Wien-Gesetz abschaetzen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Sternspektren auswerten`.
- Show a star feeding light into a prism or spectroscope, then a spectrum strip with dark vertical lines labelled `Fraunhoferlinien`.
- Show two temperature cues:
  `Wien`: a hotter star has a peak shifted toward blue/short wavelength; a cooler star has a peak shifted toward red/long wavelength.
  `Stefan-Boltzmann`: higher `T` means much larger emitted power.
- Draw the blue peak at shorter wavelength than the red peak.
- In the `Stefan-Boltzmann` panel, compare emitted power with two side-by-side bars or halos: `hoehere T` must have the clearly larger bar/halo than `tiefere T`.
- Do not draw an arrow from the hotter star to the cooler star.
- Use axis labels `Wellenlaenge` and `Intensitaet` if a small graph appears.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title and the labels listed above.

Vermeiden:

- Do not make blue light longer-wavelength than red light.
- Do not label dark absorption lines as emission lines.
- Do not show Fraunhofer lines as random colored bars that erase the spectrum.
- Do not draw energy or light arrows from the detector back to the star.
- Do not draw comparison arrows between `hoehere T` and `tiefere T`; use bars, brightness halos, or side-by-side cards instead.
- Do not invent exact temperatures unless they are consistent with the star colors.
- Do not make the image a text-only poster.
- Do not add internal instructions or production guidance.
```
