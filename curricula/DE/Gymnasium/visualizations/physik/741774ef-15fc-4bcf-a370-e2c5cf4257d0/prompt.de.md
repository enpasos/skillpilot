Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.

- Top plate is labelled `+`, bottom plate is labelled `-`.
- Draw exactly four straight vertical electric-field arrows between the plates, all pointing downward from `+` to `-`.

- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `+`, `-`, `+q`, `Fel`, and `Fel = q · E`.

- Do not draw current arrows, electron-flow arrows, extra force arrows, or multiple trajectory arrows.
- Do not bend a positive particle upward in a downward electric field.
- Do not draw field arrows from minus to plus.
- Do not show a negative particle in this image.
- Do not add explanatory cards, internal instructions, or production guidance.
