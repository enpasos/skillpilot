Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Millikan-Versuch und Elementarladung`.
- Make it visual-first: show a large Millikan oil-drop apparatus with two horizontal capacitor plates and one oil droplet between them.
- Upper plate is labelled `+`, lower plate is labelled `-`.
- Draw exactly three straight vertical electric-field arrows between the plates, all pointing downward from `+` to `-`.
- Draw exactly two force arrows on the oil droplet:
  - `Fg` starts at the droplet and points vertically downward
  - `Fel` starts at the droplet and points vertically upward
  - both arrows have equal length to show the hovering case
- Add one small label near the droplet: `Schweben: Fel = Fg`.
- Add one small charge quantization label: `q = n · e`.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `+`, `-`, `Fg`, `Fel`, `Schweben: Fel = Fg`, and `q = n · e`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not draw extra force arrows, motion arrows, electron-flow arrows, current arrows, or oil-spray path arrows.
- Do not make the hovering droplet visibly moving up or down.
- Do not draw `Fel` downward in the hovering case.
- Do not draw unequal `Fg` and `Fel` arrows.
- Do not add explanatory cards, internal instructions, or production guidance.
