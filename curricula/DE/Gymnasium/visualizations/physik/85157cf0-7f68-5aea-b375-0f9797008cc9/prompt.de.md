Titel: Interferenz in der Technik (ANC)
Beschreibung: Active Noise Cancellation (Antischall) als destruktive Interferenz. Bewertung von Lärmschutzmaßnahmen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Antischall durch Interferenz`.
- Make it visual-first with a headphone or earbud context on one side and a clean signal diagram on the other side.
- Show two sinusoidal traces with the same amplitude and frequency:
  one labelled `Stoerschall`;
  one labelled `Antischall`.
- The two traces must be exactly opposite in phase: when one has a crest, the other has a trough at the same horizontal position.
- Below them, show their sum as a nearly flat line labelled `Summe: leiser`.
- Use axes without arrowheads and no direction arrows.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `Stoerschall`, `Antischall`, and `Summe: leiser`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not draw the two waves in phase.
- Do not draw different wavelengths for the two cancelling waves.
- Do not show the sum as a large sine wave.
- Do not draw sound-ray arrows, energy arrows, current arrows, or force arrows.
- Do not add formulas, explanatory cards, internal instructions, or production guidance.
