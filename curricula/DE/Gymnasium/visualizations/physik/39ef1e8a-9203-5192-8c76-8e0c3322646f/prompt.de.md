Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann additive Farbmischung als physiologischen Effekt des Farbsehens erklaeren und graphische Darstellungen empirischer Daten fachlich auswerten.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Farbsehen und additive Mischung`.
- Show an additive light-mixing scene on a dark screen: three overlapping circles of light labeled `Rot`, `Gruen`, and `Blau`.
- The overlap colors must be correct:
  `Rot + Gruen = Gelb`;
  `Gruen + Blau = Cyan`;
  `Rot + Blau = Magenta`;
  center overlap of all three is `Weiss`.
- Add a small eye/cone inset with three cone cells labeled `S`, `M`, `L`.
- Add one compact data-card or bar-card showing `Zapfenantworten` with three colored bars, not a complex graph.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, color labels, `additiv`, `S`, `M`, `L`, and `Zapfenantworten`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not confuse additive mixing with paint or filters.
- Do not make red plus green turn brown or dark.
- Do not make all three lights black or gray.
- Do not use subtractive labels such as `CMY`, `Druck`, or `Pigment`.
- Do not draw arrows from the eye outward.
- Do not make the image a text-only poster.
- Do not add internal instructions or production guidance.
