```text

- Keine Drittanbieterlogos, keine Arbeitsblatt-Kopie, keine geschützten Figuren.

Titel: Energie und Impuls von Photonen
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann Zusammenhänge zwischen Energie, Impuls, Frequenz und Wellenlänge von Photonen darstellen und fachlich deuten.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Photon: Energie und Impuls`.
- Show three photon wave packets side by side: `rot`, `gruen`, and `violett`.
- The red wave packet must have the longest wavelength and lowest frequency impression; the violet wave packet must have the shortest wavelength and highest frequency impression.
- Add a clean formula box with exactly: `E = h f`, `p = h / lambda`, and `c = lambda f`.
- Add two comparison labels: `groesse Frequenz -> groessere Energie` and `kleinere Wellenlaenge -> groesserer Impuls`.
- Use no ray arrows and no collision arrows.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `rot`, `gruen`, `violett`, `E = h f`, `p = h / lambda`, `c = lambda f`, `groesse Frequenz -> groessere Energie`, and `kleinere Wellenlaenge -> groesserer Impuls`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not show red photons as having more energy than violet photons.
- Do not write `E = h / f`.
- Do not write `p = h lambda`.
- Do not make the three wavelengths visually identical.
- Do not add explanatory cards, internal instructions, or production guidance.
```
