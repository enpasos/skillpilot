Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Do not include technical identifiers, filenames, watermarks, platform names, product names, or school/audience labels in the image.
- Create an appealing German cartoon learning image titled `Roentgenspektren deuten`.
- Use two panels.
- Left panel `Roentgenroehre`: show electrons hitting a metal target and X-ray photons leaving the target; use only a few straight path lines.
- Right panel `Spektrum`: show horizontal axis `E` and vertical axis `Intensitaet`.
- In the spectrum, show a smooth continuous bremsstrahlung background that extends from low energy on the left to a sharp high-energy cutoff on the far right labelled `E_max`.

- Add two narrow characteristic peaks on top of the background labelled `K_alpha` and `K_beta`; `K_beta` must be to the right of `K_alpha`.

- Add a small note `Moseley: Linien verraten Z`.
- Visible learner text must be limited to the title, `Roentgenroehre`, `Elektronen`, `Target`, `Roentgenphotonen`, `Spektrum`, `E`, `Intensitaet`, `E_max`, `K_alpha`, `K_beta`, `E_max = e*U`, and `Moseley: Linien verraten Z`.

Vermeiden:

- Do not show only characteristic lines without a continuous background.
- Do not show only a continuous background without characteristic peaks.
- Do not place `E_max` at the left low-energy start; `E_max` must mark the right high-energy cutoff.
- Do not put `K_beta` to the left of `K_alpha`.
- Do not draw curved electron paths unless the curvature is explicitly irrelevant; straight schematic paths are safer.

- Do not add long explanatory text, internal instructions, or production guidance.
