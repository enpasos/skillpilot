Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

Show the harmonic series `1 + 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/4 + ...`.
Use the standard grouping argument:
`1`
`+ 1/2`
`+ (1/3 + 1/4) > 1/2`
`+ (1/5 + 1/6 + 1/7 + 1/8) > 1/2`
`+ ...`
Then show the conclusion: because infinitely many groups are each greater than `1/2`, the partial sums grow without bound.
Use grouping brackets or colored boxes only.

Vermeiden:

Do not say the harmonic series converges.
Do not show a finite decimal approximation as the final result.
Do not draw arrows. If a visual flow is needed, use numbered panels instead.
Do not use the alternating harmonic series.
