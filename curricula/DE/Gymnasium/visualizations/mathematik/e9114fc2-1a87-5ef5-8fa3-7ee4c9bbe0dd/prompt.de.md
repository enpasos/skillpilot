```text
Titel: Flächen mit Integralen berechnen
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann Flächeninhalte berechnen, die von einem oder mehreren Funktionsgraphen und Parallelen zu den Koordinatenachsen begrenzt werden, und dazu passende Integrale aufstellen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:
- Regeneration focus: make the coordinate diagram mathematically consistent.
- Visualize the area between the upper function y = 4 and the lower function g(x) = x + 1 only on the interval from x = 0 to x = 2.
- Show that the area is A = integral from 0 to 2 of (4 - (x + 1)) dx = integral from 0 to 2 of (3 - x) dx.
- Show the antiderivative 3x - 1/2*x^2 and the evaluation (6 - 2) - (0 - 0) = 4.
- Final result: A = 4.
- The lower line g(x) = x + 1 must pass through (0, 1) and (2, 3); the horizontal upper line y = 4 must remain above it on the whole interval.

Vermeiden:
- The y-axis must be exactly the line x = 0. Do not draw a second separate vertical line labelled x = 0.
- The shaded region starts at the y-axis x = 0 and ends at a dashed vertical line x = 2.
- Do not shade anything left of the y-axis or to the right of x = 2.
- Avoid any extra zero tick, duplicate origin label, or shifted coordinate origin.
- Keine technischen IDs, keine Produktnamen, keine Wasserzeichen.
```
