## Prompt

```text
Create one landscape PNG illustration for a German grade-7 mathematics learning-goal card about choosing a suitable measuring tool, carrying out a measurement, and recording a plausible result with a unit. Friendly, clear, abstract comic style with warm soft colors and clean outlines, not photorealistic and not a sterile infographic.

A simple two-part scene on one kitchen table. Left: a curious learner holds one ordinary apple and considers two tools lying separately on the table: a small ruler and a digital kitchen scale. A light blue arrow points to the scale as the appropriate choice for measuring the apple's mass; the ruler stays unused and there is no red cross. Right: the same apple rests ON the scale platform, and the scale display reads exactly "175 g" in large unambiguous digits. A small heading above the scale reads exactly "Masse". The apple size should look plausible for 175 grams. The scale display's resolution is whole grams, with no fabricated extra decimal precision.

The story should read left-to-right at compact card size: object/quantity → suitable tool → result with unit. Keep the learner's gaze on the apple and scale, not at the viewer. No other labels, numbers, unit symbols, equations, logos, trademarks, watermarks, or technical IDs. No copied worksheet or recognizable brand design. Clear background and generous margins.
```
