## Prompt

```text
Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: single landscape PNG candidate for a German upper-secondary mathematics learning-goal card.
Primary request: Show the three different formal structures of an implication, its converse, and its contrapositive in a friendly, abstract, clear comic illustration. This is only about FORM, not whether any statement is true.
Scene/backdrop: warm off-white paper, three equal-size horizontal rounded comic panels in soft blue, amber and mint, dark navy hand-drawn outlines and a small cheerful illustrated pencil near the margin. No concrete scenario or metaphor.
Panel 1: label “Aussage”; formula exactly “P ⇒ Q”.
Panel 2: label “Umkehrung”; formula exactly “Q ⇒ P”.
Panel 3: label “Kontraposition”; formula exactly “¬Q ⇒ ¬P”.
Make the order reversal between rows 1 and 2 visually clear. In row 3, show both terms individually negated and reversed. The long implication arrow ⇒ points RIGHT in each row. Keep all terms and negation signs crisp and legible at card size.
Text (verbatim, no other text): “Aussage”, “P ⇒ Q”, “Umkehrung”, “Q ⇒ P”, “Kontraposition”, “¬Q ⇒ ¬P”.
Style: polished hand-drawn, approachable, mildly playful classroom comic; NOT photorealistic and NOT a sterile vector chart.
Critical exclusions: NO plants, weather, sun, sad faces, check marks, crosses, truth-value symbols, equivalence signs, external examples, different character moods, or props that suggest one row is true and another false. No logos, watermarks, technical IDs, additional arrows, or extra formulas.
```
