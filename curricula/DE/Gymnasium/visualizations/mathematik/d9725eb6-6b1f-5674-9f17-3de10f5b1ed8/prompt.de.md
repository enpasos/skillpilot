Titel: Pythagoreische trigonometrische Beziehung herleiten
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann die Beziehung $\sin^2(\alpha)+\cos^2(\alpha)=1$ anschaulich oder rechnerisch herleiten.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

Use a first-quadrant unit-circle/right-triangle visual.
Show a radius of length `1` from the origin to a point `P`.
Label the horizontal leg `cos(alpha)` and the vertical leg `sin(alpha)`.
Label the angle at the origin as `alpha`.
Show the derivation with Pythagoras: `cos^2(alpha) + sin^2(alpha) = 1^2 = 1`.
The legs must meet at a right angle; the hypotenuse is the radius.

Vermeiden:

Do not swap the sine and cosine legs.
Do not use side lengths that contradict the unit radius.
Do not show a full derivation for a non-unit circle unless it is normalized correctly.
Avoid arrows. If any arrow or pointer is used, it must point exactly to the side or angle named by its label.
