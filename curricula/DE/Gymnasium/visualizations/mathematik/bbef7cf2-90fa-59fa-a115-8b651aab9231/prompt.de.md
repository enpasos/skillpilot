Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Show a unit circle on the left and two graphs on the right: `sin(x)` and `cos(x)`.

- Sin graph key points over `0` to `2pi`: `(0,0)`, `(pi/2,1)`, `(pi,0)`, `(3pi/2,-1)`, `(2pi,0)`.
- Cos graph key points over `0` to `2pi`: `(0,1)`, `(pi/2,0)`, `(pi,-1)`, `(3pi/2,0)`, `(2pi,1)`.
- Label amplitude `1` and period `2pi` clearly.
- Use different colors for sine and cosine and keep labels readable.

Vermeiden:

- Do not swap sine and cosine start values.
- Do not mark the period as `pi`.
- Do not draw sine or cosine as straight lines.
- Do not use degree labels on the graph x-axis.
- Do not include technical IDs, filenames, watermarks, or brand names.
