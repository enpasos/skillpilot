Titel: Punkte in allgemeinen Raumkonfigurationen spiegeln (LK)
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann Punkte in allgemeinen räumlichen Konfigurationen spiegeln und die Ergebnisse geometrisch prüfen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

Show point reflection in 3D using a simple plane.
Use exactly this example:
- mirror plane: `E: x = 2`
- original point: `P(5|1|3)`
- reflected point: `P'(-1|1|3)`
- midpoint: `M(2|1|3)` lies on `E`
- check: segment `PP'` is perpendicular to `E`

Use a small 3D coordinate sketch plus a table:
`P -> P'`, `M`, and the two checks. The sketch should show the plane `E: x=2`, the points `P` and `P'`, and the midpoint `M` on the plane.

If a reference image is provided, use its simplified x-direction cross-section instead of adding decorative 3D axes. Preserve the equal distances `P'` to `M` and `M` to `P`, and preserve every coordinate label exactly.

Vermeiden:

Do not draw a mapping arrow from `P` to `P'`. Use a dashed segment without arrowheads if a connection is shown.
Do not change any coordinate value.
Do not label the mirror plane as `x=0`.
Do not place `M` away from the plane.
Do not add coordinate axes when using the x-direction cross-section.
Do not use perspective arrows except optional coordinate-axis arrowheads at positive axis ends.
