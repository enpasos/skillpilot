Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann den chinesischen Restsatz für paarweise teilerfremde Moduln formulieren, die Voraussetzungen prüfen und die Eindeutigkeit der Lösung modulo des Produktmoduls deuten.

Zusatzanweisung:
Create a clear didactic visualization for the Chinese remainder theorem prerequisites.

The image must show the following exact mathematical content:

- Title text in German: "Chinesischen Restsatz anwenden: Voraussetzungen prüfen"
- Three congruence cards:
  - x ≡ 2 (mod 3)
  - x ≡ 3 (mod 5)
  - x ≡ 2 (mod 7)
- A checklist panel showing:
  - ggT(3,5)=1
  - ggT(3,7)=1
  - ggT(5,7)=1
  - "paarweise teilerfremd"
- A result panel showing:
  - "Produktmodul: 3·5·7 = 105"
  - "eine eindeutige Lösung modulo 105"
  - "Beispiel: x = 23"
- The example must be correct: 23 leaves remainder 2 modulo 3, remainder 3 modulo 5, and remainder 2 modulo 7.

Visual composition:
- Use a friendly classroom infographic style with clean cards, arrows from the congruence cards to the checklist, and one arrow from the checklist to the result panel.
- Do not add extra formulas, extra numbers, extra congruences, or decorative mathematical symbols that could be mistaken for content.
- Every arrow must connect the intended source and target.
- German text must use correct umlauts.
- Avoid tiny text. All formulas and labels must be legible.
