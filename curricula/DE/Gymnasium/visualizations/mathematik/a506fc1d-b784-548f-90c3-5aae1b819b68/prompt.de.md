## Prompt

```text
Compare a right triangular prism and an oblique triangular prism. Keep each prism's base planes horizontal and parallel, with the same triangular base area G and the same perpendicular height h. Translate the upper base sideways in the oblique prism so its lateral edges visibly slant while its vertical height remains h. Compare horizontal slices parallel to the bases at equal perpendicular distances above the lower bases; show that corresponding cross-sectional areas are equal at every height. Label V=G·h, G, and a right-angle marker at h. Never label the slanted edge as height or imply different volumes.
```
