Required mathematical layout:

  - "Logische Äquivalenz"
  - "Direkter Beweis"
  - "Beweis durch Kontraposition"

Avoid:
- Do not mark the converse B -> A as true.
- Do not use n = 2 as a counterexample here.
- Do not add truth tables.
- Do not use English words such as "Proof Idea".
- Do not misspell "Logische".
