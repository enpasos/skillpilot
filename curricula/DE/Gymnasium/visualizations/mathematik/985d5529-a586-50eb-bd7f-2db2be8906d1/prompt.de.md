Pflichtinhalt:

Show plane reflection in 3D by reflecting three non-collinear points.

- reflected plane `F'` goes through `A'`, `B'`, `C'`; this is the plane `x = -1`
- check: the midpoints of `AA'`, `BB'`, and `CC'` all have `x = 2`

Use a clear table with rows `A`, `B`, `C` and `A'`, `B'`, `C'`, plus a simple 3D sketch showing the three parallel planes `F`, `S`, and `F'`.

If a reference image is provided, follow its three parallel plane layout. Use dashed connector segments without arrowheads only between matching pairs `A/A'`, `B/B'`, and `C/C'`. Each connector must pass through the matching midpoint on `S: x = 2`.

Vermeiden:

Do not draw arrows from `F` to `F'`.
Do not draw only two points for a plane.
Do not change `F: x = 5`, `S: x = 2`, or `F': x = -1`.
Do not make the reflected plane intersect the mirror plane.
Do not add coordinate axes to the plane layout.
