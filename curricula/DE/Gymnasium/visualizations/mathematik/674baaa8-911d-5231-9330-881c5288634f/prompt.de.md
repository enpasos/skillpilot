Pflichtinhalt:

Use one right triangle with hypotenuse `1`.

Relative to `alpha`, label the opposite leg `sin(alpha)` and the adjacent leg `cos(alpha)`.
Then show the key conclusion: `sin(90° - alpha) = cos(alpha)`.
Make clear why: for the complementary angle, the leg opposite `90° - alpha` is the same leg that is adjacent to `alpha`.

Vermeiden:

Do not swap the two acute angles.
Do not write `sin(90° - alpha) = sin(alpha)`.
Do not write the cosine complement relationship unless the sine relationship is correct and central.
