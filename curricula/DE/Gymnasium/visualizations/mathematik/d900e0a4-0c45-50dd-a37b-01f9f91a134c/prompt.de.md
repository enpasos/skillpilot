```text
Titel: Exponentialgleichungen lösen
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann Gleichungen der Form $b \cdot a^x = c$ mithilfe logarithmischer Umformungen lösen, Ergebnisse überprüfen und in Sachsituationen interpretieren.

Zusatzanweisung:
Pflichtinhalt:

- Show the equation `3*2^x = 48` and the solving steps clearly.
- Correct steps: `2^x = 16`, then `x = 4`.
- Include a check line: `3*2^4 = 48`.
- Also show a logarithm example in a smaller side panel: `5*1,2^x = 20`, `1,2^x = 4`, `x = ln(4)/ln(1,2)`.
- Add a context cue such as "Wann wird der Zielwert erreicht?"
- Use arrows from equation to transformation to solution.

Vermeiden:

- Do not write `x = 48/(3*2)` or solve exponential equations by dividing the exponent.
- Do not use the false rule `ln(a^x)=ln(a)*ln(x)`.
- Do not omit the check step.
- Do not display a negative or non-integer result for the first equation.
- Do not include technical IDs, filenames, watermarks, or brand names.
```
