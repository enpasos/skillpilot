```text
Titel: Trigonometrische Graphen aus Funktionstermen zeichnen
Beschreibung: Die lernende Person kann für Funktionsterme der Form $a\cdot\sin(b\cdot(x+c))+d$ mithilfe von Amplitude, Periode und Verschiebungen den zugehörigen Graphen zeichnen.

Zusatzanweisung:
Create a corrected German educational infographic for drawing the trigonometric graph f(x) = 2*sin(x) + 1.

Pflichtinhalt:
- Show one clean coordinate system for x from 0 to 2pi and y from -1 to 3.
- Label the y-axis ticks -1, 0, 1, 2, 3.
- Draw the dashed midline y = 1.
- Plot and label the key points (0|1), (pi/2|3), (pi|1), (3pi/2|-1), (2pi|1).
- Show max = 3 and min = -1.
- Show a vertical double-headed amplitude arrow from y = 1 up to y = 3, labeled Amplitude 2.
- Show a second vertical double-headed amplitude arrow from y = 1 down to y = -1, also labeled Amplitude 2.
- The downward amplitude arrow must end exactly at the minimum line y = -1, not at the x-axis and not at y = 0.
- Show the period as one horizontal arrow from 0 to 2pi along the x-direction, labeled Periode 2pi.

Vermeiden:
- Do not draw the lower amplitude arrow only to the x-axis.
- Do not measure amplitude from y = 0.
- Do not change the function term.
- Do not add cosine, tangent, or a phase shift.
```
