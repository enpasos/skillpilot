# One overall result

There is one total score out of 20 points and exactly one binary overall
result: **pass at 15 points**, otherwise not passed. Partial marks have no
separate pass thresholds.

Credit equivalent mathematical methods and traceable work wherever they
appear in the submission. Count identical work only once. Award partial
marks as specified, otherwise 0.5 points for meaningful but incomplete work.
Do not repeatedly penalise one local calculation or transcription error;
correct traceable follow-through retains its credit. Such a local error alone
does not trigger an overall cap. Concise correct explanations and equivalent
notation are sufficient; no particular wording or fixed order is required.

Before comparison with the pass threshold, cap the total score at 14 points if the submission as a whole contains no sound explanation of the factorization method. This applies in particular when the explanation of how fully factor-base-smooth relations and even exponent sums yield a square congruence is entirely missing, or when the explanation of how gcd calculation can then extract a nontrivial factor is entirely missing. Merely naming the final factors, silently admitting primes outside the specified factor base, or treating every congruent pair of squares as an unchecked factorization does not replace these core actions. Correctly annotated calculations or a concise equivalent general justification anywhere in the submission are sufficient; no particular technical term, program execution or additional primality test is required. A local error in a table entry, exponent or gcd alone does not trigger the cap when the method is otherwise sound.

Multiple applicable reasons still impose only the same cap of 14 points. Compare the one resulting total with 15 points. There are no additional part-pass decisions.
