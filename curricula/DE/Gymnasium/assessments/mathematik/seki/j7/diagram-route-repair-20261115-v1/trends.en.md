# Key values and trends: equality and a plateau

Machine content review released · CC-BY-4.0 · SkillPilot

## Task

All data are invented models. Straight segments define the course between the points. A calculator is allowed. Use this supplied definition: average rate of change = (final volume − initial volume) ÷ elapsed time, here in L/h. A negative result means decreasing volume. The slope of the graph is not a physical tilt of the container.

**Case A – two containers:**

![Chart: time in hours, water volume in litres; container A has a solid line and B a dashed line.](/assets/assessment-materials/mathematik/f4fda40d-dca5-510b-bd6d-f7874b3d7b9b/water/f4fda40d-dca5-510b-bd6d-f7874b3d7b9b.png)

1. Find A’s highest volume and its time, and every intersection of A and B in the shown interval. Explain what they mean.
2. Find A’s rates of change during 0–4 h and 4–6 h, and B’s rate. Interpret the values with units. State when A contains less, the same amount or more water than B.

**Case B – a different course:**

![Volume-time chart of container C with straight segments.](/assets/assessment-materials/mathematik/d9c49084-084e-5142-859c-33c53403ccf1/plateau/d9c49084-084e-5142-859c-33c53403ccf1.png)

3. Find C’s highest volume and the entire interval during which it occurs. Find the rates in 0–1 h, 1–3 h and 3–5 h and interpret the course.
4. Someone claims: “After 5 h, C will definitely continue to empty; the chart proves that a pump will then be running.” Assess both parts and justify your answer.

## Evaluation material: release only after submission

A: The maximum is 60 L at 4 h. The intersections are (2 h, 50 L) and (6 h, 50 L), meaning equal volumes. A’s rates are (60−40)/4 = +5 L/h during 0–4 h and (50−60)/2 = −5 L/h during 4–6 h; B’s rate is 0 L/h throughout. A’s volume first increases by 5 L per hour and then decreases by 5 L per hour, while B remains constant. A is below B from 0 h until before 2 h, equal at 2 h and 6 h, and above B between 2 h and 6 h. These statements concern the displayed interval.

C: The maximum of 50 L occurs throughout 1–3 h, including the endpoints. The rates are +30 L/h, 0 L/h and −10 L/h. Its volume increases, stays constant and then decreases. The displayed interval ending at 5 h establishes neither a definite later course nor a cause such as a pump. Constant volume does not itself prove that there is no inflow or outflow.
