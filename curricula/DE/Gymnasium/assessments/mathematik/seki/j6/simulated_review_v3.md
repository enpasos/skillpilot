## Finding and Resolution

Task 6.2 in v2 explicitly named the totals $60$ and $50$, identified the truncated y-axis and asked for a "false impression". These explanations gave away intermediate results and the direction of the required critique. In v3, learners must derive the percentage themselves and decide whether the displayed chart is appropriate, with a justification based on the visible material.

Task 6.3 in v2 combined two mathematically unrelated activities: reflecting points across the line $x=3$ and extending an arithmetic number pattern. Although the combined two-point allocation was mathematically workable, the prompt obscured that two independent competencies were being assessed.

Task 6 v3 also separates the activities:

1. Task 6.3 contains only the reflection and awards 1 BE.
2. Task 6.4 contains only the number pattern and awards 1 BE.

The expected results, total of 6 BE and demand levels are unchanged. The reflection coverage and prerequisite are narrowed from the former combined reflection parent to the exact axis-reflection child. Earlier promoted source artifacts remain immutable.

## Verification

### Mathematical correctness

- Reflection across $x=3$: $A(1|2) \mapsto A'(5|2)$, $B(3|2) \mapsto B'(3|2)$ and $C(2|4) \mapsto C'(4|4)$.
- Number pattern: the constant difference is $4$, so the next values are $18$ and $22$.

- Task 6.2 no longer supplies the totals or labels the chart defect in advance.
- The chart remains concrete evidence that learners can inspect and criticize.
- Each numbered subtask now assesses one coherent activity.
- The split makes the one-point allocation per activity explicit.
- No additional cognitive load or new mathematical requirement is introduced.
- The task remains suitable for Jahrgangsstufe 6.
