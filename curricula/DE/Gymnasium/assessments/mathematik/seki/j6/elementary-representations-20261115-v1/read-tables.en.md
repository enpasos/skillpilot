# Compare table values and limit claims

## Task

A: The table contains only the measured times:

| Time in h | 0 | 0.5 | 1.5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Temperature in °C | −2.5 | 1.5 | −0.5 |

Read the highest measured temperature and its time. By how much do the measured temperatures at 0 h and 0.5 h differ? Describe the shown development using entries from the table. Does the table also establish the temperature at 1 h or at 3 h? Explain.

B: A second table shows:

| Number of bottles | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price in EUR | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |

Compare two and four bottles: give the prices and their difference, and describe a ratio relationship supported by entries from the table. Restrict your statement to the represented range.

## Evaluation material: release only after submission

A: The highest shown measurement is 1.5 °C at 0.5 h; it differs from −2.5 °C at 0 h by 4 °C (= 4 K). After this increase, the next measured value at 1.5 h is lower, −0.5 °C. Without an extra assumption, temperatures at 1 h and 3 h are not established by the table. B: Two bottles cost EUR4 and four cost EUR8; the price difference is EUR4. These entries have twice as many bottles and twice the price. Equivalently, the first entry has half of each quantity, or both compared entries cost EUR2 per bottle. The claim concerns the shown pairs.
