## A. Year-Level Fit

The reviewer confirms a strong fit for Jahrgangsstufe 5 Gymnasium. The draft stays within natural numbers, rounding, first integer-temperature reasoning, first-quadrant coordinates, rectangle perimeter/area ideas, and simple inverse operations. Formal fractions, negative coordinates, and algebraic term transformations are avoided.

## B. Didactic Quality

The contexts are assessed as plausible and school-near: material storage, weather station, classroom work area, seating area, and club registration. The reviewer explicitly notes that the contexts carry the mathematics instead of decorating isolated procedures.

## C. Competency-Oriented Elements

The reviewer highlights this as the strongest aspect of the draft. Each task includes basic computation and at least one communication, reasoning, misconception, model-critique, or interpretation element:

- Task 1: reasoning about the non-existence of a greatest inventory number
- Task 2: diagnosing and refuting a sign error in temperature differences
- Task 3: practical model critique for classroom space
- Task 4: distinguishing rectangle and square by properties
- Task 5: interpreting a division remainder in a practical grouping decision

## D. Mathematical Correctness

The reviewer reports no mathematical errors. The solution calculations are correct, and the coordinate geometry in Tasks 3 and 4 matches the expected rectangles and side lengths.

## E. Scoring and Timing

The reviewer judges 30 BE for 45 minutes as fair and realistic. The scoring gives appropriate weight to explanations and model-critique elements, not only to procedural calculations.

## F. Required Findings

There are no blocker or major findings. Both findings are minor language or polish items.

## G. Final Recommendation

The reviewer recommends the draft as a release candidate. The review explicitly confirms the Pedagogical Design Pattern Gate, including low floor / high ceiling, model critique, misconception design, and age-appropriate contexts.
