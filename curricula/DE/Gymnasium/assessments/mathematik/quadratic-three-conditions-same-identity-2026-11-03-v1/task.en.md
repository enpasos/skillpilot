The graph of a quadratic function passes through the three points
$$P=(0,1),\qquad Q=(2,3),\qquad R=(4,1).$$
Use the form $f(x)=ax^2+bx+c$. The coefficients $a,b,c$ are initially unknown.

**1 · Form a system from the points (3 points)**

Translate each of the three point conditions into an equation for $a,b,c$. State the complete system of linear equations.

**2 · Solve the system (6 points)**

Solve your system systematically and show the main calculation steps. State the coefficients and a complete equation for the function. For example, you may use substitution or elimination; any mathematically suitable method of solving the system is accepted.

**3 · Check the result (3 points)**

Check your function at all three original points by substituting and comparing with the corresponding given function value.

**4 · Explain the procedure algorithmically (4 points)**

Explain your approach as a repeatable sequence of steps that another person could use to determine the coefficients from three suitable point conditions and check the result. A concise clear explanation or corresponding comments directly alongside your calculation suffice.
