Let $f(x)=x^2$ and $g(x)=x$. Their intersection points are already given: $P_0=(0,0)$ and $P_1=(1,1)$.

1. Find the tangent slopes of both graphs at each intersection point. (6 points)
2. Find the smaller intersection angle of the graphs at each point. Give exact angles or values in degrees rounded to one decimal place, showing your method. (4 points)
3. Explain the local meaning of the two angles. In particular, explain why they can differ even though the same two graphs are involved. (2 points)
