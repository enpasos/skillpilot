# Integrate linked exponential functions

Consider

$$
f(x)=(2x+1)e^x,\qquad g(x)=2x e^{x^2+1},\qquad h(x)=e^{2x}+3x^2.
$$

Show your calculations. A total of **20 points** is available.

**1. Work with a product (8 points)**

Find an antiderivative of $f$. You may use $F(x)=(ax+b)e^x$:
determine $a,b$ by differentiating and comparing coefficients.
Check the resulting antiderivative fully by differentiation and
calculate $\int_0^1 f(x)\,dx$ exactly.
No particular integration technique is prescribed.

**2. Work with a composition (6 points)**

Find an antiderivative of $g$, verify it by differentiation, and
calculate $\int_0^1g(x)\,dx$ exactly.

**3. Work with a sum (6 points)**

Find an antiderivative of $h$, verify it by differentiation, and
calculate $\int_0^1h(x)\,dx$ exactly.
