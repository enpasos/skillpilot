# One overall assessment

There is one total score out of 20 points and one binary overall result:
**pass at 15 points or more**, otherwise not passed. Part allocations are
not separate pass thresholds.

Credit mathematically equivalent methods and traceable work wherever they
appear in the submission. Count the same performance only once. Award partial
credit as specified, otherwise 0.5 points for meaningful incomplete work.
For criteria worth 2 or more points, proportionate credit for correct steps
is available. Do not repeatedly penalise one local arithmetic or copying error;
credit correct traceable follow-through. Such a local error alone does not
trigger a total-score cap. Concise correct explanations and equivalent notation
are sufficient; no prescribed keywords or fixed order are required.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Chooses or equivalently derives a suitable antiderivative setup (1); coefficients or expression (2). | 3 |
| Traceable full differentiation using the product rule and identification with f. | 2 |
| Correct endpoint difference (1); both evaluations and result e+1 (2). | 3 |
| Determines a suitable antiderivative of g. | 2 |
| Shows the inner derivative 2x and full agreement with g. | 2 |
| Correct endpoint difference and result e²-e. | 2 |
| Both terms of H, including the factor 1/2, are correct. | 2 |
| Differentiates all of H and identifies the result with h. | 2 |
| Correct endpoint difference and result (e²+1)/2. | 2 |

Before comparison with the passing threshold, cap the total at 14 points if the whole submission contains no suitable antiderivative of an exponential–polynomial combination, no sound differentiation verification of any antiderivative used, or no appropriate associated integral calculation. Differentiation and coefficient comparison are fully equivalent methods; integration by parts or any other particular method is not required.

Multiple applicable cap reasons still give the same upper limit of 14 points. There is one overall assessment without additional part pass conditions.
