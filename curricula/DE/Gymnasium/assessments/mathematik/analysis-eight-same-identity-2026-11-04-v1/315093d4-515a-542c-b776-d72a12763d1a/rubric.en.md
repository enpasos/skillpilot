# One overall assessment

There is one total score out of 20 points and one binary overall result:
**pass at 15 points or more**, otherwise not passed. Part allocations are
not separate pass thresholds.

Credit mathematically equivalent methods and traceable work wherever they
appear in the submission. Count the same performance only once. Award partial
credit as specified, otherwise 0.5 points for meaningful incomplete work.
For criteria worth 2 or more points, proportionate credit for correct steps
is available. Do not repeatedly penalise one local arithmetic or copying error;
credit correct traceable follow-through. Such a local error alone does not
trigger a total-score cap. Concise correct explanations and equivalent notation
are sufficient; no prescribed keywords or fixed order are required.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Correct derivative values at 0 (1); complete P4 with correct powers and coefficients (3). | 4 |
| Complete e-series with start k=0 and infinite upper bound (2); first five terms (1); meaningful explanation of factorial and partial sums (1). | 4 |
| Correct fractions for S4 and S6 (1 each); both decimal values and the correct added terms (2). | 4 |
| Explains evaluation Pn(1)=Sn (2); distinguishes finite approximation from e (1); correctly interprets Pn(0)=1 (1). | 4 |
| Correctly substitutes n=6 into the given bound (1); correct interval (1); justifies three-place rounding using the whole interval (1); explains vanishing error and the limit (1). | 4 |

Before comparison with the passing threshold, cap the total at 14 points if the entire submission contains no mathematically correct infinite series representation of e, or no sound connection to the Taylor expansion of the exponential function about 0 evaluated at 1. This connection may be shown in calculations or elsewhere; no prescribed wording is required. A finite decimal or partial sum alone replaces neither the infinite series nor its Taylor connection.

Multiple applicable cap reasons still give the same upper limit of 14 points. There is one overall assessment without additional part pass conditions.
