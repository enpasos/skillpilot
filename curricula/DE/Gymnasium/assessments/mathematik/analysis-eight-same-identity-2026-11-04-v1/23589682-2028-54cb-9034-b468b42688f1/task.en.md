# Account for inner linear factors when integrating

Consider

$$
f(x)=e^{-2x+1},\quad g(x)=\sin\bigl(3(x-\pi/6)\bigr),\quad
h(x)=\cos\bigl(-2(x-\pi/4)\bigr).
$$

Trigonometric arguments are in radians. Show your calculations.
A total of **20 points** is available.

**1. Find and verify three antiderivatives (12 points)**

Find an antiderivative of each function and check it by differentiation.
For each one, show how the inner linear factor is compensated.
Each function is worth 4 points.

**2. Evaluate a definite integral (4 points)**

Calculate $\int_{\pi/6}^{\pi/2}g(x)\,dx$ exactly. Show the trigonometric
arguments obtained when substituting the two bounds.

**3. Explain the shared idea (2 points)**

Use the chain rule to explain why integration requires the reciprocal
of the nonzero inner slope. Relate your explanation to at least one
of your examples.

**4. Correct a missing factor (2 points)**

A student proposes $K(x)=\sin(-2(x-\pi/4))$ as an antiderivative of $h$.
Differentiate to show what is wrong and correct the prefactor.
