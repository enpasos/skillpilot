# One overall assessment

There is one total score out of 20 points and one binary overall result:
**pass at 15 points or more**, otherwise not passed. Part allocations are
not separate pass thresholds.

Credit mathematically equivalent methods and traceable work wherever they
appear in the submission. Count the same performance only once. Award partial
credit as specified, otherwise 0.5 points for meaningful incomplete work.
For criteria worth 2 or more points, proportionate credit for correct steps
is available. Do not repeatedly penalise one local arithmetic or copying error;
credit correct traceable follow-through. Such a local error alone does not
trigger a total-score cap. Concise correct explanations and equivalent notation
are sufficient; no prescribed keywords or fixed order are required.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Suitable exponential antiderivative with inner factor (2); complete differentiation check (2). | 4 |
| Suitable sine antiderivative with inner factor (2); complete differentiation check (2). | 4 |
| Suitable cosine antiderivative with inner factor (2); complete differentiation check (2). | 4 |
| Correct endpoint difference (1); arguments π and 0 (1 each); result 2/3 (1). | 4 |
| Explains the inner derivative factor and its compensation by the reciprocal using an example. | 2 |
| Shows that K has derivative -2h (1); justifies correcting the prefactor to -1/2 (1). | 2 |

Before comparison with the passing threshold, cap the total at 14 points if integration of one of the three explicitly required function types is entirely absent across the submission, or if a suitable compensation for the inner linear slope is nowhere evident. A correct antiderivative or correction elsewhere counts; omitting a numbered part alone does not mean a function type is absent. Distinguish local sign or arithmetic errors from a completely absent method.

Multiple applicable cap reasons still give the same upper limit of 14 points. There is one overall assessment without additional part pass conditions.
