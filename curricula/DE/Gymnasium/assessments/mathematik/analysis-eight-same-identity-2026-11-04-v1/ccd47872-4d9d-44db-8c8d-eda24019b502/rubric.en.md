# One overall assessment

There is one total score out of 20 points and one binary overall result:
**pass at 15 points or more**, otherwise not passed. Part allocations are
not separate pass thresholds.

Credit mathematically equivalent methods and traceable work wherever they
appear in the submission. Count the same performance only once. Award partial
credit as specified, otherwise 0.5 points for meaningful incomplete work.
For criteria worth 2 or more points, proportionate credit for correct steps
is available. Do not repeatedly penalise one local arithmetic or copying error;
credit correct traceable follow-through. Such a local error alone does not
trigger a total-score cap. Concise correct explanations and equivalent notation
are sufficient; no prescribed keywords or fixed order are required.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Correct expression (1+1/n)^n (1); base as step factor (1); exponent as number of steps explained (1). | 3 |
| Six traceably calculated table values (1 each); allow sensible rounding and limited calculator precision. | 6 |
| Suitable three-place approximation (1); justified using calculated values (1); distinguishes a finite table from exactness or an error proof (1). | 3 |
| Identifies e as the fixed base of the natural exponential function (2); f(0)=1 and f(1)=e (1 each). | 4 |
| New expression 200a_n (1); suitable approximation using the learner's rounding (1); distinguishes changed initial value/prefactor from unchanged base e (2). | 4 |

Before comparison with the passing threshold, cap the total at 14 points if the entire submission contains no traceable numerical approximation using the growth procedure, or no sound identification of e as the characteristic constant or base of the natural exponential function. A memorised decimal or use of the e-key alone does not demonstrate the approximation procedure. Local calculator-rounding differences with a correct method do not constitute such a complete failure.

Multiple applicable cap reasons still give the same upper limit of 14 points. There is one overall assessment without additional part pass conditions.
