# One overall assessment

There is one total score out of 20 points and one binary overall result:
**pass at 15 points or more**, otherwise not passed. Part allocations are
not separate pass thresholds.

Credit mathematically equivalent methods and traceable work wherever they
appear in the submission. Count the same performance only once. Award partial
credit as specified, otherwise 0.5 points for meaningful incomplete work.
For criteria worth 2 or more points, proportionate credit for correct steps
is available. Do not repeatedly penalise one local arithmetic or copying error;
credit correct traceable follow-through. Such a local error alone does not
trigger a total-score cap. Concise correct explanations and equivalent notation
are sufficient; no prescribed keywords or fixed order are required.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Correctly relates the derivative of ln to 1/x (2); explains the antiderivative condition (1); explains the additive constant and its zero derivative (1). | 4 |
| Correct logarithmic part 3ln(x) (2), polynomial part x² (1), and constant (1). | 4 |
| Correct derivatives of both parts (1 each); complete identification with h (1). | 3 |
| Correct upper and lower evaluations (1 each); difference e^4+5 (1). | 3 |
| Positive bounds and logarithm difference (2); correct logarithm rule (1); value 1 and independence of a explained (1). | 4 |
| Recognises 0 as outside the domain (1); explains the missing real value ln(0) and hence invalid ordinary endpoint evaluation (1). | 2 |

Before comparison with the passing threshold, cap the total at 14 points if the entire submission contains no suitable logarithmic antiderivative, no sound differentiation justification of the relationship to 1/x, or no appropriate integral calculation using that relationship. Equivalent work anywhere in the submission counts; repeating the same justification is unnecessary.

Multiple applicable cap reasons still give the same upper limit of 14 points. There is one overall assessment without additional part pass conditions.
