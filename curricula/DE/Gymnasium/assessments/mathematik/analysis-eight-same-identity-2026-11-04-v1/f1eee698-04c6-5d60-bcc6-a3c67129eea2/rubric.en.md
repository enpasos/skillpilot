# One overall assessment

There is one total score out of 20 points and one binary overall result:
**pass at 15 points or more**, otherwise not passed. Part allocations are
not separate pass thresholds.

Credit mathematically equivalent methods and traceable work wherever they
appear in the submission. Count the same performance only once. Award partial
credit as specified, otherwise 0.5 points for meaningful incomplete work.
For criteria worth 2 or more points, proportionate credit for correct steps
is available. Do not repeatedly penalise one local arithmetic or copying error;
credit correct traceable follow-through. Such a local error alone does not
trigger a total-score cap. Concise correct explanations and equivalent notation
are sufficient; no prescribed keywords or fixed order are required.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| A suitable rule for A defined for every n≥1, with complete expression or initial value/recurrence (3; proportionate credit for meaningful partial work). | 3 |
| Actually checks all five A data against the rule (2; at least two correct checks 1). | 2 |
| Justifies the choice with a suitable pattern or another mathematically sound construction. | 2 |
| Calculates the sixth A term consistently with the learner's justified rule. | 1 |
| Suitable rule for B defined for every n≥1, including signs and required initial value/index (3). | 3 |
| Actually checks all five B data against the rule (2; at least two correct checks 1). | 2 |
| Justifies the choice with a suitable pattern or another mathematically sound construction. | 2 |
| Calculates the sixth B term consistently with the learner's justified rule. | 1 |
| Explains lack of uniqueness (1); second well-defined A rule (1); justifies agreement of the first five values (1); demonstrates a different sixth term (1). | 4 |

Before comparison with the passing threshold, cap the total at 14 points if, across the submission, either sequence has no workable formation rule or there is no mathematically sound justification of the chosen rules anywhere. A traceable alternative construction is equivalent to the simple patterns in the model solution. A correct explicit or recursive representation counts wherever it appears; no particular continuation values are prescribed without fixing a rule.

Multiple applicable cap reasons still give the same upper limit of 14 points. There is one overall assessment without additional part pass conditions.
