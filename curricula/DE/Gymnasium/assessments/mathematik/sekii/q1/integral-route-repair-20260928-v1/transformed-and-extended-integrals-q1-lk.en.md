# Check inner factors and extended integration rules (advanced course)

Find an antiderivative of each function on the given domain. Explain the inner factor or reverse chain rule and check each answer by differentiation. Each part is worth 4 points.

1. $e^{3x-2}$ on R.
2. $\sin(2(x-1))$ on R.
3. $\cos(4(x+1))$ on R.
4. $(2x+1)^{1/2}$ for x>-1/2.
5. $(3x-1)^{-2/3}$ for x>1/3.
6. $(2x+1)e^{x^2+x}$ on R.

For parts 4 and 5 also explain why exponent r=-1 requires a different rule.

## Solution

Antiderivatives are $\frac13e^{3x-2}$, $-\frac12\cos(2(x-1))$, $\frac14\sin(4(x+1))$, $\frac13(2x+1)^{3/2}$, $(3x-1)^{1/3}$ and $e^{x^2+x}$, respectively. Differentiate each to verify the inner factors. For powers the factor is $1/[a(r+1)]$; r=-1 would give a zero denominator and requires a logarithm. In the last example, 2x+1 is the derivative of x²+x. Additive constants are allowed.
