1. $\int_0^8z(t)\,dt=136/3$ cubic metres, the water added rather than the absolute stored volume.

2. $\bar z=(136/3)/8=17/3$ cubic metres per hour.

3. $B(t)=12+\int_0^t z(x)\,dx=12-t^3/12+t^2+3t$. Thus $B(0)=12$ cubic metres and $B'(t)=z(t)$.

4. The rate measures change in volume per hour. The stock measures the volume present at a time; it is the accumulated rate plus the initial volume.

5. $\int_0^4B(t)\,dt=88$ cubic-metre hours, so $\bar B=88/4=22$ cubic metres. A constant stock of 22 cubic metres over four hours gives the same integral. Dividing by duration replaces the variable stock by this equivalent constant value and cancels the time unit. The endpoint average is $(12+104/3)/2=70/3$ cubic metres, different from 22. Two endpoints do not account for the curved intermediate variation.

6. Total inflow and mean inflow rate remain known. Changing the initial stock shifts the whole stock function and its mean by that amount. Over $[0,4]$ the mean is $B(0)+10$ cubic metres, so it is unknown without the initial stock.
