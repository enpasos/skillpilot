The inflow rate into a storage basin is $z(t)=-t^2/4+2t+3$ cubic metres per hour for $0\le t\le8$ hours. There is no outflow and the basin is large enough to prevent overflow.

1. Calculate total inflow from 0 to 8 hours and interpret the integral with its unit. (5 points)
2. Determine the mean inflow rate over that interval. (4 points)
3. Initially the basin contains 12 cubic metres. Form a function $B(t)$ for the stored volume and check its initial value and derivative. (6 points)
4. Explain the difference between inflow rate and stored volume in this model. (5 points)
5. Determine the mean stored volume over the first 4 hours. Justify division by the interval length using an equivalent constant stock and interpret the units. Check whether averaging the volumes at the beginning and end of this interval gives the same result. (6 points)
6. Now suppose the initial volume is unknown. Which of total inflow, mean inflow rate and mean stored volume can still be uniquely determined from the given rate? Explain. (2 points)
