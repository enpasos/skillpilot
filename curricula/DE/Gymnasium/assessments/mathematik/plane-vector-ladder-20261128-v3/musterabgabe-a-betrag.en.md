# Sample submission A for the magnitude task, V3

The perpendicular components form the legs of a right triangle. By Pythagoras, |u| = 5, |v| = 5 and |w| = 10. u + v = (0, 8), so 8 < 10. The horizontal steps cancel, and the direct route is shorter than the bent route. u + w = (9, 12), so |u + w| = √(81 + 144) = 15 = 5 + 10. Here w = 2u. In general, arrows pointing in the same direction join on the same straight line without reversal, so both lengths contribute in full to the resultant. For noncollinear arrows, the third side of the triangle is strictly shorter than the sum of the other two. For opposite directions, the difference remains, and if both lengths are positive it is less than their sum. A zero vector adds neither a displacement nor a length.
