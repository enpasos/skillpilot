Half points are allowed for meaningfully separable partial performances.
Units belong to interpretation; one accidentally incorrect unit symbol does
not automatically invalidate the entire model. Different valid trigonometric
methods and explanations alongside calculations receive equal credit.
No particular wording or location under a given part number is required.

Before comparison with 15, cap the one total score at 14 if an entire central
performance is absent across the submission or rests only on a fundamentally
unsuitable model: a meaningful composite-solid model, justified length
determination, justified angle determination, justified external-area
determination, or justified capacity determination. The cap does not require
perfect work on every detail. An isolated numerical, sign, copying, or
rounding slip does not trigger it.

Do not penalise one initial error repeatedly. Correct follow-through,
sound approaches, and suitable explanations earn credit even if an earlier
copied radius changes the final value. A small rounding discrepancy in litres
still allows credit for correct conversion and decision. A wrong basic model,
such as using slant length as the vertical height in the volume calculation,
must be assessed mathematically; it is not merely a rounding issue. A list of
formulas without assigning quantities to the situation earns no modelling
or reasoning credit.

Grade only legible demonstrated work.
