# A closed rainwater tank

20 points.

A business is planning a closed tank. Its circular internal base has a
diameter of 4 m. The vertical side wall is 3 m high. From the entire upper
circular rim, the roof runs along straight lines to an apex directly above
the circle's centre, 1.5 m above the rim. There is no horizontal partition
where wall and roof meet. The tank rests on a foundation along its entire base.

The geometric model neglects wall thickness, connections, and fittings.
The idealised internal and external dimensions therefore coincide.
The theoretical capacity includes the internal space up to the apex.
Any headspace needed in operation is initially not deducted.
A protective coating will cover only the exposed side wall and roof;
the contact surface with the foundation is not coated.

A calculator and a standard formula collection are allowed. Use unrounded
intermediate values. Give lengths, areas, and cubic metres to two decimal
places, angles to 0.1°, and litres to the nearest whole litre.

1. Select suitable geometric solids for the model and justify your choice.
   Sketch a vertical section through the common axis. Label the required
   lengths and explain the decomposition. (3 points)
2. Determine the length of a straight roof line from rim to apex. Justify
   the geometric relationship using your section. (3 points)
3. Determine the angle that this roof line makes with the horizontal plane
   of the upper circular rim. Justify your approach and mark the required
   angle. (3 points)
4. Calculate the idealised tank's complete external surface area including
   its base, then calculate the area to be coated. Explain which circular
   surfaces count in each calculation and which do not. (4 points)
5. Determine the theoretical capacity in cubic metres and litres.
   Decide through a clear comparison whether 45,000 litres would fit and,
   if necessary, state the shortfall in space. (5 points)
6. Identify one concrete limitation of the model for practical use and
   explain how it affects the usable capacity. (2 points)

Show connected modelling, calculation, and reasoning steps. Formulas or
final numbers alone are insufficient.
