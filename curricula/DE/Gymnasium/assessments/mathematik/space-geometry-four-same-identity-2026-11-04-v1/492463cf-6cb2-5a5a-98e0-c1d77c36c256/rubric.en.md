# One overall assessment

There is exactly one total score out of 20 points and one binary
overall result: **pass at 15 points or more**, otherwise not passed.
The point allocations for individual parts are not separate pass thresholds.

Credit traceable mathematical work wherever it appears in the submission.
Equivalent methods and alternative parameterisations receive equal credit;
count the same performance only once. Award partial credit as specified,
otherwise 0.5 points for meaningful but incomplete mathematical work.
Do not repeatedly penalise one local arithmetic or copying error; credit
sound, traceable follow-through. Such a local error alone does not trigger
a total-score cap. Concise correct explanations suffice; no prescribed
keywords or order of work are required.

Before comparison with the passing threshold, cap the total at 14 points if, across the entire submission, there is no mathematically workable time-dependent motion setup from the observations, no meaningful evaluation or investigation of that setup at all, or no interpretation of velocity as directed change in position per unit time. Merely stating a numerical vector does not provide that interpretation. A concise correct explanation is sufficient; the components need not be repeated individually if a general explanation clearly includes their signs and units.

Multiple applicable cap reasons still give the same upper limit of 14 points. There is still one overall assessment, without additional part pass conditions.
