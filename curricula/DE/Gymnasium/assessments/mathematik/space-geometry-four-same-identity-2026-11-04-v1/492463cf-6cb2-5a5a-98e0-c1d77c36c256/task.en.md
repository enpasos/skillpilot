# Model the straight flight of a camera drone

During a short observation interval, a camera drone flies in a straight line
with a constant velocity vector. In a fixed three-dimensional coordinate
system, all coordinates are in metres and $t$ is the time in seconds since
the start of the interval. At $t=2$ the drone is at $P(5,3,8)$; at $t=5$
it is at $Q(11,0,5)$. Use the model only for $0\leq t\leq8$.

Show your setup and calculations. A total of **20 points** is available.

**1. Form a motion model (8 points)**

Use the two observations to determine the velocity vector $\vec v$ and
the position vector $\vec x_0$ at $t=0$. State the complete model
$\vec x(t)=\vec x_0+t\vec v$, including its time interval.
Check that it reproduces both observations.

**2. Use the model (4 points)**

Calculate the drone's position at $t=6$. Also find its displacement vector
from $t=1$ to $t=4$.

**3. Interpret the velocity vector (4 points)**

Explain what each component of $\vec v$, including its sign and unit,
means in this situation. Also explain the difference between the position
vector $\vec x_0$ and the velocity vector $\vec v$.
You are not asked to calculate the magnitude of $\vec v$.

**4. Test specified positions (4 points)**

Investigate whether the drone reaches $R(15,-2,3)$ within the stated time
interval. If it does, give the time and check all three coordinates.
Then decide, with reasons, whether it reaches $S(15,-1,3)$.
A matching value for just one coordinate is insufficient.
