# One overall assessment

There is exactly one total score out of 20 points and one binary
overall result: **pass at 15 points or more**, otherwise not passed.
The point allocations for individual parts are not separate pass thresholds.

Credit traceable mathematical work wherever it appears in the submission.
Equivalent methods and alternative parameterisations receive equal credit;
count the same performance only once. Award partial credit as specified,
otherwise 0.5 points for meaningful but incomplete mathematical work.
Do not repeatedly penalise one local arithmetic or copying error; credit
sound, traceable follow-through. Such a local error alone does not trigger
a total-score cap. Concise correct explanations suffice; no prescribed
keywords or order of work are required.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| States a correct normal vector. | 1 |
| Justifies a nonzero normal vector and hence a genuine plane for every a. | 1 |
| States E0 and E1 correctly (1 each). | 2 |
| Transforms the simultaneous equations with F appropriately (1); derives (a-1)(x+z-1)=0 or an equivalent condition (1). | 2 |
| Treats a=1 separately without invalid division (1); justifies equality with F and the whole plane as intersection (1). | 2 |
| Solves the two independent conditions for a not equal to 1 (1); correct parametric intersection line (1). | 2 |
| Meaningfully interprets the same intersection line for every other parameter and the absence of a distinct parallel case relative to F. | 1 |
| Substitutes a general point of L for arbitrary a (1); establishes the identity and membership in every family plane (1). | 2 |
| Uses two different family planes or an equivalent argument to prove there are no further points common to all planes. | 1 |
| Clearly interprets the configuration of different planes sharing an axis. | 1 |
| Derives the unique value a=3/2 from the point condition (1); checks by substitution (1). | 2 |
| Comparison of normals gives only a=1 (1); justifies distinct parallel planes there (1); justifies intersection for other parameters and impossibility of equality (1). | 3 |

Before comparison with the passing threshold, cap the total at 14 points if, across the entire submission, the parameter-dependent positional investigation is replaced entirely by isolated examples, no specified positional or intersection condition leads to justified parameter values, or a geometrically sound interpretation of the plane configurations is entirely absent. The same cap applies if the case a=1 relative to F is lost solely through invalid division by a-1 and is not handled correctly anywhere else: the central change from an intersection line to an entire plane has then not been investigated. A correct treatment of the exceptional case elsewhere counts.

Multiple applicable cap reasons still give the same upper limit of 14 points. There is still one overall assessment, without additional part pass conditions.
