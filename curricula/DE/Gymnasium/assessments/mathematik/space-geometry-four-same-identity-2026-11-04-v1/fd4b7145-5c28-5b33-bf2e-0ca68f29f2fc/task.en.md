# Investigate a family of planes and its common line

For each $a\in\mathbb R$, consider the plane

$$
E_a:\quad(a-1)x+y+az=a+1.
$$

Also let $F:\ y+z=2$, $G:\ y+z=3$, and $P=(2,0,1)$.

Show your setup and calculations. A total of **20 points** is available.

**1. Describe the family (4 points)**

State a normal vector $\vec n_a$ of $E_a$ and the equations of $E_0$
and $E_1$. Justify why the equation defines a genuine plane for every
real value of $a$.

**2. Investigate the position relative to $F$ for every parameter (7 points)**

For **all** $a\in\mathbb R$, determine whether $E_a$ and $F$ are
identical, distinct parallel planes, or intersecting. Derive your case
distinction from the equations. In the intersecting case, give a
parametric equation of the intersection line and explain what the
solution means. Check any value of $a$ at which a divisor you use would
be zero.

**3. Explain the common configuration (4 points)**

Show that every plane in the family contains one common line.
Also justify why there are no further points that belong to **every**
plane in the family. Interpret this configuration geometrically.

**4. Satisfy specified conditions (5 points)**

Find all values of $a$ for which $P$ lies in $E_a$ and check your result
by substitution.
Also find every value of $a$ for which $E_a$ is parallel to $G$.
Decide whether the planes are identical or distinct and parallel, and
justify their position relative to $G$ for the remaining values.
