# One overall assessment

There is exactly one total score out of 20 points and one binary
overall result: **pass at 15 points or more**, otherwise not passed.
The point allocations for individual parts are not separate pass thresholds.

Credit traceable mathematical work wherever it appears in the submission.
Equivalent methods and alternative parameterisations receive equal credit;
count the same performance only once. Award partial credit as specified,
otherwise 0.5 points for meaningful but incomplete mathematical work.
Do not repeatedly penalise one local arithmetic or copying error; credit
sound, traceable follow-through. Such a local error alone does not trigger
a total-score cap. Concise correct explanations suffice; no prescribed
keywords or order of work are required.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| xy: correct condition (1), parameter (1), point (1), explicit checks of line and plane membership (0.5 each). | 4 |
| xz: correct condition (1), parameter (1), point (1), explicit checks of line and plane membership (0.5 each). | 4 |
| yz: correct condition (1), parameter (1), point (1), explicit checks of line and plane membership (0.5 each). | 4 |
| Explains the trace point as belonging to both line and plane (1); correctly distinguishes this from merely zeroing a coordinate of an arbitrary point on the line (1). | 2 |
| h: shows z=0 for every parameter (1); recognises that the whole line is in the plane, with infinitely many common points (1). | 2 |
| k: shows the contradiction 4=0 (1); correctly interprets the line as parallel, outside the plane, with no common point (1). | 2 |
| Correctly justifies for h and k that neither has a single unique trace point (1 each). | 2 |

Before comparison with the passing threshold, cap the total at 14 points if, across the entire submission, investigation of one of the three coordinate-plane intersections is entirely absent, there is no sound geometric interpretation of the intersection set at all, or algebraic checks of simultaneous line and plane membership are entirely absent. Substitution into the line equation may count both for finding and for checking a point if simultaneous membership is explicitly checked there; artificially repeating the same calculation is unnecessary.

Multiple applicable cap reasons still give the same upper limit of 14 points. There is still one overall assessment, without additional part pass conditions.
