# One overall assessment

There is exactly one total score out of 20 points and one binary
overall result: **pass at 15 points or more**, otherwise not passed.
The point allocations for individual parts are not separate pass thresholds.

Credit traceable mathematical work wherever it appears in the submission.
Equivalent methods and alternative parameterisations receive equal credit;
count the same performance only once. Award partial credit as specified,
otherwise 0.5 points for meaningful but incomplete mathematical work.
Do not repeatedly penalise one local arithmetic or copying error; credit
sound, traceable follow-through. Such a local error alone does not trigger
a total-score cap. Concise correct explanations suffice; no prescribed
keywords or order of work are required.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Correctly distinguishes a as selecting a family member and r as locating points on that line (1 each). | 2 |
| States g0, g1 and g2 with correct position and direction vectors (1 each; 0.5 for just one correct vector). | 3 |
| Justifies that the direction vector is nonzero for every a. | 1 |
| Derives coordinate conditions for intersection with h (1); obtains the complete restriction a=0 or a=1 with reasoning (1). | 2 |
| Investigates a=0 algebraically (1) and justifies that the lines are distinct and parallel (1). | 2 |
| Solves a=1 with r=s=0 (1); correctly identifies the intersection point and intersecting position (1). | 2 |
| Justifies nonintersection for every other a (1) and explains skewness using nonparallel directions (1). | 2 |
| Rules out identical lines for every a with reasoning; the corresponding case analysis is sufficient. | 1 |
| Derives r=0 and a(a-1)=2 for k. | 1 |
| Finds both and only the parameter values a=2 and a=-1. | 1 |
| Correctly determines both intersection points and checks each on the two relevant lines (1 each: 0.5 finding, 0.5 checking). | 2 |
| Correctly interprets the two distinct meeting points on k geometrically. | 1 |

Before comparison with the passing threshold, cap the total at 14 points if, across the entire submission, the parameter-dependent investigation relative to h is replaced entirely by isolated examples, there is no derivation of parameter values from any specified positional or intersection condition, or there is no mathematically sound geometric interpretation of the line configurations. In particular, merely stating “no intersection” without distinguishing parallel from skew lines does not provide such a positional investigation. A correct general case argument may cover several individual cases at once.

Multiple applicable cap reasons still give the same upper limit of 14 points. There is still one overall assessment, without additional part pass conditions.
