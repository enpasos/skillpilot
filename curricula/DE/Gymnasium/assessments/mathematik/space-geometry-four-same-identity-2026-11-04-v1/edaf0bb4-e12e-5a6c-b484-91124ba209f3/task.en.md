# Compare a family of lines with two fixed lines

For each $a\in\mathbb R$, define a line by

$$
g_a:\quad\vec x=\begin{pmatrix}0\\a-1\\a(a-1)\end{pmatrix}
+r\begin{pmatrix}1\\a\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad r\in\mathbb R.
$$

The fixed comparison lines are

$$
h:\quad\vec x=\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\\0\end{pmatrix}
+s\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad s\in\mathbb R,
$$

$$
k:\quad\vec x=\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\\2\end{pmatrix}
+u\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad u\in\mathbb R.
$$

Show your setup and calculations. A total of **20 points** is available.

**1. Understand the family (6 points)**

Explain the different roles of $a$ and $r$. State $g_0$, $g_1$, and
$g_2$ explicitly. Justify why the formula defines a genuine line for
every real value of $a$.

**2. Investigate the position relative to $h$ for every parameter (9 points)**

For **all** $a\in\mathbb R$, determine whether $g_a$ and $h$ are
identical, distinct parallel lines, intersecting, or skew. Derive the
necessary parameter conditions algebraically and give a complete case
distinction. Where an intersection exists, give its point. Also explain
why lines that do not intersect are not automatically parallel in this
example. Testing a few individual values is insufficient to investigate
all parameters.

**3. Satisfy another intersection condition (5 points)**

Find every value of $a$ for which $g_a$ intersects $k$. Calculate each
intersection point and check it on both relevant lines. Explain whether
the resulting family members meet $k$ at the same point.
For the parameter calculation, you may use the identity
$a(a-1)-2=(a-2)(a+1)$.
