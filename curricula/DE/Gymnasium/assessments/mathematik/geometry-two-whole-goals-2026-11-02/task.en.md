# Dividing a tetrahedron

The points
$$
\begin{gathered}
O(0\mid0\mid0),\quad A(12\mid0\mid0),\\
B(0\mid8\mid0),\quad C(0\mid0\mid6)
\end{gathered}
$$
form a tetrahedron with base triangle $OAB$ and apex $C$. Coordinates are measured in length units (LU). The plane $E:\ z=3$ cuts the tetrahedron. Let $F$ be the plane through $O$, $A$ and $B$.

The following lines are also given:
$$
\begin{aligned}
g:\quad\vec{x}&=\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\\6\end{pmatrix}
+t\begin{pmatrix}2\\0\\-1\end{pmatrix},\quad t\in\mathbb R,\\
h:\quad\vec{x}&=\begin{pmatrix}12\\0\\0\end{pmatrix}
+s\begin{pmatrix}-3\\2\\0\end{pmatrix},\quad s\in\mathbb R,\\
k:\quad\vec{x}&=\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\\3\end{pmatrix}
+r\begin{pmatrix}-3\\2\\0\end{pmatrix},\quad r\in\mathbb R.
\end{aligned}
$$

Show your calculations and reasoning in the following parts. You may draw a sketch, but one is not required.

**a) Positions relative to the cutting plane (8 points).** Investigate the position of $E$ relative to $F$ and the position of each line $g$, $h$ and $k$ relative to $E$. Calculate any unique intersection point. Justify your decisions by calculation and interpret the results geometrically.

**b) Comparing two pairs of lines (4 points).** Determine and justify the position of $h$ relative to $k$. Then assess this statement: “Since $g$ intersects plane $E$ and $k$ lies in $E$, the lines $g$ and $k$ must also intersect.” Determine and justify the actual position of $g$ relative to $k$.

**c) The cross-section (4 points).** Find the intersection points $U$, $V$ and $W$ of $E$ with the segments $CO$, $CA$ and $CB$, respectively. Explain what shape forms the entire cross-section of the tetrahedron by $E$.

**d) The two solids (6 points).** Calculate the volume of the whole tetrahedron and the volumes of the two solids formed by the cut. Give the ratio “part containing apex $C$ : remaining part” in the simplest possible whole numbers, and justify your calculation.

**e) Assessing a claim (2 points).** “The plane halves the height of the tetrahedron. Therefore, the two solids have equal volume.” Assess this claim. Use the geometry to explain how the height ratio leads to the volume ratio you found.
