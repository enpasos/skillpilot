# Official source notes for Q2 volume route candidate

The PDF files remain in their repository source paths and were checked as original official artifacts; this derivative does not modify or rename their source-goal records.

- HMKB KCGO Mathematics, Q2.2, printed/physical p. 41: describes simple spatial solids including straight and oblique pyramids and using geometric properties to calculate volume; it does not state the one-third formula and does not name cones in this Q2.2 passage.
- Thuringia Gymnasium mathematics plan 2018/2019, printed/physical p. 36: formula expectation for volumes of straight pyramids and cones, V = (1/3) A_G h.
- Saarland GOS entry-phase plan 2014, printed/physical p. 16: states the pyramid volume formula and asks learners to justify it by the equal-volume decomposition of a triangular prism into three pyramids.
- Saarland GOS entry-phase plan 2014, printed/physical p. 17: states cone:hemisphere:cylinder volume ratio 1:2:3 for equal radius and height.
