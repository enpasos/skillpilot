Material: A supplied npn common-emitter circuit has its emitter at 0 V and a 1.0 kΩ collector resistor connected to +10 V. A small input signal is superimposed on the base bias. The output is collector voltage relative to 0 V. Only in this model's active region does I_C = 100 I_B apply. Bias points and input variations are provided, not to be determined from an unspecified resistor network. Output limits are 0.2 V in saturation and 10 V at cutoff. The characteristic is given as a table:

| I_B in µA | 0 | 20 | 40 | 50 | 60 | 80 | 98 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| I_C in mA | 0 | 2 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 8 | 9.8 |
| U_C in V | 10 | 8 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 0.2 |

1. At a bias of I_B = 50 µA the input changes I_B by ±10 µA. Use the table to determine output voltage at bias and both signal extremes. Explain signal amplification, phase inversion and the output's energy source. (5 points)
2. With the same input waveform its amplitude grows to ±60 µA. Predict the output shape from the supplied limits and explain why the active-region relation cannot be extrapolated without limits. (4 points)
3. Another bias is I_B = 80 µA with a ±30 µA signal. Assess the symmetry of available output swing and propose a change allowed by the material that transmits this signal without clipping. (3 points)
