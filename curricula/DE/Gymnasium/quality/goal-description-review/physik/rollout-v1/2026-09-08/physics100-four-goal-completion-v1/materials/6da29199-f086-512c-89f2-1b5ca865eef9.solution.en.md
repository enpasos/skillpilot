1. Premise: C and Au occur together; conclusion: both formed directly from H in the present central star (1 point). M1 permits C formation only under suitable evolutionary conditions and does not support Au as an ordinary product of simple H fusion (2 points). M2 invalidates equating detection and formation sites because previously enriched matter is transferred (2 points).
2. For example, C forms during suitable earlier stellar phases and Au in an appropriate neutron-capture environment. Ejected or released enriched matter mixes into the later cloud from which the observed system forms. Justified production paths plus the distinct transfer steps: 4 points total. No memorized list of all nuclear reactions is required, and no claim that every supernova makes everything is valid.
3. The materials establish a possible history but identify no individual source object (1 point). A concrete limitation is mixing contributions from several objects or lacking chronological attribution (1 point). Additional relative element/isotope abundances or age information could be compared; neither alone guarantees a unique source (1 point). Equivalent justified observations are accepted.
