**Material and mode:** Supplied are checked, touch-safe 5 V teaching modules and this connection plan: tone generator → LED driver's audio input; its current-limited output → visible LED; LED → plastic optical fibre → photodiode receiver module with current-to-voltage converter; receiver output → audio amplifier → loudspeaker. Transmitter and receiver each have their own 5 V supply; only the fibre connects them. The driver adds a DC offset to the audio signal so the LED current stays in its permitted range. The receiver outputs changes in photocurrent as an audio signal. No lasers, mains voltage or exposed power components; keep the volume low. Reconnect only with power off, and switch on only after the teacher's check.

Before starting, the teacher marks **S** (one of the selected student experiments) or **D** (demonstration). S requires an actually observed learner-built setup; a written answer cannot replace it. D requires functional explanation and analysis of the supplied demonstration data, not a claimed build.

1. Explain the signal conversions and the functions of the driver, fibre and photodiode receiver using the plan. Explain why a constant bright light level does not transmit a sustained tone. (4 points)
2. **S:** Connect the modules according to the plan, conduct approved function tests at 400 Hz and 800 Hz, and record one independently measured receiver frequency for each, with the uncertainty stated by the instrument. Interrupt the optical path without changing an electrical connection and test the effect. **D:** Instead analyse these demonstration data: input 400 Hz → receiver 401±3 Hz; input 800 Hz → receiver 798±4 Hz; no periodic receiver signal after optical interruption. In either mode, explain which observations support transmission of the time-varying signal through light and what accuracy the data allow. (8 points)
3. After restoring the optical path, no sound is heard although the LED is on. The receiver reading is constant. Give a fault hypothesis consistent with the material and a targeted test. Explain why simply increasing brightness need not solve the problem. (4 points)
