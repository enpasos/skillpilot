# Economics goal-description understanding-evidence review criteria v1

Apply the bound general v2 prompt independently to each current canonical
Gymnasium Wirtschaftswissenschaften goal. This machine review creates AI
candidates only. It does not attest human approval or classroom experience.

- **Exact scope and sources:** Preserve the goal's own operations, economic,
  legal or social-scientific objects, demand level, prerequisites and bilingual
  meaning. Title phase is compatibility metadata. Do not infer a jurisdiction,
  school stage, course profile or full source coverage from a title or sourceRef.
  A shared source bullet contributes only the clause claimed by this goal.
- **Relationships instead of labels:** State the content-specific mechanism,
  distinction, decision constraint or institution that organizes the competence.
  Naming instruments, repeating terms or summarizing a supplied image alone
  does not show independent understanding.
- **Models and causality:** Distinguish model assumptions from observation,
  correlation from causation, ceteris-paribus reasoning from a universal claim,
  nominal from real variables, stocks from flows and private from social costs
  whenever these distinctions belong to this goal. Do not add an unrelated model.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** Interpret material and data in their stated
  period, population, units and comparison basis. Distinguish an observation,
  plausible explanation and unsupported causal claim. An indicator measures a
  defined aspect and does not establish every dimension of prosperity or welfare.
- **Judgment and plurality:** Separate factual reasoning from normative criteria
  and stakeholder interests. Make a judgment explainable through goal-specific
  criteria, consequences, distribution, trade-offs and limitations, without
  requiring agreement with one political or ethical position.
- **Legal accuracy:** In legal goals, distinguish the applicable facts, rule,
  subsumption and legal consequence. Avoid claims of unconditional legality,
  automatic liability or universal remedies. Verify current primary legal sources
  for concrete legal assertions; if necessary evidence is absent, retain an open
  finding rather than inventing it.
- **Independent performance and transfer:** Name what the learner independently
  explains, analyzes, models, calculates, compares or judges and why this exposes
  the organizing relationship. Transfer changes a relevant mechanism, incentive,
  constraint, assumption, stakeholder configuration or material; a number swap
  or renamed company alone is insufficient. Stay within the competence.
- **Atomicity:** Interpreting several indicators for one defined comparison or
  explaining and assessing one policy may be one goal. Distinct competencies
  needing separate evidence require split_review. Do not conceal that issue by
  lengthening a replacement description or lowering an existing gate.
- **Description/profile boundary and DE/EN:** Keep the description concise and
  learner-facing. Preserve equivalent competence and demand in both languages.
  Detailed cases, variations and assistance/scoring criteria belong in the
  positive-understanding-evidence-v2 profile. AI recommendations are not profiles.
- **Conservative decision:** Use keep for an adequate existing text, revise only
  for a concrete bounded correction, split_review for independent competencies,
  and block for unresolved correctness or scope. All records remain candidate
  with ai_candidate authority. A prepared PDF, image or agreeing model is not
  fachliche approval, persistence evidence, M7 completion or human release.
