## Chemical and visual checks

- **Reactants:** two separate `Mg` atoms and one bonded `O₂` molecule with
  two O atoms are visible at all three sizes. No extra reactant is drawn.
- **Transfer:** the middle still shows two Mg atoms and exactly four gold
  electron dots, two beside each Mg. Four arrows end at the oxygen atoms,
  one from each Mg to each O. Thus the pictured transfer is consistent with
  two Mg atoms losing four electrons and the two O atoms gaining two each.
  The O–O bond is absent in the product stage.
- **Product model:** the large labeled ions alternate across the visible
  three-by-three central motif: `Mg²⁺, O²⁻, Mg²⁺` / `O²⁻, Mg²⁺, O²⁻` /
  `Mg²⁺, O²⁻, Mg²⁺`. Partial red ions at the edges visually continue the
  same arrangement beyond the frame. There are no separated Mg²⁺/O²⁻
  dyads or outlines suggesting discrete MgO molecules. All visible charge
  labels on the large ions have the correct signs and magnitudes. This is a
  **schematic two-dimensional excerpt**, not a claim about exact three-
  dimensional MgO crystal geometry.
- **Responsive use:** the panel sequence, four electrons, arrows and single
  connected product arrangement remain distinguishable at 360 px. The
  superscript charges are small there but still discernible, and the larger
  color/arrow structure also carries the concept. The image has no tiny
  compulsory equation or crowded text block. At 680 px the charges and
  alternating motif are clear without cropping. The friendly comic style
  fits the candidate's orientation role.

**Model boundary to retain in alt text and teaching:** the right panel is a
cropped illustrative piece of an extended solid. Its visible finite window
contains more large ions than the single two-Mg/one-O₂ event at left, so it
must not be used as the atom-by-atom product count for that one event. The
current candidate alt text already calls it an excerpt. A stronger final alt
text can state explicitly that the left/middle stages show one balanced
transfer example while the right shows the repeating product structure. This
scale change is visible through the product crop and is acceptable for an
orientation image, but it should be checked again during any future active
asset review.
