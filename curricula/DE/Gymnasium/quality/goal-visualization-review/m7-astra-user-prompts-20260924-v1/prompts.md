# Kopierfertige Bildprompts

Jeder Abschnitt ist einzeln in Astra nutzbar. Pro Prompt ein quadratisches PNG erzeugen. Gemeinsamer Stil: freundlich, klar, comicartig, warmer Cremegrund, kräftige gut lesbare Linien und Beschriftungen, passende Cyan-/Orange-/Navy-Akzente; keine Fotorealistik, sterile Technikoptik, Wasserzeichen oder Dekoration über fachlichen Elementen. Fachliche Geometrie, Zahlen und Beschriftungen haben Vorrang.

## 1. 06ce2b1b-e888-5322-9ed9-dfc6d322956a — Natürlicher Logarithmus als Umkehrfunktion

Create one square PNG for German upper-secondary mathematics. Show y=e^x and y=ln(x) as reflections across y=x in a truly square coordinate plot, x and y both from −2 to 4, with identical unit lengths and tick spacing. The line y=x must be a true 45-degree diagonal. Draw e^x through (0,1), (1,e), and the exact point (ln 4,4)≈(1.386,4); draw ln(x) only for x>0 through (1,0), (e,1), and the reflected exact point (4,ln 4)≈(4,1.386), approaching x=0 from the right. Place every marker exactly at its stated coordinates on its curve. Add subtle coordinate guides for x=ln 4 and y=ln 4 so both reflected points are unambiguous; keep equal unit spacing and do not shift a marker to make a label fit. In particular, (ln 4,4) belongs on y=e^x at x≈1.386, while (4,ln 4) belongs on y=ln(x) at x=4. Offset text labels enough to stay readable without moving either marker. The two points (ln 4,4) and (4,ln 4) must be exact reflections across y=x. Label the curves eˣ and ln(x), the mirror line y=x, and axes x and y. The curves must be coordinate-swapped mirror images. No invented coordinates or extra curves.

## 4. 5619ca5b-dc2a-504e-ad89-2e0ca0a83822 — Volumenformel der Pyramide

Create one square educational PNG that explains the volume of a pyramid through many thin horizontal "storeys". Draw a pyramid with base area G and perpendicular height h beside a comparison prism with the same G and h; mark the right angle at the base of h. Show a few translucent horizontal slices in the pyramid, visibly becoming smaller toward the apex. Let z measure height upward from the base: at z=0 the slice area is G, at height z its side lengths have scale factor 1−z/h and its area is A(z)=G(1−z/h)², and at z=h the area is zero. A thin storey contributes approximately A(z)·Δz; increasingly thin storeys fill the pyramid in the limit. Put the exact calculation in a small, readable extension inset: V=∫₀ʰ G(1−z/h)² dz=(1/3)G·h. Label the prism V=G·h and the pyramid V=(1/3)G·h; make clear that 1/3 compares volumes, not heights. Make the shrinking cross sections and their order visually clear for a Grade 10 learner; keep the integral inset secondary. Do not depict a finite stack of flat storeys as an exact pyramid, do not make the slice areas shrink linearly, and do not use a three-tetrahedra dissection.

## 7. ae483d98-54e0-5985-96d2-fc1351d22e4f — Stichprobenumfang beim Hypothesentest

Use legible schematic discrete binomial bars for the sample proportion p̂=K/n: blue for H0 and orange for p1; a representative selection of bars is enough. In the n=50 panel, reject H0 for K≥32 (p̂≥0.64); in the n=200 panel, reject for K≥113 (p̂≥0.565). Mark each panel's right-tail rejection region under H0 as α and its non-rejection region under p1 as β. Show that the n=200 distributions are narrower and β is smaller. The two actual α values are each at most 5% but need not be identical because binomial outcomes are discrete. Keep each cutoff at its own correct location; do not reverse the tails, use a continuous density as if it were exact, or claim the numerical cutoff is unchanged.
