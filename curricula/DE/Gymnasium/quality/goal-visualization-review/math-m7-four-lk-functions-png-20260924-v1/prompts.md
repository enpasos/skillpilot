## Natural logarithm as inverse of the exponential function, candidate 1

```text
Use case: scientific-educational. Create a 1:1 SQUARE PNG illustration for German SkillPilot upper-secondary mathematics goal 'Den natürlichen Logarithmus als Umkehrfunktion der e-Funktion erklären (LK)'. Friendly, charming, abstract COMIC look with opaque pale cream/sky-blue background, navy ink lines, warm orange and cyan, clean and calm, not photorealistic and not a sterile chart. The image must remain legible in a 360×360 px learning-goal card. One LARGE central coordinate system with uniformly spaced x/y ticks and clear axes through origin; draw blue exponential curve y = e^x strictly above horizontal x-axis, passing exactly through (0,1), approaching y=0 to the left and rising right. Draw orange natural logarithm curve y = ln(x) ONLY for x>0, passing exactly through (1,0), approaching x=0 downward and rising slowly right. Draw thin dashed diagonal y=x; the two curves must visibly be mirror images across that diagonal and should never cross their forbidden axis domains. Mark the two exchanged points (0,1) and (1,0) with small visible dots and compact labels. Concise large header 'e-Funktion und ln: Umkehrung'; an unobtrusive bottom badge 'ln(x) nur für x > 0'. No other equations, no wrong or duplicate tick labels, no extra functions, no person, no formula book, no watermark. Scientific fidelity is essential: inverse relation, swapped coordinates, asymptotes and domains must agree with the drawing; if exact plotted graph cannot be achieved, use a simplified but correct schematic rather than decorative misleading geometry.
```

Candidate 1 is rejected: in the labelled coordinate system the dashed `y=x` misses the `(2,2)` and `(3,3)` positions, because horizontal and vertical units have different pixel widths. The `ln` curve is also too flat (at `x≈3` it lies below `y=1`, whereas `ln 3>1`). Independent actual-pixel review confirmed this at native and 360-pixel size.

## Bell curve as a concentration model, candidate 1

```text

Friendly abstract clear COMIC style, pale cream/sky-blue background, navy hand-drawn labels, warm orange curve, not photorealistic and not a sterile chart. One large coordinate plot of a nonnegative symmetric bell-shaped concentration profile, with the x-axis labelled 'Ort x (m)' and the y-axis 'Konzentration C (mg/L)'. Model in a compact legible header exactly 'C(x) = 3 · e^(−((x−4)/2)²)'. Center/peak at x=4 with y=3, marked '(4,3)'. Left and right orange bell sides are visibly mirror-symmetric about dashed vertical x=4; the curve approaches but NEVER crosses the horizontal x-axis at both ends. Mark x=2 and x=6 equidistant from center, with two dots at the SAME height and one concise shared label 'C(2) = C(6) = 3/e'. If point labels make the plot crowded, keep the shared equality under the graph. Three large clean parameter badges at bottom: 'A=3: Höhe', 'μ=4: Mitte', 's=2: Breite'. This is a concentration model, NOT a normalized probability density; do not label any area as 1 or probability. All values exact: at x=2 or 6, exponent = −1 so height=3/e; at x=4 exponent0 so peak3. Keep enough space and type size for 360×360 px display. No formula book, no extra numbers, no watermark, no transparencies, no impossible negative concentration. A schematic but correct symmetric curve is better than detailed false plotted coordinates.
```

Candidate 1 was independently reviewed in native and 360-pixel size and is on **HOLD**, not approved: with the `1` and `2` y-ticks about 158 px apart, the marked horizontal line at about y=729 is only about 5 px above the `1` tick (y≈734), i.e. plotted near 1.03 rather than `3/e≈1.103`. The two labels and symmetric x-positions are correct, but exact text and plotted height disagree. One targeted edit used the exact [value-line edit prompt](bell-curve-value-line-edit-prompt.md). Its line moved to y≈708–709, now about 25 px above the `1` tick and plotted near 1.16; the target would be about y≈718. This overshoots the specified correction. Both source PNGs, measured disposition and origin hashes are preserved in the [bell-curve candidate package](b71c332f-ef9d-5c27-983b-7103269ff419/README.md). Neither candidate is linked or V-approved; further image generation is deferred.
