## Source-only acceptance hazards to carry into comparison

1. At least one row in each of nine process areas, E Dynamics, four E choices, five Q content areas, course-specific column/inline-only kinds, compulsory-vs-choice and repeated exact competencies must be represented in the purposive sample.
2. Broad contextual prose and appendices need explicit inventory decisions; absence from source-goal denominator is reasonable when they are documented as context/operator/example/organization and their binding conditions remain preserved elsewhere.
3. A source count well above the old 154 may be valid if table bullets and both process/course columns, rather than old didactic paraphrase groups, are the unit. Count printed duplicates and course variants explicitly; do not add rows simply to meet a corridor.
4. Two old Dynamics claims about slope/friction and mechanical impulse conservation have no matching bullet on p28. P40–41 do not repair them: the quantum momentum definition is eA-only, and the second occurrence is uncertainty. General Newton/application wording cannot honestly be called an exact official demand for the two old statements.
5. For a “split” from a full printed bullet, every surviving child needs the full parent and restricting course/choice conditions. Exact reworded teaching expansions belong outside sourceText.
