# Archived rational-number-line predecessor

Date: 2026-08-31

This archive preserves the former Google Gemini / Nano Banana Pro image for
canonical Mathematics goal `f6b13b8e-1ecd-5420-905d-21290aa996a6` as rejected
predecessor evidence. Independent pixel measurements found that the point
labelled `−1,5` represented approximately `−1,364`; the `0,75` and `1,25`
points were also displaced. Keeping that image active would reinforce an
incorrect number-line representation.

The canonical, public, and backend JPEG copies were moved here byte-for-byte.
All three have SHA-256
`0f15837a2b8c33d3a9477375c6c36b9c85cb6c98cc2db96dfd4f88ac70837611`.
The original provider prompt and reconstruction prompt remain byte-identical
beside the active canonical visualization under their explicit
`nano-banana-original` names.

The exact source-to-archive mappings, hashes, feedback binding, and accepted
replacement hashes are recorded in `archive-manifest.json`. The predecessor
may not be restored without a new hash-bound fachliche review.
