**Material5:** Historically common drying and salting remain in use; modern controlled pasteurisation and cold chains can be combined with them. Drying removes water; salting reduces available water. Suitable pasteurisation reduces many vegetative microorganisms but is not general spore sterilisation and can change heat-sensitive constituents. Cooling slows many reactions/growth rather than killing all microbes. Moisture ingress, inadequate treatment, recontamination and a broken cold chain limit outcomes.
