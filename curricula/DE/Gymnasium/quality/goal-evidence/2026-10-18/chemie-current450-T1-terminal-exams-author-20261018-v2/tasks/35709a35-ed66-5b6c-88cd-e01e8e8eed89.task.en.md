### Supplementary complete task material

Complete profile cards, freely chosen relative model energies in kJ per mole of reaction extent (not measurements): exothermic reactants 0, products −40; pathway A peak +70, B peak +35. Draw both profiles and identify activation barrier and reaction energy. Fresh endothermic model at 20 °C: reactants 0, products +25, A peak +80, possible catalysed B peak +50. Other external conditions of each profile comparison are equal. Compare catalysis with heating without representing temperature as a changed peak energy.

### Task 1 (6 points)

For a fresh exothermic reaction, two qualitative profiles have the same reactant and product levels but different peak heights. Explain which can represent a catalyzed path and why both remain exothermic.

Justify the scientific claims and state one specific limit to the inference.

### Task 2 (6 points)

An independently described endothermic reaction is slow at 20 °C. Two proposals are heating it or using a suitable catalyst. Use a qualitative profile to explain which barrier change only the catalyst causes and why the reaction stays endothermic in both cases.

Justify the scientific claims and state one specific limit to the inference.
