### Task 3: expectation for the actual channel

The phenyl group of C₆H₅MgBr makes a new C–C bond to the carbonyl C of C₆H₅C(=O)CH₃; the π-electron pair moves to O. The simplified model gives (C₆H₅)₂C(OMgBr)CH₃. Separate protonation produces (C₆H₅)₂C(OH)CH₃, tertiary alcohol 1,1-diphenylethanol. This notation balances the magnesium alkoxide; it does not claim isolated free carbanions or one exclusive solution species.

The personally controlled carbonyl addition to dry Grignard solution belongs to the addition step; the later aqueous ammonium chloride workup performed by specialists belongs to protonation/quenching. Protic material added beforehand would consume reagent. Actual execution requires logged, directly observed personal operations. This model deliberately supplies no filled-in temperatures, times or observations. Responding correctly to a safe stop creates no replacement evidence for an unperformed addition. Warming or cloudiness alone neither identifies pure alcohol nor measures isolated yield. Initial weighed charges are particularly no measurement of the actual reagent concentration.
