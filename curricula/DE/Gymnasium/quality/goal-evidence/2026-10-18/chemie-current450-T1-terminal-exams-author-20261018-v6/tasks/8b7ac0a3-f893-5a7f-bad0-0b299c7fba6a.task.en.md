### Task 1 (6 points)

Valence MO schemes for homonuclear diatomic molecules; 1s core orbitals are intentionally omitted. Each bracket is one orbital; up/down each denotes one electron with that spin. Side-by-side pi orbitals are degenerate. A star marks antibonding; unstarred orbitals are bonding. z is the bond axis. Each ladder gives its qualitative order, not a shared absolute energy scale. Different 2s/2p mixing gives pi2p below sigma2p for N2 and the reverse for O2 here. The order is supplied and need not be derived.
```text
N2: relative Energie nimmt nach oben zu / relative energy increases upwards
hoch/high   sigma*2p_z     [    ]
            pi*2p_x       [    ]   pi*2p_y [    ]
            sigma2p_z     [up down]
            pi2p_x        [up down] pi2p_y [up down]
            sigma*2s      [up down]
niedrig/low sigma2s       [up down]
```

Independently count bonding and antibonding valence electrons in the supplied N2 ladder. Derive bond order using an explained relationship. Explain why total occupied electrons alone does not determine bond order. Bound any strength/stability claim; no exact bond energy is required.
