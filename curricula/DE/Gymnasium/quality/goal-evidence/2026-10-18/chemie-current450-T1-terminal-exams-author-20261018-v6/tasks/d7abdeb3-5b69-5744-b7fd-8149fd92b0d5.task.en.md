# Plan Grignard alcohol syntheses and perform under supervision

Complete the two paper cases and the separate supervised laboratory assignment. Total 18 points: 6 each for Tasks 1 and 2 and 6 for Task 3. Passing requires 18/18; every essential aspect is required. Actual execution requires directly observed actions of your own. A model calculation, model conversation or copied log cannot replace them. One suitable actual performance suffices for the laboratory assignment; the paper cases require no two additional syntheses.

### Material M3: supervised carbonyl addition in a specialist laboratory

This voluntary enrichment activity belongs in an appropriately equipped school outreach or university laboratory. An ordinary classroom or home experiment is unsuitable. Before starting, the directly supervising specialist provides instruction on the actual apparatus, substances, escape routes, eyewash and local alarm procedure and checks the prerequisites. If one is missing, do not start; Task 3 remains open.

**Bound practical source:** Merck–TU Darmstadt–Juniorlabor, [Grignard reaction laboratory script](https://www.juniorlabor.de/file_download/59/OS_Skript_Grignard_Zm.pdf), “Teil 2”, physical PDF page 10; preparation in the same script, pages 7–9. The provider offers the module as a six-hour pupil laboratory activity for at most twelve participants. This is a practical educational source, not evidence of a compulsory curriculum requirement. The original is third-party material with its own rights.

**Operating card for this assessment:**

- Specialists provide the phenylmagnesium bromide solution prepared in the original script; magnesium activation, bromobenzene addition and any attempts to restart reagent formation are outside the learner assignment. Composition, batch identity and release are recorded before handover. The original charge uses 2.4 g magnesium and 15.7 g bromobenzene in absolute ether; this is no measured reagent concentration.
- Required equipment comprises a suitable fume hood, dry apparatus with stirrer, addition funnel, effective reflux condenser and unobstructed pressure relief, an ice bath and a checked thermometer. Exclude flames and other ignition sources. The specialist checks dry equipment and solvents and appropriate clothing and gloves against current safety data sheets. Never seal the apparatus against pressure. Maintain anhydrous conditions until the separate workup.
- The specialist supplies the carbonyl solution containing 10.8 g acetophenone and 20 mL absolute diethyl ether and fills the dry addition funnel. After checking identities, the learner directly controls the tap under immediate supervision and slowly adds this solution to the stirred, ice-cooled Grignard solution. Ether must not boil. Log start/end, actual addition and observed conditions. Stir for one hour at room temperature afterwards.
- Before starting, the specialist records a temperature operating range for the actual apparatus. On reaching its limit, boiling, cooling or stirring failure, leakage, broken glass or uncertain identity: stop addition immediately, withdraw hands and alert the specialist. Do not resume without another check. Learners never add water, acid or alcohol as an emergency response. For fire, release or personal exposure follow the locally practised alarm and first-aid procedure; specialists take over the apparatus and every further chemical action.
- **Specialists alone perform subsequent workup:** Slowly add the original script’s saturated aqueous ammonium chloride solution to the ice-cooled mixture, then stir for ten minutes. This deliberately separate step protonates/quenching species after addition; it is no intervention during addition. The source specifies 80 mL. An unexplained, overheated or deviating mixture is not routinely carried on through this step; the specialist secures it. Specialists also perform separation, further workup and waste collection under local disposal rules. Learners take no reaction residues away.

The reagent-preparation section of the script contains ambiguous water-bath temperature wording. It is not adopted as a learner instruction here; specialists perform all reagent preparation. Its old blanket waste statement likewise does not replace a current local disposal decision.

### Task 3: one actual reagent application (6 points)

1. Before starting, explain from M3 which substances react, why the apparatus remains dry and your response to a stop signal. Check supplied identities and conditions with the specialist. Complete the preparatory fields in the blank log.
2. Personally perform the carbonyl addition specified in M3 under immediate supervision and record only actions and observations that actually occur. Truthfully log a justified safe stop; it is not a complete performance and does not require a repeat while conditions remain unexplained.
3. Show the new C–C bond, magnesium alkoxide and separate protonation for this actual reaction. Relate the observed operations to these steps. Explain why warming or cloudiness alone establishes neither purity nor isolated yield.
