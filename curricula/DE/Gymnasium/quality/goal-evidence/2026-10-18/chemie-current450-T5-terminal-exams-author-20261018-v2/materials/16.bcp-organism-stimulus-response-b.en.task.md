### Materials and separate teaching data

Under school supervision use twelve cultivated healthy potted Mimosa pudica plants with no recent damage: six for the main investigation and six separate plants, not previously stimulated, for transfer. Form three similarly sized pairs in each set; on each plant choose one clearly visible open pinna with at least six completely open opposing leaflet pairs. Use a clean soft brush with a flexible tip, stable supports, specimen labels, clock, room thermometer and a fixed camera with second-based timing; do not film personal data. Do not collect wild plants or eat material. No solvent, electrical stimulus or cutting tool. The primary data will be the learner own future videos and raw counts; the separately identified arithmetic block is no substitute.

### Procedure and protection

Before starting, the learner writes a hypothesis, response criterion and analysis rule. Work during the daytime under constant diffuse white/daylight with no direct sun or draught,24±2°C; keep plants under the same type of location/lighting without lamp heating and acclimatise quietly for at least20min. Record time, actual temperature and previous care. Use a pinna only if all N selected pairs stay open during a10-s baseline recording and the plant is not wilted. Randomly assign one plant in each size pair to touch and the other to sham, and randomise the order of all six plants beforehand. Each plant then stands on its own stable support. Do not hold the test pot or move a camera attached to its table. t=0 is first actual contact or the nearest point of the non-contact sham movement. Touch: one1-s stroke of only the flexible brush tip across an end leaflet blade; no pressing, squeezing or touching thorns/stem, and no claim of a calibrated force. Sham: the same hand/brush movement and duration about10mm beside the same leaf region, touching neither plant, pot nor table. Do not blow. Record all N pairs from the same fixed view immediately before t=0 and at5/15/30/60s; film the whole event. Stimulus strength is qualitative because brush contact is not force-calibrated. Do not stimulate again if no response is visible. Each plant receives only this one event that day.

### Measurement and analysis rule

Predefine N as the number of fully visible selected opposing leaflet pairs, not independent plants. Count a pair as closed(C) when both blades are folded toward each other in the image and touch/overlap along at least half their visible length; open or partly folded pairs are not fully closed. If orientation changes, a pinna is obscured or overlap is ambiguous, record U=unclear rather than guessing closure. Open/partial count+C+U must equal N. Record original C and U counts, file and frame timecode at every time. The primary endpoint is each plant change at30s, Δp=100·[C30/N−C0/N] in percentage points; C0=0 is the predefined open-baseline criterion. Where U>0, give bounds from C/N to(C+U)/N instead of an exact proportion; lost views remain missing and are not replaced afterwards by an apparently better plant. Display each plant value with group mean/range or intervals; compare means in percentage points, not relative percent increase. Each main condition has n=3 plants; leaflet pairs and time points are subsamples/repeated measures of that plant. This supports a bounded exploratory inference, not a population proof or a significance claim.

### Stopping and care

The supervisor supplies suitable cultivated plants and checks stable equipment, working space and care conditions. Handle pots only at the rim and avoid thorns/stems; wash hands and keep them away from the face. No ingestion, wild collection, dropping, tapping, overheating, cutting, anaesthetising or repeated triggering. Stop for wilting, injury, accidental jolt/contact during sham, sustained lighting/temperature change or an unscorable camera view; document the deviation and do not try again more strongly. An absent response is not a reason to increase stimulation and is not alone a performance failure. After60s continue non-contact observation and record state after5/10/20min; do not force reopening or promise a fixed recovery time. If recovery fails or wilting appears, the supervisor takes over care and no further tests occur. Continue caring for the plants afterwards rather than discarding them as test waste. These readiness/care steps belong to a later actual performance, not an additional machine-M7 gate.

### Personal task

Reveal only after main analysis. New fictional claim: “When a pot is moved, leaves close before the brush touches; direct leaf contact therefore need not be the only possible cause.” Independently formulate a new testable hypothesis and a comparison separating pot movement from mere hand approach, without a model control. Use six other previously unstimulated open plants and the same scoring rule; the supervisor checks only safety and feasibility before performance. Allowed movement is at most10mm slowly over at least2s on a level secure support; no lifting, tilting, jerking, impact or leaf contact. Independently plan assignment, constants, recording and stop rule, actually carry out the approved comparison and justify what it shows about the new causal explanation. Main-investigation results are not replacement measurements for this new factor.
