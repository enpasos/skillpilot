### Materials and separate teaching data

External record: connected polygonal fields at100×, blurred at400×; round feature moves when pressure changes.

### Procedure and protection

Under supervision place very thin epidermis in a water drop and lower coverslip at an angle. Locate at40× total, refocus at100×; use40× objective only with adequate clearance and fine focus. No stains. Avoid broken glass and clean slides as instructed.

### Personal task

Select, prepare and examine an original specimen. Produce a labelled original drawing with total magnification and observed features. Interpret the external record and name an artefact check. Do not add unseen nuclei.

### Fresh independent variation

A fresh specimen is folded several times. Independently decide between increased magnification and repreparation, justifying the choice from observation.
