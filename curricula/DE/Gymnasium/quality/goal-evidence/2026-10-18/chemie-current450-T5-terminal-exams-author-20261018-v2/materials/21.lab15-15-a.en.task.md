### Materials and separate teaching data

Prepared school-grade plaster and cork sheets with solvent-free adhesive,100×100 mm each; plaster5 mm, cork5 mm. Goggles, clamp, thermometer±0.5°C, closed40°C water bottle as heat source. Original material cards: plaster stiff but brittle, cork light/compliant and less heat-conductive; adhesive joins layers but is not automatically moisture-resistant. Practice outer temperatures at10 min: plaster32°C, plaster+cork25°C; room20°C; same inner source but no repeats.

### Procedure and protection

Set function/contact surfaces first. Under supervision join precut parts without knives/hot glue; equal adhesive and cure time, no loads above people. Measure temperature at the same point and control source contact; do not use model as a real structural component.

### Personal task

Actually combine materials into a model wall and test against one layer. Explain layer functions and a critical interface. Justify an everyday/technical use and name a needed additional test.

### Fresh independent variation

Fresh use exposes the outer wall to moisture. Independently choose protection and an interface test and explain why insulation alone does not establish system suitability.
