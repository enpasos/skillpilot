Identification is an argued trait assignment at a stated resolution; three groups do not automatically mean three species. Algal suspicion remains uncertain without necessary microscopic traits. Do not conceal uncertainty by inventing species names.

The new combination of rosette,undivided blade and strong longitudinal veins must be followed through suitable literature using independently verified traits; longitudinal veins alone do not establish a grass. Record each key decision and precise literature entry. If species-separating traits are absent,a justified group or unresolved result is appropriate; the author brief supplies no species answer. A new light,water or nutrient experiment does not replace identification.
