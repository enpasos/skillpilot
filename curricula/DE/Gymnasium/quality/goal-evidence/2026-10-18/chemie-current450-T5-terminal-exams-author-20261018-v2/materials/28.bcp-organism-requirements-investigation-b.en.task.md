### Procedure and protection

Predefine hypothesis, random assignment, germination criterion≥2mm and four-day duration; do not flood paper. Do not sample liquid/remove seeds or paper or routinely discard liquid. Before correction weigh the complete dish in its original configuration. For an intact setup, replace a water loss plausibly due to evaporation using water drops only until the dish returns to its own recorded starting mass m0 within±0.02g; never add salt solution. Record before/after masses, water addition and time. Spilled solution, removed liquid, lost seeds/paper or unexplained losses may also change salt/material amounts: report separately, do not automatically repair with water or salt-solution top-ups and do not silently treat the unit as an on-protocol replicate. Do not remove liquid to correct mass gain. Gravimetric correction is a practical approximation of water balance; gas exchange and biological changes preclude claiming continuously and exactly measured salt concentration. Do not open/culture mould; notify supervisor and dispose by school rules.
