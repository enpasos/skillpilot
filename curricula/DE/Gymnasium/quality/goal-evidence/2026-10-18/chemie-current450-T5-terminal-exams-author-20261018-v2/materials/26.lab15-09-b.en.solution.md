Starter bacteria can convert milk sugar into lactic acid; lower pH changes protein interactions and favours coagulation. A pH4.5 strip reading is approximate, not exact acid concentration. Thickening alone identifies neither microbes nor product safety. A no-starter batch can still change on prolonged storage; control interpretation is time-bounded.

Lactose-free may mean enzymatically split milk sugar. Whether the specific starter uses available sugars needs testing with matched protein/temperature conditions.
