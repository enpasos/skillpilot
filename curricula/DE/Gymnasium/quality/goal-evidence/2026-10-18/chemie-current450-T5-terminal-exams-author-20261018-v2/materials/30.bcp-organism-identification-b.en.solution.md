Identification is an argued trait assignment at a stated resolution; three groups do not automatically mean three species. Algal suspicion remains uncertain without necessary microscopic traits. Do not conceal uncertainty by inventing species names.

An observed divided frond with actually visible spore-bearing patches calls for different key traits and possibly different literature from the old duckweed/algae comparison. Actual observations,key alternatives and the literature entry support identification;a secure fern species does not follow merely from the word fern or a generic frond sketch. Missing traits limit taxonomic resolution. Light/nutrient experiments and abundance comparisons are not substitute evidence.
