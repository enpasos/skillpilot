Measured Na and K references provide discriminating line signatures; decide against references rather than visible flame colour alone. NIST orientation: Na near589.0/589.6nm; K near766.5/769.9nm. Mixed signatures or contaminated blanks leave pure-candidate identity unresolved. Elemental evidence identifies within the declared NaCl/KCl set but does not independently establish chloride or analytical purity. No results are invented.

KCl and NaCl can both match solubility/conductivity; a suitable calibrated cation or elemental test with references must additionally discriminate.
