### Materials and separate teaching data

Food-grade potato and maize starch, water, forceps, slides/coverslips, light microscope and teacher calibration: at100× one division=10 µm. Text reference: starch occurs as granules differing in shape/size; species identification from this alone is uncertain. Fictional selected granules: A30/40/50 µm, B10/20/20 µm; only three each.

### Procedure and protection

Goggles, avoid dust, inhalation and tasting. Disperse a tiny damp starch sample in water and mount thinly; use separate tools. Measure with calibration at100×, examining at least ten nonselectively chosen granules in each own sample; scale applies only to that setting.

### Personal task

Prepare both slides, examine and draw multiple granules with scale. Compare size distributions rather than one attractive granule. Evaluate the small external samples and explain limits on material identification.

### Fresh independent variation

A fresh change to400× lacks recalibration. Independently decide which observations remain valid and which size estimates need checking.
