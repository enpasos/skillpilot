## 1. Recover salt from a sand mixture

Filtration removes sand, not dissolved NaCl; crystallisation removes water. Purity=2.565/2.70=95.0%; NaCl recovery=2.565/3.00=85.5%; gross product mass gives90%, not pure NaCl yield. Excess rinse water increases evaporation demand; wet crystals distort mass. Own performance must be observed.

Sugar and salt can remain together in filtrate. Conductivity does not quantify sugar; a selective, suitably calibrated additional method is needed.
