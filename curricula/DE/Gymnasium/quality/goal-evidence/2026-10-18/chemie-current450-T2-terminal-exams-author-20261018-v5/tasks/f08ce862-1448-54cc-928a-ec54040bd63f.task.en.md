### Complete bound material card

Complete task model, not a manufacturing procedure: Me means CH3. M=Me3SiCl has one hydrolysable group; D=Me2SiCl2 has two; T=MeSiCl3 has three. Hydrolysis replaces each Si–Cl by Si–OH and forms HCl. Two Si–OH groups can condense to Si–O–Si and H2O. Model formulations: A mainly D with a few M end groups, no crosslinks; B high T content with D, dense network; C long flexible D chains with a few trifunctional crosslinkers, sparse network. Given properties at service temperature: A flows; B forms a hard shape-stable film; C deforms reversibly. Required uses are a flowing lubricating film, a hard coating and an elastic seal. These data describe selected grades, not all silicones.

### Task 1 (6 points)

Describe manufacture of A, B and C using the model. Write balanced hydrolysis of D and a local silanol condensation. Explain M/D/T functionality and the three structures. Assign all three oil/resin/rubber families and all uses with reasons. Transfer: C is made with substantially more T crosslinker at the same chain length. Does suitability as a soft seal automatically remain unchanged?

Justify the scientific claims and state one specific limit to the inference.

### Complete bound material card

Et=C2H5. In this second model series, water replaces Si–OEt by Si–OH and releases EtOH. Silanols condense as in case1. Formulation O: Me2Si(OEt)2 with a little Me3SiOEt, followed by controlled condensation into end-limited uncrosslinked chains. H: much MeSi(OEt)3, densely crosslinked after sufficient conversion. K: existing long HO–[SiMe2–O]n–H chains plus a little MeSi(OEt)3, specified as a moisture-curing condensation system. Given target grades: O flowing damping fluid; H hard protective varnish; K elastic joint sealant. K application changes from moist air to an entirely dry environment; everything else stays the same. No cure-time or temperature-resistance numbers are supplied.

### Task 2 (6 points)

Explain manufacture, functionality and structure for O, H and K. Write hydrolysis of MeSi(OEt)3 and local condensation. Justify all three family/use assignments. Evaluate dry application of K and compare it with O and H. Evaluate the claim that every silicone needs atmospheric moisture and every silicone resin suits any temperature.

Justify the scientific claims and state one specific limit to the inference.
