### Complete bound material card

Idealized material sheet: a methyl-bearing siloxane film is 40 nm thick. Smooth surface: water contact angle 105°, roll-off angle 35°. Nanostructured surface at the same silicone chemistry: 155°, 5°. A chemically different hydrophilic control is also 40 nm thick and has contact angle 25°. A smaller roll-off angle means a drop can move at a smaller tilt in this material sheet. Dirt removal and abrasion resistance were not measured. The 40 nm value describes film thickness only; no particle measurement is supplied.

### Task 1 (6 points)

1. Explain silicone relevance for a nanoscale surface application in this example: which chemistry/structure evidence can account for water repellency and mobile drops? 2. Compare smooth/nanostructured surfaces and the hydrophilic control. Does film thickness alone establish wetting behaviour? 3. Evaluate “40 nm thickness proves 40 nm silicone particles and a permanently self-cleaning surface.” Explain supported claims and a needed further observation.

Justify the scientific claims and state one specific limit to the inference.
