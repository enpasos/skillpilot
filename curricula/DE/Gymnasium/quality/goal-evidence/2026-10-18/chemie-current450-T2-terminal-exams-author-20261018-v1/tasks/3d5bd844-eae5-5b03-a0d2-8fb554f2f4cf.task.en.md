### Task 1 (6 points)

Data and conditions:
Product ASA2-(CH₃COO)-C₆H₄-COOH. Candidate building blocks: salicylic acid2-HO-C₆H₄-COOH; ethanoic anhydride(CH₃CO)₂O; ethanolCH₃CH₂OH; benzoic acidC₆H₅COOH. Supplied rule: activated anhydride transfers CH₃CO to phenolic O, producing ethanoic acid. No actual synthesis. Formal comparison with ethanoic acid is allowed if activation/equilibrium requirements are acknowledged.
Tasks:
1. Mark the acyl C–O disconnection and select complete suitable reactants.
2. Balance the forward reaction/byproduct and name the mechanistic reaction type.
3. Reject benzoic acid plus ethanol as a route to ASA and assess a formal ethanoic-acid alternative.

Justify the scientific claims and state one specific limit to the inference.
