### Task 1

1. Disconnect phenolic O–C(=O)CH₃, leaving salicylic acid with free phenolic OH and an acetyl donor. Salicylic acid plus ethanoic anhydride supplies these. Ring COOH remains in the scaffold.
2. C₇H₆O₃ +(CH₃CO)₂O → C₉H₈O₄ +CH₃COOH balances C11/H12/O6. O acylation proceeds by nucleophilic acyl substitution/addition-elimination; this anhydride route yields ethanoic acid rather than water.
3. Benzoic acid/ethanol would yield ethyl benzoate, not an ortho acetylated phenol retaining COOH. Salicylic/ethanoic acid is a formal water-eliminating O esterification but needs suitable activation/dehydration and is not automatically equivalent to the supplied anhydride route.

Scope limit: Retrosynthesis disconnects a plausible bond; complete reactants and a forward balance test plausibility. Transfer: Transfer from ASA to paracetamol disconnection and distinguish competing O/N acylation. Stop assessment once all aspects and independent transfer are sufficiently shown.

### Task 2

1. Disconnect N–C(=O)CH₃.4-Aminophenol supplies ring/OH/NH₂ and anhydride supplies acetyl. Aniline lacks para OH; methanol supplies neither ring nor amino function.
2. C₆H₇NO +(CH₃CO)₂O → C₈H₉NO₂ +CH₃COOH balances C10/H13/N1/O4. N acylation by nucleophilic acyl substitution gives NH-C(=O)-CH₃, with ethanoic acid byproduct. A water-condensation equation would misrepresent this anhydride route.
3. O acylation would give CH₃COO-C₆H₄-NH₂, a phenolic ester retaining amine, not paracetamol. Plausible disconnection proves no selectivity, yield or actual synthesis; supplied selective conditions belong to the model.
