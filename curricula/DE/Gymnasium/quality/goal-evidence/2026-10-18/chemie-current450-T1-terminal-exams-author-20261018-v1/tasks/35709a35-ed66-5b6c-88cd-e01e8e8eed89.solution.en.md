### Task 1

The learner assigns the lower peak to the catalyzed path, measures the activation barrier from the reactant level, and explains that unchanged lower products mean the same exothermic reaction energy.

Scope limit: Barrier and reaction energy are different profile features.

### Task 2

The learner keeps products above reactants, describes heating as providing more thermal energy and the catalyst as an alternative lower-barrier path, and does not equate either with changing reaction energy.

Scope limit: Kinetic accessibility and energy balance are kept separate.

Additional solution criteria: Supplementary cards give exothermic E_A=70 or35 and ΔE=−40 kJ/mol, endothermic E_A=80 or50 and ΔE=+25 kJ/mol. Heating changes particle energy distributions; for the same compared state conditions it does not lower the pathway barrier as a catalyst does.
