### Task 1

Copper is metallic; mobile electrons transport charge even in the solid. NaCl is an ionic crystal: ions are fixed in the solid but mobile in solution and melt, so the melt should conduct. Sugar is a molecular substance; the supplied neutral dissolved molecules provide no sufficient mobile ions for the conductivity considered here. Visible disappearance during dissolution alone does not distinguish salt from sugar. Conductivity at substance level is explained using charge carriers at particle level.

Scope limit: Solubility alone does not identify bonding type; statements about pure solutions apply to the explicitly supplied model.

### Task 2

Ethanol is an alcohol and dimethyl ether an ether; they are constitutional isomers. Ethanol molecules can form O−H···O hydrogen bonds with one another; dimethyl ether molecules lack a suitable hydrogen donor. Both have additional interactions. At similar molecular mass, a higher boiling point is therefore plausible for ethanol. The molecular formula alone is insufficient; connectivity matters. This does not yield a precise measured value or a universal ordering for molecules of very different sizes.

Scope limit: One functional structural feature explains a controlled comparison; it does not replace complete substance identification.
