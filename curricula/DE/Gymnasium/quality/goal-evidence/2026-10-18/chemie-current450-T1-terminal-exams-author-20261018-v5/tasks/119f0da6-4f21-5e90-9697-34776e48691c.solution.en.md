### Task 1

A transfers a proton: H3O+ donates and NH3 accepts it. B is redox: Zn loses two electrons and Cu2+ gains them. C is nucleophilic substitution: OH− donates an electron pair, electrophilic C accepts it and Br− is the leaving group. Backside attack with simultaneous bond changes identifies the specified SN2 mechanism. The same product balance alone would not establish a unique mechanism. An electron-pair donor is not automatically a reducing agent.

Scope limit: A reaction type and a particular mechanism are different levels of detail.
