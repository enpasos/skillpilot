2. DailyMed identifies calcium carbonate as an antacid. The model reaction yields 4.996 mmol CO₂; gas release is a plausible chemical contribution to gas-related discomfort, not an observed adverse effect. Mg(OH)₂ yields 0 mmol stoichiometric CO₂; this does not imply absence of adverse effects. Mole amounts establish no clinical risk ranking.
3. Neutralization consumes existing H⁺; barrier changes reflux access; PPI reduces new secretion. Capacity alone gives no rate, duration, suitability or diagnosis information, so these model portions support no individual treatment selection.
