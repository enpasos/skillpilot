### Task 1 (6 points)

Assign available/missing evidence to analysis, GMP and legal framework. Assess the advertising inference and identify specific open quality questions. Material: Own fictional pharmaceutical teaching dossier, not an actual product. Method card: chemical analysis checks characteristics such as identity, content and impurities within a validated method scope. GMP concerns controlled, documented manufacturing/testing processes and traceability; a peak time alone does not document this. AMG§1 states the general purpose of protection in medicines circulation, especially quality, efficacy and safety (official:https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/amg_1976/__1.html, actually accessed16Oct2026). This does not yield an individual authorisation decision. Fictional batchX: chromatography is reference-compatible; detection limit, complete impurity testing and batch/cleaning records are absent. Advertisement:“One peak proves quality and legal approval.”

Justify the scientific claims and state one specific limit to the inference.

Assess“the label and records make current analysis unnecessary”using the supplied method card. Which evidence helps and which specific question remains open? Another brochure cites onlyAMG§1. The complete method card fromcase1 distinguishes analysis, documented GMP and legal protective purpose. No actual authorisation or clinical-effect claim is tested.

Justify the scientific claims and state one specific limit to the inference.
