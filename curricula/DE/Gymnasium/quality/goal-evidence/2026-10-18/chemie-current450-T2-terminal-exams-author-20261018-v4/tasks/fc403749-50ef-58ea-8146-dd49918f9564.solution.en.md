### Task 1

DNA/RNA: nucleotides/sugar-phosphate-base, phosphodiesters; proteins: amino acids/peptides; fats: glycerol/fatty acid residues, three esters, not nucleotide polymers. Explain information, catalytic, storage roles structurally.

Scope limit: Molecular size or chain shape alone establishes no information coding. Nucleic acids are identified by nucleotides, bases and a sugar-phosphate backbone; a glucose polymer lacks that combination.

### Task 3

DNA/RNA contain sugar-phosphate-base nucleotides linked by phosphodiester bonds. Base sequence carries genetic information; DNA stores it and various RNA types participate, for example, in its translation into proteins. Starch and cellulose are glycosidically linked glucose polymers: starch stores energy in plants, cellulose structures cell walls; linkage geometry affects function. Proteins have peptide bonds and catalytic/structural functions; triacylglycerols have three ester links and store energy.
