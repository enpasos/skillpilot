## Concrete microscale method: indigo vat and comparison

Original prospective school design after qualified local clearance. The supervisor prepares every bath; learners alter no reagents. No experiment or colour measurement has occurred. Investigate reduction/leuco uptake/air oxidation; synthesis of indigo alone cannot replace dyeing.

### Materials and supervisor-only bath preparation

Use identified indigo/indigotin (C.I.Vat Blue1, CAS482-89-3), fresh sodium dithionite Na₂S₂O₄,1.000-M NaOH and deionised water. Per10.00-mL bath: V contains0.0200g indigo,3.00mL NaOH stock,5.50mL water and1.50mL freshly prepared10.0%mass/volume dithionite. K has the same indigo/base quantities but7.00mL water without dithionite. Z has V composition without indigo. Thus V/K nominally contain2.00g/L indigo and all baths0.300M NaOH; V/K differ in reducing condition. Staff freshly prepare small dithionite portions from1.000g filled to10.00mL with water; do not store or heat in sealed vessels.

Staff use20-mL beakers in a hood, loose watch glasses, thermometer and controlled water bath:10min at60±2°C, equal occasional gentle stirring, no boiling. Cool loosely covered to25–30°C and use immediately. Record actual temperature/time, vat age/state. A predicted reduced solution is not an observation. If no usable reduced phase appears or unexpected gas/heat occurs, staff stop/investigate rather than forcing with additional reagent.

For fresh comparison staff prepare a new V bath. D is an aqueous0.500-g-commercial-product/L solution of Direct Red80/Sirius RedF3B(C.I.35780,CAS2610-10-8; Sigma365548 with declared25%dye content), plus10.00g/L NaCl. Example:0.0500g product+1.000g NaCl filled to100.00mL; at25%content this nominally contains0.125g/L dye. No unidentified dye mix,picric acid or mordant. Use D for a qualitative direct cotton comparison for60min at90±2°C under supervision; do not claim equal molar concentrations or quantitative colour strength against indigo. Check lot and safety sheet. This is an original adapted condition, not manufacturer-validated dyeing.

Provide desized, prewashed white100%cotton from one lot,1×3-cm pieces, equally prewetted with water; at least six pieces for V/K, fresh A/B/C and undyed background. Use long forceps,timer,separate labelled rinse/receiving beakers,two fresh20.0-mL water portions per piece at20–25°C,white background and equal lighting.

### Protection and personal performance

Use a school hood,splash goggles,coat and gloves selected from substance/safety sheets. Base is corrosive; dithionite is a reducing agent with dust/decomposition hazards. Keep acids,oxidants,flames and pressure-tight vessels away. Staff handle powders,base,hot liquids and local permission/disposal. Learners handle only the supplied25–30°C vats with forceps,no skin contact or carrying beakers. Stop and notify staff for splashes,respiratory irritation,leaks,unexpected heating or failed exhaust. Do not sniff,taste,apply or take products home.

1. Main case: independently justify hypothesis/K. Immerse matched cotton pieces separately in V/K for30s. Remove and expose for2min under equal light. Record immediate colour/change without copying target colours. Rinse each with its own forceps,15s in each of two fresh20.0-mL water portions. Collect/record both rinses separately and air-dry on white.
2. Fresh case after the independent response: remove A from fresh V after30s and immediately observe2min in air. Observe B through the glass during its30s in V,then remove and document the same2min in air. Submerged observation proves no complete oxygen exclusion. Personally place C in the cool direct bath; staff heat/hold60min at90±2°C and cool to≤30°C before the learner removes C. Give A/B/C the same final rinse. Dip an additional equally prewetted same-lot piece in Z for30s,expose2min and rinse identically as an undyed reagent/fibre-background control. Different concentrations,times and temperatures preclude a quantitative ranking of methods.
3. Record V/K and A/B/C bath state/colour,fibre immediately/in bath/after air,both rinses and deviations. The supervisor actually observes dipping,removal,air exposure and rinsing. Report weak,unexpected or particulate staining unchanged; unreduced indigo may adhere as surface pigment,so K need not remain colourless.
4. Collect all bath/rinse liquid as labelled dye/reducing laboratory waste. Staff prevent incompatible/acid mixing and handle local treatment. Collect contaminated cotton/gloves separately. Assume neither drain permission,learner neutralisation nor successful dyeing.
