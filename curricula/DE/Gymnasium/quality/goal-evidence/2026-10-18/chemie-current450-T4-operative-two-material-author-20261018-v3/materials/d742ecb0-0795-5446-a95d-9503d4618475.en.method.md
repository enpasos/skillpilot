### Channel1: personal surface-tension series/CMC

1. Prepare without foam,fill to the clear liquid meniscus and equilibrate≥15min at25°C. Work low→high,rinsing between conditions and interspersing water checks. Place25.0mL in a clean beaker. Immerse the capillary vertically and measure h from external flat liquid level to the bottom of the concave meniscus,not total capillary length. Read at60/120s; approximate stationarity requires change≤0.5mm. Otherwise continue to300s and report no plateau. Reimmerse/read three times; do not copy repeats. Reject trapped bubbles or poor wetting. Record water hW before/after; drift>1mm or marked hysteresis invalidates the series until staff resolve the cause.
2. Personally measure density from mass of10.00mL in a dry tared vessel at the same temperature,technical duplicates,including water. Supplied water reference γW=71.97mN/m at25°C. Report contact-angle/meniscus assumptions,resolution and scatter; visibly changed wetting cannot be called a proven γ difference. Claim no calibrated ring-tensiometer absolute precision.
