### Task 1

The Si–O backbone with methyl groups differs from the C–C backbone of supplied PE. Identical methyl groups would not establish an identical backbone. Uncrosslinked chains can slide relative to each other; covalent network points restrict sliding and allow recovery in the supplied elastomer. Thus the given crosslinked PDMS suits the required shape stability, whereas the oil does not. Crosslinking changes behaviour at the same polymer chemistry; this does not prove universal superiority of all silicones over all carbon-based polymers.
