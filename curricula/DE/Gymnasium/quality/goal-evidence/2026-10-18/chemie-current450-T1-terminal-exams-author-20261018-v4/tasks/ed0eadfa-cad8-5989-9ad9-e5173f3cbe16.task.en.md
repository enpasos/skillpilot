# Classify substance cards using particle models

Work independently. Data explicitly marked as models are teaching assumptions. Total 12 points. Passing requires 12/12: every essential aspect must be demonstrated. Each task earns 4 performance points only for a complete coherent answer (0–3 for gaps) and 2 points for a justified scope limit (0–1 for gaps). Missing required practical execution or actual exchange cannot be offset by written answers.

### Task 1 (6 points)

Supplied substances: copper with a metallic lattice and mobile electrons; NaCl with a fixed Na+/Cl− lattice; sugar consisting of neutral molecules. The supplied dataset shows solid copper conducts, solid NaCl does not, NaCl solution conducts and pure sugar solution does not. Neither soluble solid remains visibly crystalline after dissolution. Classify by structure and conductivity. Separate observations from particle explanations and predict conductivity of molten NaCl using the supplied model.

Justify the scientific claims and state one specific limit to the inference.

### Task 2 (6 points)

Fresh structural comparison: ethanol CH3CH2OH and dimethyl ether CH3OCH3 both have C2H6O. Equal external conditions are assumed. Ethanol has an O−H group; dimethyl ether has oxygen lone pairs but no O−H bond. A learner claims they must have equal boiling points because their molecular formula is identical. Assign their compound classes and justify a qualitative boiling-point prediction from structure. State the prediction limits.

Justify the scientific claims and state one specific limit to the inference.
