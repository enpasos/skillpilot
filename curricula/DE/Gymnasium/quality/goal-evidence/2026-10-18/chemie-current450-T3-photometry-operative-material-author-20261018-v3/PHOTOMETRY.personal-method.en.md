## Part 3: Personal photometry with Brilliant Blue FCF

This part requires personally performed, observed work. The anonymous model analytes and numbers in Parts 1 and 2 are not measurements from this experiment. Here, use only Brilliant Blue FCF (E133, FD&C Blue No. 1) in deionized water, not a dye mixture or a drink. The following preparations and acceptance limits are an original teaching design, not a certified analytical method.

### Chemicals, equipment and preparation

- The supervisor supplies aqueous Brilliant Blue stock S with assigned content 20.00 mg/L and an independently prepared control stock K with 10.00 mg/L. Record identity, batch, assigned contents, preparation or certificate, date and available uncertainties. K must not be a dilution of S. Without this provenance, an independent trueness check has not been established.
- A separately prepared pure Brilliant Blue sample U has a supervisor-known content between 10 and 15 mg/L. Learners receive its code and chemical identity; its exact assigned content is withheld until submission.
- Use a visible spectrophotometer capable of scanning at least 550–700 nm, absorbance output and documented wavelength resolution/bandwidth; matched 1.00-cm cells; seven 50.00-mL volumetric flasks; a 25-mL graduated pipette with suitable graduations for 2.50–12.50 mL; pipette filler; water; labelled beakers and lint-free wipes. Equipment identity and its manufacturer's instructions must be available at the bench.
- Work under qualified supervision after local chemical/method authorization; wear eye protection and a lab coat. Do not eat, drink or mouth-pipette. Avoid and clean spills; collect dye solutions separately as aqueous laboratory waste. Stop and consult the supervisor for damaged cells, uncertain labels or absent instrument instructions. No heating or organic solvent is used.

### Procedure and assignment

1. Label five flasks 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4 mg/L. Using a pipette conditioned with S, transfer respectively 0, 2.50, 5.00, 7.50 and 10.00 mL S; fill each to 50.00 mL with water, stopper and mix thoroughly. Fill the zero standard with water only. Calculate and record every concentration before measuring. Never return pipetted liquid to a stock bottle.
2. Before changing between S, K and U, wash the pipette with water and condition it with the next solution; use separate stock beakers. For the independent control dilute 12.50 mL K to 50.00 mL: assigned content 2.50 mg/L. For U dilute 10.00 mL to 50.00 mL: dilution factor 5. Label and mix separately. The control is not a calibration point.
3. Stabilize the instrument and perform the water blank adjustment according to its manufacturer. Handle cells by nonoptical surfaces, fill without bubbles, clean the exterior and maintain orientation. Store the instrument's displayed absorbance after this adjustment as the raw measurement variable; also record water response and adjustment time.
4. Scan the personally prepared 3-mg/L solution from 550 to 700 nm. Record wavelength increment and spectral bandwidth; identify the highest measured response and its wavelength. Brilliant Blue is expected to peak near 630 nm. Resolve an edge maximum or a substantially inconsistent spectrum before proceeding. Do not claim finer wavelength accuracy than the instrument and scan support.
5. At the personally determined wavelength, measure zero and standards in ascending order. Rinse with the next solution between changes and fully cover the optical beam. Then measure the control and diluted U three times each, refilling the cell for every reading. Finally recheck water. If its response changes by more than 0.010 absorbance units, investigate, repeat the blank adjustment and remeasure the affected series; retain earlier data.
6. Plot the personal calibration and determine slope and intercept from the personal standards. Inspect residuals and range; do not silently discard a deviating point. Use the supported linear readings. Account for an intercept only once in back-calculation. Interpolate U only within the demonstrated range; otherwise prepare and measure a suitable new dilution.
7. From the three U readings calculate mean, range or standard deviation, diluted concentration and original sample concentration. Separately calculate the control's relative deviation from 2.50 mg/L. This teaching design specifies ±5% as its control limit; passing it does not replace a full measurement-uncertainty budget. If the limit is exceeded, withhold the result as provisional and justify a concrete check of preparation, blank or instrument.
