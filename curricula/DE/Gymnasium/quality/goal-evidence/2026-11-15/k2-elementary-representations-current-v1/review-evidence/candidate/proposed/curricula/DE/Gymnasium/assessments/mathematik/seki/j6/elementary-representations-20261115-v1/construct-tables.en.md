# Construct tables from measurement notes and categories

DRAFT / needs_review · CC-BY-4.0 · SkillPilot

## Task

A: Unordered measurement notes say: −0.5 °C after 1.5 h; −1.5 °C initially; 0.5 °C after 0.5 h. Construct a clear table yourself, with a title, appropriate row/column headings and units. Order by time and explain the measurement at 1.5 h.

B: A club election has 11 people choosing robotics, 8 drama and 6 music. Construct a suitable titled table with meaningful headings. Explain how the counted people are identified and why club names have no physical unit. The category order is unrestricted.

## Evaluation material: release only after submission

A: For example Temperature measurements; time in h / temperature in °C; pairs (0,−1.5),(0.5,0.5),(1.5,−0.5). The measured temperature after 1.5 h was −0.5 °C, not °C/h. B: Club election; club / number of people; robotics–11, drama–8, music–6. Names denote categories; counts denote people. Transposed tables are equivalent.

## Analytic rubric

All data are invented. This is an unpublished author draft. Score each of the four core criteria 0 (no sound performance), 1 (partial), or 2 (at least adequate independent performance). Passing requires adequate evidence for all four core performances. Subject-correct equivalent results, explanations, table orientations and clearly labelled equivalent value representations receive the same core credit; the reference solution is non-exclusive. Units may be stated in headers instead of repeated in every cell. For counts, clearly naming the counted entities, for example “number of people”, is sufficient; no physical unit is required for a category or a count. An isolated transcription or arithmetic slip does not by itself reduce an otherwise adequately and independently demonstrated core criterion where the remaining answer clearly establishes the association, quantity meaning and comparison and the correct setup is visible. This does not excuse a missing core performance, swapped associations, systematic sign/scale errors, the wrong kind of quantity, or unsupported factual claims. No bonus points, hidden conditions, or new result types.

- **construct-tables-1** (0/1/2): A: independently constructs a coherent table, preserves all three associations and orders time; a bare list or independently sorting the two quantities is insufficient. Equivalent fraction/decimal representations and correctly labelled equivalent units are accepted.

- **construct-tables-2** (0/1/2): A: a fitting content title, clear quantity headers and units, and an explanation of the measurement at 1.5 h establish the two measured quantities, without treating the temperature as a rate. A transposed table is equivalent; its explanation may refer to the corresponding column.

- **construct-tables-3** (0/1/2): B: independently constructs a coherent categorical table and retains all three associations; category order and table orientation are unrestricted.

- **construct-tables-4** (0/1/2): B: a fitting title and clear headers distinguish club categories from the number of people; the explanation establishes that club names are not physical quantities. “Number of people” without a repeated cell unit is correct; counts may be explained as dimensionless.

Maximum: 8; passing: 8.
