# Read diagrams with decimal scales and categories

DRAFT / needs_review · CC-BY-4.0 · SkillPilot

## Task

A: Read the following measurement chart. Identify both axes and their units. Read the marked measurements at 0 and 0.5 h, explaining how uniform scaling and signs determine the values.

![Measurement chart with labelled time and temperature axes and three marked points.](/assets/assessment-materials/mathematik/4f265904-c805-57c3-8b40-25a1d32c7e7f/temperature.en.png)

B: Read the following horizontal bar chart. Which axis gives club names and which gives the number of people? Read drama and music and explain a two-person scale step. Distinguish categories from measured/count values.

![Horizontal bar chart with club names and a labelled count axis.](/assets/assessment-materials/mathematik/4f265904-c805-57c3-8b40-25a1d32c7e7f/clubs.en.png)

## Evaluation material: release only after submission

A: Horizontal time in h, vertical temperature in °C; −0.5 °C at 0 h and 0.5 °C at 0.5 h. Equal marked vertical steps denote 0.5 °C; the negative value lies below zero. B: Clubs/categories vertically, numbers of people horizontally. Drama 6 people, music 10; one tick interval denotes 2 people. Category labels are not numerical temperature or time coordinates.
