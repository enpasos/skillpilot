# Source basis for the bounded final Physics review

Read on 15 November 2026. These are scientific and teaching references for the
local wording/profile corrections, not claims about any official Land year.
No external source is republished. Final candidate acceptance is separate.

- [OpenStax, University Physics 2, 11.3](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/11-3-motion-of-a-charged-particle-in-a-magnetic-field): equations 11.5–11.8 separate perpendicular radius and parallel advance. With magnetic force alone, speed stays constant. The angle comparison must hold speed, mass, charge magnitude and field strength fixed. Our explicit static-field/no-other-force wording bounds the school model.
- [F. Bezanilla, University of Chicago, The Nerve Impulse](https://nerve.bsd.uchicago.edu/med98a.htm): the Nernst example specifies KCl in both compartments and permeability only to K+. The sections on multiple channels and resting conductance distinguish zero total current from zero current through every channel. The membrane circuit includes reversal-potential batteries with conductances; a passive RC network alone does not supply regenerative energy. Potential convention is inside relative to outside. The revised profile should use one explicit model consistently, with changed cases within that model.
- [Peder Larson, Principles of MRI, Spatial Encoding](https://larsonlab.github.io/MRI-education-resources/Spatial%20Encoding.html): the frequency-encoding gradient acts during acquisition; a phase-encoding gradient precedes it in the stated Cartesian sequence. A simple causal explanation must permit overlap of the relevant operations, rather than invent one universal four-card order.
- [Peder Larson, Principles of MRI, Bloch Equation](https://larsonlab.github.io/MRI-education-resources/Bloch%20Equation.html): the background field remains present through RF excitation; transverse magnetization decays while longitudinal magnetization recovers. These are distinct processes, with separate time constants. No quantitative Bloch-equation mastery is added to the qualitative goal.
- [DEGA Recommendation 101, March 2006](https://www.ald-laerm.de/fileadmin/dega-akustik.de/publikationen/DEGA_Empfehlung_101.pdf): section 1.3.1, printed page 13, defines sound pressure level using RMS pressure and a reference pressure; A3.5/A3.6, printed pages A11–A12, distinguish pressure level from perceived loudness. Used only for these stable definitions, not a claim about current legal standards. A normalized spectrum is insufficient for an absolute calibrated pressure level or individual perceived loudness.

The optical-instrument correction is a contract-scope check: the authored goal
says telescope **or** microscope. Requiring independent mastery of both without
an additional source-bound scope decision would enlarge the promised skill.
Three demonstrations can instead vary geometry and interpretation inside one
chosen model, with the other model offered as an alternative.

NCBI chapter and PMC page opens encountered browser checks; search snippets were
not treated as full-page reading. The University of Minnesota loudness page was
inspected but not used as the defining authority because its simplified wording
mixes the distinctions under review. No classroom effectiveness is inferred.
