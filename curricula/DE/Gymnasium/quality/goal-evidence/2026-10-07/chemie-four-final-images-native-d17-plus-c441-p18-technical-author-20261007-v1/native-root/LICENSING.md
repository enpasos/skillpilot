# SkillPilot licensing

## Project rule

**Unsere Software steht unter Apache-2.0. Unsere eigenen Skill-Landschaften und Lerninhalte
stehen unter CC BY 4.0. Fremde Inhalte behalten ihre eigenen Bedingungen.**

This is the binding project rule, not a choice between two licenses for the
same material.
It grants the rights that exist and that SkillPilot or the contributing rights
holders are entitled to license; it does not create copyright in facts or
other unprotected material.

The full texts are [Apache License, Version 2.0](LICENSE) and
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt).
The identifiers above are the SPDX license expressions used in new metadata.
Allocation follows the material's function, not its file extension: a JSON
schema is technical infrastructure; a JSON skill landscape is content.
Educational material passed to the coach remains content, not software.

The content grant includes our rights in individual contributions, selection
and arrangement, and any applicable sui generis database rights. We introduce
no additional database license, CC0 default or general dual licensing.
Both licenses allow commercial reuse subject to their terms; they do not
require recipients to publish their own improvements. This is not a promise
of compatibility with every other license or partner's import policy.

## Attribution and reuse

For project-owned contributions, the licensor is **enpasos - Enterprise
Patterns & Solutions GmbH**; contributors license the rights they retain in
their own contributions. Preserve the supplied author, copyright, source and
other applicable notices; the SkillPilot name does not replace individual
credits or third-party attribution.

For content reuse, this is a suggested starting point, not an extra condition:

> SkillPilot — [content title, version/date and source link], CC BY 4.0
> (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: [describe changes,
> if any]. Additional supplied credits and notices: [retain as applicable].

CC BY 4.0 permits attribution in a reasonable manner suited to the medium and
context, including links to the required information. A suitable consolidated
credits/source page can serve a collection; we do not demand a notice beside
every learning goal. Retain supplied notices, identify modifications and
include the license link or text. Software redistribution follows Apache-2.0,
including its license-text, applicable notice and modification requirements.
Neither license is a warranty of correctness or suitability.

## Third-party material, AI and private data

- Dependencies, fonts, imported media, official curricula, retained source
  files and quotations keep their own rights and conditions. A source citation
  does not by itself authorize redistribution. Their presence in the repository
  does not make them SkillPilot-owned.
- Linked books, videos, simulations and services retain the actual provider's
  conditions. Curating a link licenses our contribution, not its destination;
  compiler and content provider are distinct roles.
- Mixed material must identify third-party portions and their conditions.
  Unclear origin must not silently become an own-work license declaration.
  The two-license rule does not clear unresolved rights in an entire artifact.
- `AI-generated` and `SkillPilot-curated` describe provenance, not licenses or
  proof of ownership. Our applicable license covers only rights we can grant;
  neither AI use nor review guarantees copyright or freedom from third-party
  rights. Source evidence and quality review remain separate.
- Names, logos and trademarks are not licensed for unrestricted use by this
  policy. Private learner/session/chat data, credentials and user uploads are
  outside this public grant. Hosted-service and AI-account terms are separate.

## Applying the rule without extra hurdles

Use these defaults automatically for clearly attributable own material.
Document genuine exceptions where they occur; do not introduce a manual
license approval for each learning goal, a learner consent step, or a new
claim that every fact or AI image is protected. Rights/source checks for
third-party or unclear material and existing quality gates remain independent.

Exports carry the applicable license text, this scope notice and the
relevant attribution/source/change notices, including for subsets. Technical
exporters must not relabel a mixed package as wholly Apache-2.0 or wholly
CC-BY-4.0, and license metadata must not fabricate redistribution clearance.

Contributions follow the license for their area. Contributors must be entitled
to grant those rights and identify included third-party material, as explained
in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Payment for commissioned work alone is
not evidence of sufficient licensing rights.
