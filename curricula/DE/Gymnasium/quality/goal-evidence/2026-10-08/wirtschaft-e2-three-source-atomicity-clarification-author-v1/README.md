# Wirtschaft E2: three open source and atomicity decisions

The current ordinary goal IDs `6618c8bf`, `c49497e8`, and `25c700f8` stay active
and open. This package performs no live classification, target exclusion,
source remapping, graph edit, positive-profile approval or image generation.
It is a bounded source/atomicity author dossier for independent adjudication.

The existing BY extraction preserves generic extension-competency sentences
but omits the subsequently listed content. The actual official
[WR8 source](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/gymnasium/8/wirtschaft-und-recht)
and [WR10 source](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/gymnasium/10/wirtschaft-und-recht/wwg)
were read in the relevant sections. Their detailed subjects include independent
content directions; the current prerequisites do not explicitly establish
them all. The observed gap concerns these three current goals. Earlier source
reviews and valid other-goal evidence are not restarted or overwritten.

`whole-goals.original.json` captures the three actual current whole goals.
`current-whole-prerequisite-source-context.inert.json` contains every current
direct and reverse prerequisite whole object and the exact source-snapshot
goal. Neither a snapshot summary nor an own operationalisation is presented
as an official detailed quotation.

`twelve-source-units.proposals.inert.json` makes possible bounded competencies
reviewable with DE/EN text, stable provisional IDs and individual atomicity
rationales. They are proposals, not twelve adopted new goals. In particular,
the online-review unit may already fit `d3c11bfa`, and the free-choice/nudging
unit strongly overlaps `1d989a89`; independently justified reuse may avoid
duplicates. Word-search absence alone does not prove semantic absence.

An independent decision must determine the smallest source-faithful semantic
change: demonstrate a concrete bounded interpretation of an original atom
without deleting source aspects, or retain its stable ID as a real area only
when reviewed children/reused atoms represent the full applicable scope. A
source-faithful split needs current mapping, placement, prerequisite, A/M,
D/P and visualization evidence for the affected current goals. Relabelling
three generic atoms solely to remove them from the denominator is excluded.

No child graph or new strict denominator is installed before that decision.
The original graph, source/applicability fields, historical artifacts and
protected Math/Physics M7 floors remain unchanged. No new P claims or images
are authored for an unresolved unit.
