# Wirtschaft E1-17: description and positive-understanding criteria

Functional review instructions: Apache-2.0. Own curricular/evidence texts:
CC-BY-4.0. Criteria are not a review verdict.

Keep adequate current German wording; translate titleEn/descriptionEn with the
same assessable scope. Review whole goals, native pages, prerequisites, reverse
relations, source/applicability and final images. Related aspects can form one
assessable causal analysis, comparison, description or judgement; independent
skills remain a concrete atomicity question.

Every profile must identify its actual conceptual distinction and observable
content-specific reasoning. Require fresh examples/variation and transfer, not
recitation of labels. Hypothetical data and supplied model descriptions are
explicit didactic cases, not factual reports about current society.

Distinguish aggregate and individual change, group averages and distributions,
correlation and established causes, policy objectives and actual outcomes,
technical opportunity and guaranteed benefit. Socialisation combines structural
conditions and agency; social origin does not determine every decision. Models
must be compared on the same case and their stated assumptions and limits.
Business adaptation needs costs, demand, process, quality or flexibility
mechanisms instead of an unconditional automation narrative.

Migration, cultural exchange, group perspectives and gender roles must use
specific experiences and institutional conditions. Avoid assuming a homogeneous
group opinion, innate group ability or an immutable culture. Explain within-group
variation and cross-group shared interests when the supplied material supports
them. Integration concerns participation and access, not automatic one-way
assimilation. Network resources involve mobilisable information, trust or support,
not simply money or number of contacts.

Two independent description rounds must read their own bounded final inputs
without the author's preferred verdict or other-round results. Record actual
run identity, observed tool/model information, hashes and times. Resolve actual
findings; relevant adopted changes require final affected-context review.

Profiles stay ai_candidate/needs_human_review, E1/G1 unless actual further
evidence supports another authority. Expected cases are not real learner
performance, human approval or trial. Images require actual separate review.
Current valid A/M evidence for unchanged goals must not be restarted; adopted
translations require targeted semantic parity and binding verification. No
runtime, private-data or publishing changes follow from this author package.
