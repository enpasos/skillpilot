# Chemistry positive-understanding-evidence profile criteria v1

Author each record under the closed `positive-understanding-evidence-v2`
contract. AI-authored records remain `needs_human_review` with
`reviewAuthority: "ai_candidate"`, normally bounded to E1/G1. The profile
guides future assessment and does not claim mastery, human approval, or a
runtime change.

- Select one supported archetype. State one or more exact, content-specific
  expectations with matching independent observable performances in German and
  English. Require two distinct demonstrations, a fresh meaningful variation,
  and independent transfer. Give two concrete fresh case briefs with expected
  reasoning, not just an answer key.
- Preserve the goal's actual source and learner-facing projection. In a
  cross-stage goal, use the common competence core and age-appropriate cases.
  Do not infer nationwide scope from canonical applicability alone.
- Distinguish observation, particle model, chemical equation, graph, and
  judgment. Balance atoms and charge where equations are needed, and keep
  substances, particles, and energy flows physically meaningful.
- For energy profiles, separate reaction energy from activation energy;
  catalysts lower an alternative barrier while leaving reactant and product
  levels unchanged. Quantitative comparisons state a common amount or energy
  basis and relevant system boundary.
- For experiments, identify variables and controls, measurements or supplied
  data, uncertainty or limitations, and a defensible chemical inference.
  Use supervised or data-based alternatives for fires, hazardous reagents, or
  fumes; never ask for unsupervised dangerous activity.
- At least one case must stand independently of the reviewed visualization.
  Avoid tasks solvable by recognizing a diagram, repeating a coach cue, or
  substituting new numbers into the same pattern. Use a chemically changed
  condition that can reveal shallow understanding.
- Keep the two languages semantically equivalent. Record any unresolved
  scientific or source concern in `dissent`; never manufacture consensus or
  human attestation.
