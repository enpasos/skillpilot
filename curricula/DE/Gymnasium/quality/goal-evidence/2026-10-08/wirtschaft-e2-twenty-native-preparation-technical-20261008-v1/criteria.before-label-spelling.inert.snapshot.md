# Wirtschaft E2-20: bounded description and understanding-evidence criteria

Own functional review instructions: Apache-2.0. Own curricular and evidence
content: CC-BY-4.0. This file supplies authoring criteria, not review verdicts.

Review each whole DE/EN learning goal, current native page, direct and reverse
prerequisite context, source/applicability context and the actual final image.
Keep adequate existing German wording. English fields must be genuine,
scope-preserving translations. Never broaden a goal from its title alone or
infer curricular source equivalence from a successful schema check.

The assessment unit is the specific causal explanation, comparison,
interpretation or judgement requested by the goal. Related dimensions can be
parts of that one unit; independent routines remain an atomicity issue. Identify
a concrete remaining gap rather than adding stylistic clauses.

Positive-understanding-evidence-v2 expectations must state the actual subject
distinction, observable reasoning and fresh transfer. Data cases must preserve
units, denominator, nominal/real distinctions and aggregation limits. Explain
GDP, HDI and environmental measures through their distinct dimensions. Averages
do not establish individual distribution. The HDI income component is based on
GNI per capita; it is not simply GDP per capita and contains no ecological score.

Competition cases must distinguish efficiency possibilities, market power,
alternatives, entry barriers, coordination and mergers. Size or concentration
alone does not prove harm or legality. Environmental cases must distinguish
private incentives, third-party effects, good characteristics and instrument
mechanisms. Public goods are non-rival and non-excludable in the stated model;
shared fish stocks are rival common-pool resources. Recycling, reuse and repair
are distinct; sustainability needs a justified life-cycle boundary.

Consumer and behavioural cases must distinguish preferences, restrictions,
information and identifiable presentation effects. Do not claim psychological
causation from one observed purchase. Financing cases must distinguish equity,
debt, cash flow, investment loss and personal liability. Legal-form, guarantee
and authority arrangements in the examples are explicitly stipulated didactic
cases, not current legal advice or fabricated statements about statutory rules.

Independent description rounds must read their own bounded inputs without the
author's preferred verdict or the other round's results. Record actual run
identity, observed tool/model information, input/output hashes and times. After
an adopted relevant change, review the final affected context. Unresolved
REVlSE/split/source/image issues stay open. Profile/schema validity alone
does not supply an independent description review or actual image inspection.

Profiles remain ai_candidate/needs_human_review with E1/G1 unless actual
different evidence supports another status. Examples describe expected
performance, not real learners, human review or trials. Separate human release
gates remain intact. No runtime, private-data or publication work is authorised
by this candidate package.
