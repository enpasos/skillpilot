GOAL 5 dfaef897-dcd4-55d4-a1fe-3dd90b818853 Zuckerkonfigurationen und Anomere darstellen / Represent sugar configurations and anomers
Die lernende Person kann Glucose und Fructose in Fischer- und Haworth-Projektion darstellen, den Ringschluss durch den intramolekularen nucleophilen Angriff einer Hydroxygruppe auf die Carbonylgruppe erklären und D-/L-Konfiguration sowie Anomerie anhand der betreffenden Stereozentren unterscheiden.
The learner can represent glucose and fructose in Fischer and Haworth projections, explain ring closure through intramolecular nucleophilic attack of a hydroxy group on the carbonyl group, and distinguish D/L configuration and anomerism using the relevant stereocentres.

PROFILE.expectations[0].observablePerformanceEn = A is D-glucose and B L-glucose; the highest-numbered stereocentre C5 determines D/L (OH right/left). All four centres are mirrored: enantiomers. In the usual Haworth orientation (O upper right, C1 right), C2-OH is down, C3-OH up, C4-OH down and C5-CH2OH up; C1-OH is α down or β up. PROFILE.expectations[1].observablePerformanceEn = F is D-fructose and F* L-fructose because C5-OH is right/left; this does not give rotation sign. Fructose is a ketose (C2=O), glucose an aldose (C1=O). O5 or O6 attacks C2; the π pair moves to O2 and proton transfer produces a hemiketal (within the curriculum's broader hemiacetal wording), without condensation. Furanose: O5–C2–C3–C4–C5; pyranose: O6–C2–C3–C4–C5–C6. New C2 is anomeric. In the usual Haworth orientation with ring O above/right and C2 on the right, D-furanose has C3-OH up, C4-OH down and C5-CH2OH(C6) up; C2-OH is α down or β up, with C1-CH2OH opposite. D-pyranose has C3-OH up, C4-OH down and C5-OH down; C6 is ring CH2. C2-OH is again α down or β up, with C1-CH2OH opposite. D/L uses retained C5 configuration. These are two ring sizes with an anomer pair each, not four mutual enantiomers. F/F* are mirrors; changing α/β alone does not change D.
