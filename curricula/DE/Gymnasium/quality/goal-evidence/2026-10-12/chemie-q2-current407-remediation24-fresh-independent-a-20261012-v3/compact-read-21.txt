GOAL 21 9abd8069-17ad-5d30-9102-f8eff6055045 Nahrungsbestandteile nach Polarität und Resorption einordnen / Classify food components by polarity and resorption

The learner can classify food components as hydrophilic, lipophilic, or amphiphilic and use this to justify vitamin resorption, residence time in the body, and suitable solvents in food preparation.
