PROFILE.expectations[0].observablePerformanceEn = One justified approach treats food-grade status as a prerequisite, then weights waste prevention and resource use: use A in cold food and the existing oxidised B in the approved soap process rather than discard it. Other data-consistent weighted judgments are valid; B's lack of food-grade status is binding.
