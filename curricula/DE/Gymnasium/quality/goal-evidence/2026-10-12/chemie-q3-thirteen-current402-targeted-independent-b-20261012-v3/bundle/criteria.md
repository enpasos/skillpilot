# Goal-evidence review criteria v1

Apply these criteria independently to every cited learning goal:

- **Source and wording fidelity:** title and description are correct,
  understandable, suitably atomic, and do not claim more than the competence.
- **Observable understanding:** the evidence profile names observable facets,
  not only topics or activities.
- **Contrast and limitation:** where alternatives matter, the learner must
  experience cases that reveal genuine advantages and limitations.
- **Cue resistance:** highlighted images, answer options, leading wording, and
  coach-supplied explanations do not count as independent mastery evidence.
- **Transfer and independence:** checks vary the relevant case and require an
  unaided explanation, comparison, construction, or application as appropriate.
- **Misconceptions:** plausible shallow strategies and false positives are
  represented, along with evidence that would disconfirm them.
- **Prerequisite coherence:** direct `requires` relations are sufficient,
  non-circular, and consistent with the page order and neighboring goals.
- **Scope discipline:** one observed failure can block or correct that item,
  but broader rules require replication and a stated mechanism.
- **Accessibility and presentation:** the page, image, labels, links, and
  description are legible and do not leak the intended answer.
- **Privacy:** publication and review artifacts contain curriculum data only,
  never learner identity, state, conversations, or credentials.

Use `NORMATIVE` only for an actual privacy, safety, accessibility, source,
provider-policy, or factual requirement. Do not use reviewer confidence,
majority agreement, or professional role as a substitute for evidence.
