## What the contracts actually require

`goalBookSourceAtlasInputs.ts` selects only authoritative `curricularAtomic`
definitions and generates direct `goalEntry` references for their source scope
union. Its `sourceAtlasDescendants` function records structural inheritance and
respects existing supplement boundaries. It does not add prerequisite closure,
orientation, curricular areas or `prerequisiteOnly` references. Therefore a
missing item in that target list is not by itself a missing canonical definition
or an absent learner route. The source atlas and authored learner views serve
different purposes.

`collectCompositionProjectionRoleGoalIds` honors authored `target` and
`prerequisiteOnly` roles, direct-reference specificity, and subtree distance.
It does not infer support roles from `requires`, a phase, a source omission, or
prior-learning plausibility. Our role matrix uses that existing API.

The backend preserves direct canonical requirements: when an authored projection
omits a known direct requirement from both target and support roles, the frontier
fails closed. It skips only unrelated omitted requirements inherited from legacy
clusters. Known support still requires actual mastery. An out-of-scope prerequisite
is not automatically mastered. These branches are in `LearnerService.java` around
5882 and 6079; effective ancestor requirements are collected around 10873.

`CompositionViewService.findLearnerScopeView` requires exact school form and stage.
Most jurisdiction-specific Chemistry views are `CrossStage`; they must not be
presented as evidence that a committed `SekII` offering selects them. The diagnostic
separately records exact-stage authored membership and CrossStage membership.
The exact-stage inspection selects the compatible authored definition with the
most specific scope, following the repository contract. It does not instantiate
a learner, execute the package/runtime projection or verify runtime course
filtering, learning, or mastery. Thus `target` in this report is **authored
membership evidence**, not runtime acceptance.

The central M7 implementation intersects exact-current D/P/A/M/V gates over the
authoritative curricular atoms (`reportDeepUnderstandingRollout.ts`, around 1640).
It has no additional requirement that every prerequisite be a curricular target
in every source atlas scope. Consequently these 147 list comparisons cannot be
converted mechanically into 147 M7 failures, or mechanically cleared. Real
scientific prerequisite, operator or provenance faults still require their own
exact-current remediation and review.
