# Primary-source observations before final book review

Observed independently on 2026-10-05. These are source facts, not D decisions.
Full official-source extracted text is not stored in this review folder.

- Hessen live official [KC chemistry PDF](https://kultus.hessen.de/sites/kultus.hessen.de/files/2026-07/kcgo_chemie_21.04.2026.pdf):
  Ausgabe 2024, Stand 21.04.2026; 52 pages; E.1 and E.2 printed/PDF page 35.
  The registered retained local PDF instead states Stand 01.08.2025, also
  52 pages and E.1/E.2 on page 35. Their normalized E.1/E.2 page contents have
  the same SHA-256, recorded in `primary-source-facts.json`. Neither version
  date may be replaced by the other merely because those clauses agree.
- The older official [_0 PDF](https://kultus.hessen.de/sites/kultus.hessen.de/files/2024-11/kerncurriculum_gymnasiale_oberstufe-chemie_0.pdf)
  states Ausgabe 2024 without the later Stand date; it has 50 pages and the
  relevant E.1/E.2 clauses on page 33. Its operators and substantive clauses
  about metal-displacement observations, electron donor/acceptor pairs, and
  hydrochloric acid/sodium hydroxide titration with concentration calculation
  agree with the later relevant clauses. Its page number is historical.
- Live [Bavaria Chemie 9, C9.6](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/gymnasium/9/chemie/ch):
  the relevant expectation describes metal behaviour in metal-salt solutions,
  interprets that behaviour as particle-level redox, and makes electron
  release/uptake explicit through partial redox equations. The heading is
  Chemie 9 (HG, SG, MuG, WWG, SWG); NTG uses a different section number.
- Live [Bavaria Chemie 13 grundlegend, C13.3](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/gymnasium/13/chemie/grundlegend):
  the relevant expectation combines interpreting titration curves, calculating
  characteristic points, and deriving substance and suitable-indicator
  information. The neighbouring expectation separately plans, performs and
  documents titration for concentration determination; another separately
  names half-titration. These neighbouring clauses must not be imported
  silently into an individual goal.
- Bremen's registered retained `GyO_Chemie_2022.pdf` states Stand 2022.
  Its page 23 was read both as PDF text and as the rendered table: simple pH
  titration-curve interpretation and concentration determination appear above
  the LK marker; multiprotonic curve interpretation appears beneath it.
  The current official [LIS search result](https://www.lis.bremen.de/detail.php?font=0&max=10&search%5Bsend%5D=true&search%5Bvt%5D=unterrichten&skip=20&template=20_search_d)
  still links the registered PDF, but both web open/click and an independent
  HTTP fetch returned 404. The retained primary PDF supports the observed
  2022 clauses; successful live retrieval or current byte identity is not
  claimed.
