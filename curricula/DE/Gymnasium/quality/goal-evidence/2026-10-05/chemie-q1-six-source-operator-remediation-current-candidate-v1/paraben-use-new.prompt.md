Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: German Gymnasium chemistry learning-goal illustration, PNG landscape approximately 16:9, native size near 1600 x 900.
Primary request: a friendly, abstract and clear comic illustration helping students orient themselves to judging the use of parabens as preservatives. Large German title "Parabene: Verwendung beurteilen". Pastel blue, green and warm orange panels, bold outlined cartoon laboratory objects, enough empty space for readability at 360px width.
Three simple connected parts:
Left: "Struktur". Show an accurate single methylparaben structure: a benzene hexagon with three alternating double bonds, an OH substituent at the top carbon, and at the opposite bottom carbon an outward substituent drawn as C(=O)-O-CH3. The ring must have six carbons, OH and the ester group must be opposite (para), the carbonyl is outside the aromatic ring, O has correct valence. Label "p-Hydroxybenzoesäureester". If necessary use a large simplified structural illustration, never a five-membered ring or COOH in place of the ester.
Middle: "Wirkung prüfen". Two cartoon microorganism culture dishes and a small comparative-data card show that efficacy is examined from observations under defined conditions. A friendly shield motif may suggest inhibition, but no dead-everything picture and no claim of sterility or guaranteed safety. Short label "Wirkungsdaten".
Right: "Verwendung prüfen". A small product/container icon next to a magnifying glass over a dated document and a balance represents checking the particular product, dosage and legal material. Short labels "Produkt", "Dosierung", "datierte Regeln". No numeric regulatory limit, green blanket approval seal, named country, universal permission or medical advice. The visual should show consideration and comparison, not announce approval.
Keep key text large. No long paragraphs, tiny table cells, photorealism, sterile engineering style, logos or technical identifiers. This is a learning orientation, not a scientific source or a performed experiment.
