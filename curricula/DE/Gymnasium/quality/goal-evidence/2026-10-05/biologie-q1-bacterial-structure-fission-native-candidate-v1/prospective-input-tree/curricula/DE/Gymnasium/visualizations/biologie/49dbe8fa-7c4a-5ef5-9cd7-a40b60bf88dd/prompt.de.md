Create one friendly, abstract, clear comic-style biology learning illustration as an opaque PNG, horizontal about 16:9, native size near 1600 x 900. LEFT: one rounded rod-shaped bacterium with exactly ONE large closed loop representing its chromosomal DNA directly in cytoplasm, no nucleus. Subtle cell wall and membrane outlines are fine; keep chromosome visible and not enclosed in a nucleus.
Exact large title: "Bakterielle Zweiteilung". Three short large panel labels: "Ausgangszelle", "Kopie und Verteilung", "Zwei Zellen". Short footer: "Vereinfachtes Modell".
