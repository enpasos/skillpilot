The additional sigma-complex net +1 lies in the ring; the attacked carbon bears H and NO₂ and is sp³. The full pi ring is interrupted and nonaromatic. / HSO₄⁻ oxygen pair→H; C–H pair→ring bond. H⁺ rather than H⁻ transfers, leaving the electrons in the ring. C₆H₆+NO₂⁺+HSO₄⁻→C₆H₅NO₂+H₂SO₄ has zero charge on both sides.
