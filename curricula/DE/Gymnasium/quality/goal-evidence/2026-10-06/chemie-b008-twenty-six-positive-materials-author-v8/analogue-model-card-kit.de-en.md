# Printable text model cards / Druckbare Text-Modellkarten

SkillPilot authored candidate, CC-BY-4.0. This is a text working kit, not a new
learning-goal image or a programme-generated SVG. Printing/learner use has not
been performed or accepted.

## Lower material/reaction kit / Unteres Materie-/Reaktionsset

Prepare labelled paper tokens / beschriftete Papierkarten bereitstellen:
4 × H, 2 × O; 6 × Na⁺, 6 × Cl⁻; 8 × H–O–H cards, with O end negative and H
ends positive. Keep particle species/charge visible. A solid/lattice region,
solution region and reaction-arrangement region are labelled on blank paper.
Do not present tokens as physical atom photographs.

## Upper organic kit / Oberes organisches Set

Provide paper/physical molecular segments for the hypothetical scaffold
⁺H3N–CH2–CH2–C(=O)–NH–CH2–C6H5. Labels identify ammonium, carbonyl oxygen,
amide plane and phenyl plane. A teacher-supplied spatial kit/cards must expose
tetrahedral versus planar regions; a flat text chain alone is not the spatial
performance. Provide receptor contact labels: negative / negativ,
hydrogen-bond donor / H-Brücken-Donor, hydrophobic / hydrophob.
Two posed versions L and L2 can be physically arranged; L2 is deliberately
misaligned. Learner arrangement is retained as own work, not prefilled here.

## Substrate/enzyme kit / Substrat-/Enzymset

S: R–C(=O)–O–R′ ; H2O; E contact model able to adjust; ES combined state;
P1: R–C(=O)–OH ; P2: R′–OH; inhibitor I binds without conversion.
Use reversible binding and a separate conversion arrow; release E after
conversion. This is a simplified model, not real protein or clinical proof.
