# Scientific fact checks for Bio10 AUTHOR candidates

All cases are original synthetic teaching designs. No real experiments, clinical assessment, private data, human approval or release trial. External source rights remain separate; the portable official HE curriculum copy is not relicensed as own material.

- NEI: optical focusing and retinal conversion precede optic-nerve information to brain; no light carried inside nerve. [Primary or authoritative source](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/healthy-vision/how-eyes-work).

- Scientific review: afferent route, ganglion-cell axons and partial chiasmal crossing; thalamic relay and visual cortex. [Primary or authoritative source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21601061/).

- Scientific review: retinal photoreceptor/bipolar/ganglion sequence and contralateral visual-field organization; schematic disruptions bounded, not clinical diagnoses. [Primary or authoritative source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8053434/).

- NIDCD: sound mechanics, cochlear hair-bundle deflection and subsequent neural signal; cochlear base/high and apex/low frequency. [Primary or authoritative source](https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/how-do-we-hear).

- NIDCD: damaging noise may injure inner-ear structures; no intentional exposure test proposed. [Primary or authoritative source](https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/how-does-noise-damage-your-hearing).

- IASP definitions: nociception and pain are distinct; sensory activity alone does not establish the personal experience. Peripheral/central sensitization are bounded mechanisms, not individual diagnoses. [Primary or authoritative source](https://www.iasp-pain.org/resources/terminology/?ItemNumber=1698).

- NIDDK: type1 autoimmune beta-cell loss; type2 reduced response and inadequate compensation; diverse ages and multifactorial contexts. [Primary or authoritative source](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes).

- NIDDK: genetics and environment/lifestyle can contribute; risk factors do not supply an individual diagnosis or blame. [Primary or authoritative source](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/symptoms-causes).

- NIDDK: beta cells release insulin at high blood glucose, alpha cells glucagon at low; glucagon signals liver release. No hormone-as-glucose-carrier claim. [Primary or authoritative source](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/news/archive/2021/story-discovery-medications-diabetes-obesity-emerged-research-pancreatic-hormone).

[NIH scientific vision](https://braininitiative.nih.gov/sites/default/files/documents/brain2025_508c.pdf).

- Scientific work distinguishes explicit episodic memory involving hippocampal/medial-temporal systems from other memory systems, supporting rejection of one exclusive memory store. [Research synthesis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC33628/).
