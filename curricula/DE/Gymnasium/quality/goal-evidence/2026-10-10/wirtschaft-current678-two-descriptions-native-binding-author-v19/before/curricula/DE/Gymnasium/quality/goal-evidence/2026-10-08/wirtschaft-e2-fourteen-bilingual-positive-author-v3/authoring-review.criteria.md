# Wirtschaft E2-14: bounded authoring and understanding-evidence criteria

Own functional review instructions: Apache-2.0. Own curricular and evidence
content: CC-BY-4.0. These criteria supply no preferred review verdict.

Read the whole current goal, genuine scope-preserving English translation,
native page, source/applicability and direct/reverse prerequisite context.
Adequate German goals remain unchanged. Three separately handled E2
placeholders are outside this authoring scope.

Profiles assess the actual connected explanation, comparison, decision or
model application, with individually specified fresh cases. Identify concrete
missing understanding rather than imposing stylistic additions or an extra
task quota. Expected performance is not an observation of actual learners.

Environmental conflicts require explicit interests, effects, distribution and
normative criteria. Efficiency, sufficiency and consistency differ. A single
ecological, social or financial improvement is not total sustainability.

Consumer cases distinguish defect remedies, an applicable statutory withdrawal
right, voluntary guarantees and their conditions. Use provided current legal
extracts; do not invent universal return rights or actual deadlines beyond
verified sources. Separate a contractual entitlement from enforcement and
evidence. These are fictional education cases, not personal legal advice.

Money cases distinguish unit of account, means of payment and store of value,
nominal amounts, real purchasing power, general prices and relative prices.
Investment cases have fictional options and supplied risk/return conditions.
Expected return is not guaranteed; liquidity and safety differ. No personal
investment or credit recommendation is authored here.

Budget cases require an actual spreadsheet plan and linked formulas with a
consistent period. A forecast shortfall is not an already realised negative
bank balance. Credit cases assess combined commitments, total costs, duration
and uncertainty, not only the apparent size of one instalment.

Market models separate shifts from movements and retain assumptions.
Digital-market judgements require both supplier and consumer consequences;
stars, network size or low headline prices do not independently prove quality
or welfare. Behavioural explanations require a plausible mechanism and a
controlled relevant comparison. Nudging ethics include actual choice,
transparency, interests and evidence; desired effects are not proven effects.

Separate blind description rounds must inspect their own bounded final inputs
and actual final images. Record actual identities, times and hashes. Unresolved
findings remain open. Profile schema validity is a technical check and never
supplies independent description review, image approval or human acceptance.
Profiles remain needs_human_review/ai_candidate E1/G1 until actual evidence
supports a different claim. Human release and trial gates remain separate.
