## Scientific findings

- A full preparation competence cannot be inferred from a convenient instructional sequence. Examples rejected here include making solutions before interpreting supplied conductivity data; all five-acid/four-base names before calculating a strong-acid pH; electrophilic addition before classifying polymers; and actually conducting purity experiments before analysing supplied food data. Each rejected edge has its own current-task reason in the frozen physical file.
- Quantitative titration genuinely needs the meaning of substance amount where the current tasks calculate reacting particle amounts, stoichiometric factors and aliquot corrections. Actual shared original titration requirements support specific positive cases; unrelated indicator or inherited parent-topic cells were not treated as enough.
- A named analytical test does not alone prove that the original requires its actual execution. The current full practical prerequisite was held where the original merely names Fehling tests, says “Nachweis”, or distinguishes reducing carbohydrates. Positive practical cases bind an actual execution operator, such as NI's shared iodine–starch experiment and the two separately read Bavarian test clauses.
- Several inherited source anchors name a nearby substance class while the current dependent asks for critical research, criteria-based judgment or sustainable comparison. In particular, protein function is not a source for sustainable protein-source evaluation, and a halogen-hydrocarbon topic is not automatically a demand for critical FCKW/HFKW research.
