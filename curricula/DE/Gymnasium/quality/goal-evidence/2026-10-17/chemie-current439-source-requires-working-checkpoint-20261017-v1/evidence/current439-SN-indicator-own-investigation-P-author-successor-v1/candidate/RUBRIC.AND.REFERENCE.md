# Rubric and reference orientation — keep separate from own raw observations

Applies to the exact whole successor profile and both complete operative cases. This is an authored assessment contract, not an assessment result. Do not prefill learner records with this sheet.

| Required positive evidence | What the assessor must actually see | What leaves the requirement unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Own experimental action | After actual school release, the learner personally labels and samples the defined solutions, applies the correct fresh indicator product under supervision and uses the matching original chart; the observing teacher can attribute the actions | Only reading supplied observations, watching the teacher/partner, describing intended steps, signing a copied account |
| Own observation and control | Contemporaneous raw colour/pattern, conditions, material identifiers and actually observed known P7 control precede the code key and interpretation | Model colours called observations; source/supplier target pH copied as a measurement; absent/implausible control not investigated |
| All three classes and justified rough ranges | The actual pattern supports acidic, approximately neutral and alkaline classification, with the relevant chart field or neighbouring fields identified | One colour name without its chart; generic diagram or another indicator's chart; unsupported precision or hiding ambiguity |
| Fresh independent transfer | In case 2 the learner actually uses 90201, the fresh citric acid sample and the matching paper procedure/chart, then explains the scale/method distinction independently | Reusing case 1 colours or answers without a new test, or merely changing the code letters |
| Honest limit | Learner separates what was observed, inferred and supplied as reference; no exact pH is claimed from a coarse colour comparison, and unresolved results stay unresolved | Claiming exactly pH 7 from a neutral-looking colour, overwriting deviations, implying low hazard from pH alone |

No numerical grading threshold or additional task/repetition quota is introduced. The existing profile contract requires two distinct demonstrations with fresh variation and transfer. Assess the complete evidence in these two cases; repeat only to resolve an actual missing/uncertain aspect. Safety release and observed performance are factual prerequisites, not a score that can be compensated by a correct worksheet.

| Case | Teacher's actual material identity after code-key reveal | Reference orientation, never a learner raw observation |

| 2 | Fresh B aliquot | Alkaline; broadly around pH 8–9 if supported by this paper/chart and actual conditions |

Do **not** stipulate red/orange/green/blue as universal answers. The actual manufacturer's chart is the comparison standard for the specific product and lot; the learner's actual observed colour or ordered pattern is the raw datum. A coarse scale label is not a calibrated exact measurement or a formal statistical confidence interval. If actual matching supports a broader or ambiguous region, record that limit and investigate as needed; do not manufacture the expected pH.

A defensible example of reasoning (model text, not an experiment report): “I recorded my colour pattern before the answer key. It matched the region around 4 on this product's scale, supporting acidic conditions below 7. I cannot infer an exact value such as 4.00 from that match. The known reference behaved plausibly. A different product requires its own chart and application method.”

The same classes under two indicator products are chemically compatible even if their colours or patterns differ; matching appearance alone does not establish matching pH. Conversely, two different colours do not automatically contradict one another. The evidence is the product-specific comparison and the actual conditions. No advanced indicator structure theory, titration, exact neutral-point temperature treatment or hazardous preparation is added to this introductory goal.
