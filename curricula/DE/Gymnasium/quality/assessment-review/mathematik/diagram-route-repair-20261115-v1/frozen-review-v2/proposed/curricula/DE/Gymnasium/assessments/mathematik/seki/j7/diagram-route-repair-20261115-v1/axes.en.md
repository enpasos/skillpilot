# Read charts: a time series and a budget

DRAFT / needs_review · CC-BY-4.0 · SkillPilot

## Task

All data in this task are invented. A calculator is allowed. The straight segments define the displayed course between the marks.

**Case A – water:**

![Chart: time in hours, water volume in litres; container A has a solid line and B a dashed line.](/assets/assessment-materials/mathematik/f4fda40d-dca5-510b-bd6d-f7874b3d7b9b/water/f4fda40d-dca5-510b-bd6d-f7874b3d7b9b.png)

1. Name the quantity and unit on each axis. Explain what a horizontal step from 0 to 2 and a vertical step from 0 to 20 mean.
2. Read the water volume in each container at 3 h. Justify reading between marks and write a complete comparison with its unit.

**Case B – a budget to read independently:**

![Horizontal bar chart: categories Bus and Material; the amount axis is labelled in 100 EUR.](/assets/assessment-materials/mathematik/f4fda40d-dca5-510b-bd6d-f7874b3d7b9b/budget/f4fda40d-dca5-510b-bd6d-f7874b3d7b9b.png)

3. Which axis carries categories and which carries amounts of money? Explain what “100 EUR” means for a number read from that axis.
4. Read both expenses and find their difference in EUR. Use a scale position to justify how you obtained the amount.

## Evaluation material: release only after submission

A: The horizontal axis shows time in hours (h); 0 to 2 means 2 h. The vertical axis shows water volume in litres (L); 0 to 20 means 20 L. At 3 h, A contains 55 L and B 50 L. The time is halfway between 2 and 4, so the straight segment for A gives the midpoint of 50 and 60. A then contains 5 L more than B.

B: The vertical axis carries the categories Bus and Material; the horizontal axis carries amounts. Its numbers are multiples of 100 EUR. The bus costs 2.5 × 100 EUR = 250 EUR and the material 4 × 100 EUR = 400 EUR. The difference is 150 EUR. Equivalent complete statements and methods receive equal credit.

## Analytic rubric

Only the following subject-matter essentials are scored. Each criterion earns 0, 1 or 2 points: 2 means a minimally adequate, independently visible core performance; 1 means a recognizable but incomplete partial performance; 0 means no defensible performance on that criterion. Criteria are scored separately, without offsetting one against another. The passing threshold equals the total of all essential points because the existing runtime contract checks only the total score.

Full essential credit requires neither the reference wording nor a prescribed calculation method, chart type, polished sketch, formal interval notation or repeatedly written units. Subject-correct clear explanations, calculations, understandable ratio/change comparisons and suitable tables receive equal credit. Implicit information counts when unambiguous. An isolated transcription or arithmetic slip does not reduce essential credit when correct reading, reference base, method and contextual conclusion are unambiguous in the rest of the answer and the slip does not determine an essential decision. An unresolved contradiction of a criterion’s core claim prevents 2 points on that criterion; no hidden additional passing veto is introduced.

Supplementary calculations and details receive feedback but no compensation points. Partial credit remains useful feedback but does not by itself pass this assessment identity. These tasks introduce no new learner-result or repetition semantics.

- **axes-a-axis-meaning** (2): A: quantities and scales. 2: Meaningfully relates horizontal time in hours and vertical volume in litres and interprets the shown scale gaps as time/volume gaps. An unambiguous correct contextual statement can demonstrate the mapping. 1: Only one axis/scale meaning is made clear; the other remains unresolved. 0: Axes or quantities are fundamentally confused or not interpreted.

- **axes-a-scaled-reading** (2): A: justify an intermediate reading. 2: Reads A=55 L and B=50 L at 3 h in context and justifies the intermediate value using the straight segment or uniform division. The 5 L difference may clarify the comparison but is not an additional mandatory arithmetic point. 1: Values or context are partly correct, but the intermediate-value reasoning is absent or incomplete. 0: No defensible mapping between 3 h, chart position and volume.

- **axes-b-axis-distinction** (2): B: distinguish category and quantity axes. 2: Distinguishes the expense items Bus/Material as categories from the amount-of-money axis and maps them correctly to this horizontal bar chart. The term category is not prescribed; the distinction must be unambiguous. 1: Recognizes one axis role but does not sufficiently distinguish them or contradicts the category assignment. 0: No suitable distinction between expense items and readable money amounts.

- **axes-b-unit-factor** (2): B: explain and apply the unit multiplier. 2: Explains that the axis number counts multiples of 100 EUR and demonstrably applies that meaning to at least one actual bar. A suitable expression such as 2.5 times 100 EUR can itself provide the explanation. 1: Names the multiplier or a suitable amount without sufficiently connecting it to the read-off number. 0: Treats 2.5 or 4 directly as EUR or provides no defensible multiplier interpretation.

- **axes-b-context-reading** (2): B: read both money amounts in context. 2: Associates Bus and Material with the scaled amounts 250 EUR and 400 EUR and thereby gives a correct magnitude comparison. The additional subtraction yielding 150 EUR is not a separate passing requirement. 1: One amount is correctly associated or the comparison is recognizable, but the second scaled association remains unresolved. 0: No defensible association of the scaled amounts with the expense items.
