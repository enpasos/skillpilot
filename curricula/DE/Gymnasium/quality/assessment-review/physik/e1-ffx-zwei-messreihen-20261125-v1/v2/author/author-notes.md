### Separation of estimate quality and law
- Derivation quality is judged only under own g.
- The law criterion compares written numbers only, using the factor-1/2 and decimal-place rule.
- The v1 claim that existence of a stated estimate is the only cross-link is withdrawn. The solution now states the mandated link: a missing estimate costs 0.5 under own g plus 2.5 under law. Nothing else is deducted for it: (b) under own g is not assessed, uncertainty and variation then use single-run values, and the comparison judges the values actually compared.
- Adding runs cannot change the law criterion.

### Point capacity
All figures assume full marks elsewhere.
- One coefficient differing from the own estimate: 21.5, pass.
- Both coefficients differing: 19, fail.
- One coefficient differing plus four slips: 19.5, fail.
- Four slips: 22, pass.
- Single facets other than the law: loss ≤1, giving ≥23.
- Both laws absent: ≤17. Both graphical determinations absent: ≤19.
- Crossed omissions: A graph absent with B law absent ≤18; the mirror case is the same. A graph absent with B coefficient differing: 19.
- Investigation absent or maintained misconception: ≤12.
- No 20-point guarantee is claimed beyond this arithmetic.

**Finding for the concept owner.** A whole law missing in only one series still passes at 20.5, but only with every other point earned. A graphical determination missing in only one series passes at 21.5. This follows from the mandated ≤0.5 tiers.
