Sie liegt entsprechend einer eigenen Geschwindigkeitsskala in m/s, nicht entsprechend einer Ortsstrecke in m. In II liegt sie vor B, denn der Pfeil ist 2 cm statt des Körperabstands von 3 cm lang. Die Körperorte und die wirkliche Geschwindigkeit bleiben unverändert.

EN:

The arrow belongs to A. My new drawing starts there because the velocity is a property of A.

I read I as rightwards relative to the stationary ground. My arrow in II points that way too.

The ruler gives 3 cm: 3 cm · (2 m/s)/cm = 6 m/s. For II: (6 m/s)/((3 m/s)/cm) = 2 cm. The separate legends are in my actual drawing; its ruler defines drawn centimetres.

At first I thought A touched B at the tip. I explicitly retract that. The tip is neither a present or future position of A nor evidence of contact with B. Its position follows a separate velocity scale in m/s, not a position distance in m. In II it lies short of B because the arrow is 2 cm long whereas the object separation is 3 cm. Object positions and the actual velocity remain unchanged.
