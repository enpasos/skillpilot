code: MGT201098
title: Sustainable Management & Technology
title_en: Sustainable Management & Technology
ects: 5

content:
Sustainable supply chains; Sustainable behavior change; Defossilization; Political governance of bioeconomy and sustainability; Circular economy; Consumer options for reducing plastic packaging; Societal support for sustainable climate policy: What can we learn from behavioral economics?; Renewable raw materials - When is it worth using them as materials?; (Bio-)gas and electromobility: What benefits how much for the climate?; Sustainable and digital transformation.

outcomes:
Participants acquire the ability to support the transformation of their organizations into sustainable entities. They learn to assess which trends and technologies have the potential to prevail on the market in the future, are enabled to strengthen economic, social and ecological sustainability and to recognize new business areas and technologies in good time. In addition, methodological knowledge is imparted, e.g. in life cycle assessment, circular economy, bioeconomy and design of sustainable supply chains.

preconditions:
Participants should be able to demonstrate several years of professional experience (leadership experience desired), an interest in sustainability and building a network, and the courage to make changes.
