# NAT3035 Quantum Information - rawgraph breakdown

Node: Quantum Information (NAT3035)
Description: The learner can explain foundational concepts of quantum information and apply them to central applications.

  Node: Foundations and formalism
  Description: The learner can use the basic formalism of quantum information theory.

    Node: Pure vs mixed states
    Description: The learner can distinguish pure and mixed states and represent them mathematically.

    Node: Quantum evolution and completely positive maps
    Description: The learner can describe unitary evolution, channels, and completely positive maps.

    Node: Measurements and POVMs
    Description: The learner can model quantum measurements and interpret outcome statistics.

    Node: Schmidt decomposition and purification
    Description: The learner can compute Schmidt decompositions and use purification techniques.

    Node: Tomography, estimation, and hypothesis testing
    Description: The learner can explain basic methods of quantum state tomography and estimation.

    Node: Quantum mechanical computations
    Description: The learner can perform computations relevant to quantum information tasks.

  Node: Entanglement theory and nonlocality
  Description: The learner can analyze entanglement in pure and mixed states and its signatures.

    Node: Pure and mixed-state entanglement
    Description: The learner can classify entanglement in pure and mixed states.

    Node: Entanglement entropy and measures
    Description: The learner can compute entanglement entropy and related measures.

    Node: Entanglement conversion and distillation
    Description: The learner can describe distillation, conversion, and purification protocols.

    Node: Bell inequalities and nonlocality
    Description: The learner can explain Bell tests and their implications for nonlocality.

  Node: Quantum communication protocols
  Description: The learner can explain how entanglement enables communication protocols.

    Node: Dense coding
    Description: The learner can explain dense coding and its resource requirements.

    Node: Quantum teleportation
    Description: The learner can outline the teleportation protocol and required resources.

    Node: Quantum cryptography (QKD)
    Description: The learner can explain basic quantum key distribution protocols.

  Node: Quantum computation and algorithms
  Description: The learner can explain computational models and key quantum algorithms.

    Node: Quantum circuits and gate model
    Description: The learner can model computations using quantum circuits and gates.

    Node: Deutsch, Grover, and Shor algorithms
    Description: The learner can describe core steps and speedups of standard algorithms.

  Node: Quantum simulation and metrology
  Description: The learner can explain simulation and metrology as central applications.

    Node: Quantum simulation basics
    Description: The learner can explain how quantum systems simulate other quantum systems.

    Node: Quantum metrology and estimation
    Description: The learner can relate estimation precision to quantum resources.

  Node: Decoherence and error correction
  Description: The learner can analyze decoherence and countermeasures for fragile quantum information.

    Node: Decoherence mechanisms
    Description: The learner can explain sources and consequences of decoherence.

    Node: Lindblad master equation
    Description: The learner can model open-system dynamics using Lindblad equations.

    Node: Error correction codes and strategies
    Description: The learner can explain elementary error correction schemes.

  Node: Advanced and optional topics
  Description: The learner can relate quantum information to advanced topics in many-body physics.

    Node: Many-body entanglement
    Description: The learner can explain entanglement in many-body systems.

    Node: Topological quantum computation
    Description: The learner can describe topological approaches to robust quantum computation.

    Node: Quantum complexity
    Description: The learner can explain complexity classes and resource scaling in quantum computing.

    Node: Tensor networks
    Description: The learner can explain tensor-network representations for quantum states.
