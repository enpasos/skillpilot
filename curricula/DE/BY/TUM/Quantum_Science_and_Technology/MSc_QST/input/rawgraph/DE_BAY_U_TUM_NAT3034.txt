# NAT3034 Quantum Hardware - rawgraph breakdown

Node: Quantum Hardware (NAT3034)
Description: The learner can explain and apply core concepts of quantum hardware and analyze major implementation platforms.

  Node: Foundations and motivation
  Description: The learner can connect key quantum concepts to hardware platforms and applications.

    Node: Quantum 1.0 vs Quantum 2.0
    Description: The learner can distinguish historical and modern quantum technologies and relate them to hardware goals.

    Node: Two-level system and harmonic oscillator
    Description: The learner can model basic quantum systems used as qubits and resonators.

    Node: Superposition, entanglement, relaxation, and dephasing
    Description: The learner can explain coherence and decoherence mechanisms relevant for qubits.

    Node: Quantum vs classical information and applications
    Description: The learner can contrast quantum and classical information and name key application areas.

  Node: Light-matter interaction and QED
  Description: The learner can analyze light-matter interaction in cavity and circuit QED settings.

    Node: Quantization of the electromagnetic field and photon states
    Description: The learner can describe field quantization and properties of photon states.

    Node: Photon sources, detectors, and entangled photons
    Description: The learner can explain how photons are generated, detected, and entangled.

    Node: Two-level atoms, dipole moments, Jaynes-Cummings, and Rabi dynamics
    Description: The learner can model atom-field coupling and predict Rabi oscillations.

    Node: Cavity and circuit QED, cooperativity, and AC Stark effect
    Description: The learner can explain strong coupling criteria and cavity-induced level shifts.

  Node: Superconducting quantum circuits
  Description: The learner can analyze superconducting hardware platforms for qubits.

    Node: Superconducting resonators and quality factors
    Description: The learner can relate resonator geometry to frequency and quality factor.

    Node: Josephson junction qubits and anharmonicity
    Description: The learner can explain how Josephson junctions create nonlinear qubits.

    Node: Qubit Hamiltonian engineering
    Description: The learner can derive effective Hamiltonians for superconducting qubits.

    Node: Single- and two-qubit gates with control and readout
    Description: The learner can outline gate operations and measurement schemes for superconducting qubits.

    Node: Decoherence mechanisms in superconducting circuits
    Description: The learner can identify dominant decoherence channels and mitigation strategies.

  Node: Semiconductor quantum circuits
  Description: The learner can analyze semiconductor platforms for qubits and resonators.

    Node: Semiconductor qubits (quantum dots, donors, defects)
    Description: The learner can explain how semiconductor qubits are realized and controlled.

    Node: Resonators and coupling strength in semiconductors
    Description: The learner can relate device parameters to coupling strength and control fidelity.

    Node: Gate operations, control, and readout
    Description: The learner can outline gate and measurement schemes for semiconductor qubits.

    Node: Decoherence and dephasing in semiconductors
    Description: The learner can describe noise sources and dephasing mechanisms in semiconductor devices.

  Node: Atoms and quantum gases
  Description: The learner can explain how ultracold atoms and quantum gases are used as hardware platforms.

    Node: Ultracold gas preparation
    Description: The learner can describe laser cooling, trapping, and evaporative cooling techniques.

    Node: Interactions and optical lattices
    Description: The learner can explain interactions in optical lattices and their control.

    Node: Bose-Hubbard and Hubbard models
    Description: The learner can apply lattice models to describe cold-atom systems.

  Node: Quantum sensing
  Description: The learner can explain hardware concepts for quantum sensing and their limits.

    Node: Noise sources and sensitivity limits (SQL)
    Description: The learner can analyze noise sources and relate them to the standard quantum limit.

    Node: Optomechanics and position measurements
    Description: The learner can describe optomechanical sensing and position readout.

    Node: Quantum backaction and shot noise
    Description: The learner can explain measurement backaction and shot-noise limits.

    Node: NV centers and spin-qubit sensors
    Description: The learner can describe sensing with defect centers in diamond and semiconductors.

    Node: Beyond SQL with squeezing and QND
    Description: The learner can explain how squeezing and quantum non-demolition techniques surpass the SQL.
