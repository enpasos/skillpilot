# NAT7001 Quantum Optics - rawgraph breakdown

Node: Quantum Optics (NAT7001)
Description: The learner can explain and apply core concepts of quantum optics and solve representative problems from the module scope.

  Node: Optical foundations
  Description: The learner can connect classical optics to quantum optics through key wave concepts.

    Node: Ray to wave optics transition
    Description: The learner can explain when ray optics is insufficient and derive wave-optics consequences for simple setups.

    Node: Gaussian beams
    Description: The learner can calculate beam waist, Rayleigh range, divergence, and intensity profiles for Gaussian beams.

  Node: Quantized electromagnetic field
  Description: The learner can model light as quantized field modes and compute basic state properties.

    Node: Field quantization and mode operators
    Description: The learner can set up creation/annihilation operators for a single mode and compute photon number expectations.

    Node: Fock states
    Description: The learner can compute photon number statistics and measurement outcomes for number states.

    Node: Coherent states
    Description: The learner can derive Poisson photon statistics and quadrature expectations for coherent states.

    Node: Squeezed states
    Description: The learner can explain quadrature squeezing and compute variance reductions with a squeezing parameter.

    Node: Thermal states
    Description: The learner can describe thermal photon statistics and compute mean photon numbers for a mode.

  Node: Light-matter interaction in two-level systems
  Description: The learner can analyze quantized light-atom interaction in basic two-level models.

    Node: Two-level system dynamics
    Description: The learner can solve for Rabi oscillations and interpret population dynamics of a driven two-level system.

    Node: Jaynes-Cummings model
    Description: The learner can derive dressed states, Rabi splitting, and predict vacuum Rabi oscillations.

  Node: Quantum vacuum effects
  Description: The learner can explain observable effects attributed to vacuum fluctuations.

    Node: Spontaneous emission
    Description: The learner can explain spontaneous emission as a vacuum-induced process and estimate decay rates.

    Node: Purcell effect
    Description: The learner can quantify cavity-enhanced emission and relate the Purcell factor to mode volume and Q.

    Node: Casimir force
    Description: The learner can explain the origin of the Casimir force and describe its dependence on geometry.

    Node: Lamb shift
    Description: The learner can explain the Lamb shift as a vacuum-related level shift and outline its physical origin.

  Node: Coherence and correlations
  Description: The learner can analyze coherence properties of light and interpret correlation functions.

    Node: Coherence functions
    Description: The learner can compute and interpret first- and second-order coherence functions g1 and g2.

    Node: Photon statistics and correlations
    Description: The learner can distinguish Poissonian, bunched, and antibunched light via correlation data.

  Node: Experimental quantum optics
  Description: The learner can relate theoretical models to experimental realizations and measurements.

    Node: Experimental settings for quantized light-atom interaction
    Description: The learner can describe setups used for vacuum Rabi oscillations and strong coupling tests.

    Node: Non-destructive photon measurements
    Description: The learner can explain principles of quantum non-demolition measurements of photons.

  Node: Entanglement and quantum information
  Description: The learner can connect quantum-optical resources to information tasks.

    Node: Entangled photon pair generation
    Description: The learner can explain typical generation methods (e.g., SPDC) and expected correlations.

    Node: Quantum teleportation
    Description: The learner can outline the teleportation protocol and identify required entanglement resources.

    Node: Quantum cryptography
    Description: The learner can explain the basic idea of QKD (e.g., BB84) and the role of single photons.
