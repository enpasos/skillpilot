title: Aktuelle Themen zu Quantennetzwerken
title_en: Current Topics in Quantum Networks
ects: 4
ects_plan: 5
semester_plan: -

content:

In this seminar, we will discuss recent publications in the field of quantum science and technology, with a focus on networked quantum systems. To this end, each student will read one paper per week, which will then be discussed in the seminar. In addition, each student will prepare a presentation about a research highlight. The learning outcome will be evaluated based on this presentation, with a focus on the clarity and quality of the presentation, as well as the response to arising questions.In the preparation of the talk, the students will be supported by members of the Quantum Networks Group.
The content of the seminar will cover the following topics:- Progress in different quantum technological hardware platforms, including trapped atoms and ions, superconducting circuits, color centers, and quantum dots.- Recent advances and novel protocols in the fields of distributed quantum computation and quantum communication- Key experimental concepts in quantum hardware, e.g. resonators, waveguides, quantum bits, quantum processors, quantum error correction techniques- State-of-the-art nanofabrication for quantum networking- Challenges for the up-scaling of quantum hardware- Theoretical concepts for distributed quantum information processing: Entanglement, quantum gates,
quantum algorithms, quantum memories etc.

outcomes:

- After the successful completion of the module the students are able- To prepare scientific presentations on a scientific topic and to clearly present topical research results in the field of quantum networks.- To discuss the state-of-the-art in this research field.- To analyze and assess the latest developments in quantum science and technology.- To describe the foundations and the state of the art in distributed quantum computing and quantum communication

preconditions:

There are no specific preconditions that go beyond those of the M.Sc. study program.
