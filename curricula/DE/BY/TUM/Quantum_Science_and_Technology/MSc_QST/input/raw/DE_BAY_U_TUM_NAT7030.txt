title_en: Experimental Quantum Computing and Quantum Error Correction
ects: 9

semester_plan: -

content:

This module gives an introduction to quantum
computing and quantum error correction from an experimental perspective. It
covers the underlying concepts of quantum computing (qubits, gates, quantum
circuits) and introduces various quantum computing platforms that have pushed
towards first experiments in quantum error correction (superconducting qubits,
trapped ions, neutral atoms). The course then discusses basics of quantum errors
and quantum error correction and exemplifies the concepts using concrete code
implementations (repetition code, surface code, color code, qLDPC codes). The
concept of fault-tolerance will be discussed, as well as universal logical gate
sets and how to realize them. The goal of the course is to provide a good
fundament to understand both the experimental challenges as well as the
theoretical background of quantum error correction and to provide guidance to
understanding the rapid experimental progress in this fast-paced field.

outcomes:

- After completing the Module the student is able to:
- 1. Describe and explain quantum circuits in terms of elementary quantum operations.
- 2. Explain the salient features of various quantum computing hardware platforms such as superconducting qubits, trapped ions and neutral atoms.
- 3. Understand the formalism to describe quantum errors through quantum channels.
- 4. Understand and describe various quantum error correction codes used in experimental platforms to date.
- 5. Analyze and evaluate recent developments in experimental quantum correction and their impact.
