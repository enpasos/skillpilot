content:

The formalism of quantum mechanics represents a paradigm shift for elementary operations, like applying unitary quantum gates, compared to classical computers. In the seminar "Advanced Topics of Quantum Computing", students investigate current state-of-the-art quantum algorithms, their underlying mathematical description and theoretical analysis, and associated methods to (numerically) simulate the functionality of a quantum computer. Potential topics include:
- Quantum hardware and physical realizations of quantum computers
- Quantum circuits
- Quantum simulation (using a quantum computer to simulate a target quantum system)
- Tensor network methods
- Qubitization and the quantum eigenvalue transformation
- Variational quantum algorithms and quantum machine learning
- Quantum optimization
- Quantum error correction
- Quantum cellular automata
- Quantum cryptography

outcomes:

- After completing the seminar, students recognize the general goals and challenges of quantum computing. They can understand a subtopic in detail and can implement an associated algorithm or explain its underlying principles.

preconditions:

- Fundamental knowledge of quantum mechanics and quantum computing
- Programming skills helpful (but not required for all topics)
