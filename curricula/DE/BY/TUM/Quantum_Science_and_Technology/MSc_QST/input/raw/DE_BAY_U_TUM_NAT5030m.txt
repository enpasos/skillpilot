title_en: Cavity-, Circuit- and Waveguide QED
ects: 5
ects_plan: 5
semester_plan: -

content:

- This seminar covers current research topics in the field of light-matter interactions in cavities, waveguides and superconducting quantum
- The focus of this seminar will be placed on new theoretical concepts, including quantum information processing and quantum simulation schemes, as well as their experimental implementations.

outcomes:

- After the successful completion of the module the students are able
- To critically read and understand current scientific literature
- To understand current scientific trends in the field of cavity-, waveguide- and circuit QED
- To understand basic theoretical concepts in the description of light-matter interactions at the quantum level
- To present scientific results to non-experts in a clear manner

preconditions:

required: Quantum Mechanics
beneficial, but not strictly necessary: QST Experiment: Quantum Hardware, QST Theory: Quantum Information, Quantum Optics
