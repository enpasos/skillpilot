Effective Quantum Field Theory provides the modern view on the foundations of quantum field theory, the concept
of renormalization, and why quantum field theories are so successful in describing the laws of Nature. This courses
starts from the notion of scale separation and effective descriptions of physical systems, not necessarily quantum or field, and develops the formalism and quantum-field theoretical tools on toy examples as well as in various contexts relevant to the interactions of the Standard Model of particle physics. The following topics will be covered:
- Principles:
- Scales, degrees of freedom, power counting, non-renormalizable QFTs, the bottom-up and top-down view
- Tree-level matching, higher-dimension operators, EFT and symmetries
- Loops:
- Anomalous dimensions, renormalization group, matching, method of regions, functional matching
- Specific EFTs
- Decoupling of a heavy quark, Goldstone bosons, Fermi theory with QCD corrections, Standard Model Effective Theory, Heavy Particle EFT (HQET), Non-relativistic and Potential Non-relativistic EFT, Soft-collinear effective theory, Unstable Particles, Condensed matter and Open Quantum Systems

outcomes:

- analyze the scales and degrees of freedom of a physical system and construct effective field theories bottom-up and top-down,
- compute matching coefficients and renormalize them,
- solve renormalization group equations
- compute observables in principle at any order in the expansion parameter(s).
