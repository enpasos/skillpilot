title_en: Concentration of Measure
ects: 5

content:
inequalities of Chernoff, Hoeffding, Bernstein, isoperimetric inequality, Johnsson-Lindenstrauss inequality, martingale method and Azuma's inequality, Poincare inequality, Gromov-Milman theorem

outcomes:
- After successful completion of the module students are able to understand concentration of measure inequalities and techniques, and apply them to examples in the context of applications studied in the course.

MA0003 Analysis 3 (Measure and Integration Theory);
MA0009 Introduction to Probability and Statistics (MA1401 Introduction to Probability Theory);
