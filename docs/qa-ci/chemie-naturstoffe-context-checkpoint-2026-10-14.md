Der [abschließende Messbeleg](https://github.com/enpasos/skillpilot/blob/main/curricula/DE/Gymnasium/quality/goal-evidence/2026-10-14/chemie-NAT9-current419-isolated-integration-technical-20261014-v1/FINAL.actual305-of419-nine-new-seven-existing-context-rebindings.inactive-checkpoint.manifest.json)
