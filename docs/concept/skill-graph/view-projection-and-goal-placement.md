## 3. View Projection and Goal Placement

A careful reader must be able to reconstruct every visible learner-facing tree and learner map directly from documented JSON fields without inspecting runtime code or relying on title/`phase` heuristics.

<a id="sec-sga-vp-families"></a>
### 3.1. View Families, Learner Map, and Five Projection Rules

| View Family | Primary Question Answered | Exclusive Structural Source of Truth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Content tree** | What belongs under what in subject-matter terms? | `goals` + explicit `contains` edges (ordered by `contains` array). |
| **Program tree** | Where in the curriculum is a goal introduced, revisited, or assessed? | Shared plan units or `programUnits` (`parentUnitId`, `order`, `after`) + placements (`primary` attaches; `secondary`/`assessed` render as badges/overlays, missing yields `unplacedInScope`; `own` records learning-unit ownership under contract 1.2 and attaches nothing). |
| **Competency view** | Along which capability or process axis is a goal classified? | `competencyCatalog` + `competencyRefs` (orthogonal taxonomy projection). |
| **Goal tree (composition tree)** | How does one resolved scope see the subject including subject clusters as one default tree? | Shared Land plan or derived reviewed `*.view.json` (`structure` + `canonicalSubtree` / `goalEntry`) + full canonical `contains` expansion. |
| **Learner map** | How do learners navigate ordered plan units, subject learning units, and atomic goals? | Contracts all non-unit clusters along `contains` within the resolved goal tree: indirect child units and atoms move up, subjects act as filters rather than parent units, and arrows between direct children of an opened unit aggregate sibling `after` and atomic `requires` without hiding backflows. |

Five binding rules govern all projections:

1. **Rule 1 (No heuristic inference):** Never infer parent-child edges from titles, localized labels, `phase`, `dimensionTags.phase`, or string similarity.
2. **Rule 2 (Explicit edge provenance):** Every visible parent–child edge in the tree or map has a documented structural derivation path (`contains` including deterministic non-unit cluster contraction for the learner map, `parentUnitId`, `goalPlacements`, `competencyRefs`, or `children` of a shared Land plan or composition view). Separately, map arrows aggregate documented `after` and atomic `requires` paths while retaining traceable causes.
3. **Rule 3 (Single relation family per view):** Enrich views with badges or subject filters, never by silently mixing parent hierarchies.
4. **Rule 4 (Reviewed Land plan / composition view wins):** When a scope-specific shared Land plan or derived `.view.json` exists, that projection defines the default learner-facing goal tree and learner map while `programUnits`/`goalPlacements` remain valid for program views and diagnostics (`ALL` is a query-only sentinel, never serialized in placements or applicability).
5. **Rule 5 (Single-occurrence tree per resolved scope):** Within one resolved scope (such as `DE-HE / Gymnasium / G9 / SekII / Mathematik / LK`), each visible goal appears **at most once** under **at most one** visible parent; pairwise overlapping `canonicalSubtree` expansions are compile-time errors.

### 3.2. Prerequisite Closure, Carried Source vs. Learning Placement, and Canonical Identity

Under the Product Owner decisions of **9–15 November 2026** ([Graph Definition §§5.1–5.2](graph-definition.md)):

- **Unpruned prerequisite closure (9 Nov 2026) & learner-read resilience (11 Nov 2026):** Selecting atomic goals must preserve their complete transitive prerequisite demand on the unpruned canonical graph (`A requires B requires C` retains `B` even if `A` and `C` alone matched initial scope filters). Unknown IDs, cycles, or unaccounted required atoms remain hard errors in validation, curriculum QA, and native acceptance; on learner reads, when a learning context's canonical graph is not strictly compilable, the runtime keeps the reviewed seed scope without the derived closure, claims no learning basis, offers no goal whose demand is broken, and logs the failure once per curriculum and error code (errors in the seed selection itself remain hard errors).
- **Personal learning-scope basis $L$ (10 Nov 2026):** Necessary prerequisites belong to learning, progress, and completion, not merely hidden lookup support. Given valid reviewed atomic seed targets $S$, compile the fixed global target basis $L = S \cup \{p \in A \mid \exists t \in S : (t, p) \in R_{\mathrm{at}}^+\}$ on the unpruned canonical graph. $L$ depends solely on personal configuration and curriculum/projection revision, **never** on current mastery. Mastered atoms remain in $L$ as already satisfied; open prerequisites receive navigable acquisition targets in the full Personal Curriculum with explainable `required by` provenance (without inventing new source mappings, enrolling unrelated siblings, or mutating mastery). Level 3 focus equals the selected roots' descendant union intersected with $L$ (normal mode masks checks to focus *after* effective transitive reachability on canonical `requires`, without erasing paths through outside-focus intermediates).
- **Carried in vs. placed in & earliest actual need (15 Nov 2026):** *Carried in* denotes a goal's evidenced official source assignment (`officialSource`); *placed in* denotes its didactic learning placement inside a plan unit. A canonical phase marker (`dimensionTags.phase`) justifies at most an explicitly labeled provisional `canonicalDefault` assignment and never an official Land source claim. When a goal receives justified earlier preparation (`reviewedPreparation`) or reviewed placement (`reviewedPlacement`), its original source assignment remains intact; source checks audit *carried in*, whereas route and backflow checks audit *placed in*. Necessary prerequisites in $L$ outside the chosen Land plan's carried targets receive their learning placement deterministically at **earliest actual need** (in the earliest plan unit of any dependent goal in $L$), while carried late targets are never moved forward silently.
- **Subject-local `projectionRole` specificity:** Authored view references carry `projectionRole: "target" | "prerequisiteOnly"` (default `"target"`). Specificity resolves overlaps via (1) direct `goalEntry` over `canonicalSubtree`, (2) deeper/nearest `canonicalSubtree` over broader ancestor subtrees, and (3) `"target"` on equal specificity. The position of a `prerequisiteOnly` reference asserts no stage assignment of its own; if genuinely needed, the goal receives exactly one acquisition placement in the closed Personal Curriculum $L$.
- **Preserved canonical atomicity:** Collapsing or referencing a canonical cluster never creates an opaque substitute atom and never transfers cluster results to children (see also the historical `DPK-006c-c` boundary in [Dual Package Implementation Status](../../dev/dual-curriculum-package-implementation-status.md) and tooling split in [Curriculum Authoring Editors](../../dev/curriculum-authoring-editors.md)).

### 3.3. Plan Units and Subject Units in Projected Views

Per Bundesland and duration model (`G8` / `G9`), **one shared Land plan** across the selected subjects is the sole placement authority; course profiles (`GK`, `LK`) select contributions from that plan rather than assigning contradictory stages to the same goal, and single-stage views (`SekI` or `SekII` alone) are derived excerpts. The full root of a Land plan carries exclusively the two root plan units `SekI` and `SekII` with `SekII after SekI`; beneath `SekI` lie the year units applicable to the Land and duration model (with explicitly evidenced first and last years, never a universal `J5` start), and beneath `SekII` lie the phase units `E` and `Q1`–`Q4` ordered by sibling `after`. Orientation goals, standalone practice tasks, and Abitur exams reside inside the stages (never beside `SekI` and `SekII`). The nationwide canonical overview is a subject overview, not a Land plan.

Four invariants govern how plan units and canonical subject [Learning Units](learning-units.md) interact in a resolved course:

1. **Single direct course owner & cross-Land leaf wholeness:** Every planned atomic goal has exactly one directly owning unit (`Own(U)`) in the resolved course; ancestors aggregate child obligations without duplicating ownership. Canonical leaf subject units are cut so that they sit whole (unsplit) inside a single plan unit across all 16 Länder; larger parent subject units whose child units span multiple years or phases in a Land plan appear as a single whole only in the nationwide subject overview.
2. **Cross-subject identity:** Shared canonical goals (for example `target` in Mathematics and `prerequisiteOnly` in Physics) count once in the shared Land plan at their justified or earliest-needed learning placement while retaining subject-local roles.
3. **Unified ordering rules E1–E4 without access locks:** Plan units and subject learning units satisfy the same four ordering rules **E1–E4** ([Unit Progression](learning-unit-progression.md)) on the closed scope $L$: canonical subtree or projected plan container (E1), interval convexity without interleaved outside goals (E2), sibling-local completeness of `after` against crossing atomic demand with zero backflows against unit order (E3), and irredundancy of `after` (E4). `after` orders the learner map and "Unit by Unit" mode without locking direct goal or assessment attempts.
4. **Local recognition only inside units with assessments:** Implicit recognition applies exclusively inside declared units with assessments (with own assessments and valid complete `coverageReviews`) satisfying R1–R5; plan units (`SekI`, `SekII`, years, `E`/`Q1`–`Q4`) and assessment-free subject units order without recognizing anything implicitly, and parent exams neither reopen completed child units nor pass protected child exams or required cards.
