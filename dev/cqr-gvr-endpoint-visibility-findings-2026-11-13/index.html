    CQR-104: current endpoint visibility findings

    Candidate evidence for the F5 work in the hard-gate plan, based on main 2ad03c810c896d0bab96cd69d1cd96f45973d8e2. This is not main or production acceptance. The existing eight required visibility scopes remain required; the four upper-secondary gaps are not promoted by this report.

    Reproduce the measurement

    npm --prefix app run report:route-endpoint-visibility -- \
  --output ../docs/qa-ci/status/route-endpoint-visibility-audit.json

    The command exits 1 while any measured obligation fails. It invokes the actual CQR-104 evaluator, first with the missing SekII configuration and then with the existing compiled-jurisdiction mode. It does not change the registered profiles, pins, applicability, assessment contracts or reviews. The JSON report contains every finding and 381 raw input hashes: the measured canonical documents, all view files read to determine membership, and the shared selector/evaluator inputs. Its applicability-report hash binds derived compiler output, not new source evidence or a complete provenance receipt.

    Implemented corrections

    The evaluator now uses each authored view's course and duration filters for both the actual projection and the independently expected endpoints, in every subject. Previously this was complete only for Economics. For example, an LK-only ENG/ECG assessment cannot be required in the national Biology GK view. Country applicability alone does not establish course or duration applicability.

    The national Biology view already referenced eight assessment collections, but used shallow goalEntry nodes. Those references displayed the folders without their tasks. They now use canonicalSubtree, retaining the same identities, labels, roles and canonical contents. Of their 26 existing assessments, 21 belong in the GK projection; five LK-only tasks remain filtered out. No task, prerequisite, source claim, assessment coverage or review was authored by this projection correction.

    testBiologyAssessmentProjection.ts, invoked by test:graph-gate-contracts, reproduces the old shallow-folder failure, checks the exact restored GK task set independently from the view, retains all previous canonical atomic targets, and checks the LK exclusion. It also runs the actual evaluator over all eight pinned scopes and verifies that the remaining Biology gaps still fail. This is a partial repair, not twelve-scope acceptance.

    Remaining findings after the correction

    Counts below are view/jurisdiction projections, not additional QA scopes. Each subject is still one unpinned upper-secondary QA scope.

    Scope Relevant views Compiled projections Projections missing expected endpoints Other findings
    Mathematics Sek II 80 140 140 State/course/stage placement and assessment obligations need reconciliation.
    Physics Sek II 68 128 64 60 projections also expose unexpected terminals under this prospective configuration.
    Chemistry Sek II 41 101 101 Two configured terminals have no compiled jurisdiction applicability.
    Biology Sek II 3 18 2 Five national-view jurisdictions have no applicable configured terminal.

    The simpler, non-jurisdiction-expanded configuration still fails in 80/80 Mathematics, 64/68 Physics, 41/41 Chemistry and 2/3 Biology views. It is not a substitute for the scoped applicability check.

    Biology

    The current evaluator expects the Insulin/Plasmid assessment (1d0c84df-8040-5cab-8a7c-359366937e44) and colour-vision assessment (7c7f147c-2b64-5b0c-90c1-ea9a9bf4b03a) in both Sachsen-Anhalt Sek-II views from their existing applicability metadata. Both views omit them. A separate author analysis and independent task-contract review on 13 November examined the actual tasks, solutions, rubrics, atomic goal descriptions and official curriculum. They found a concrete ST eAN/LK subject basis, together with contract gaps that prevent accepting an unchanged placement repair.

    The official Sachsen-Anhalt Biology curriculum, 1 August 2022 explicitly includes photoreceptors and colour/contrast perception at eAN (printed/PDF pp. 57–59), and genetically modified organisms, restriction enzymes, vectors and gel electrophoresis at eAN (pp. 60–61). The gAN pages 46–49 support broader perception, protein-biosynthesis and material-based genetic-test work. They do not establish the same specific prior knowledge for both unchanged tasks. A sufficiently supported transfer task could suit gAN, but that requires its own task/material and course decision. Global GK/LK tags inherited from Hessen do not decide ST applicability and must not be narrowed globally merely to repair ST.

    Existing assessment Actual task and rubric Contract mismatch
    Insulin Explain cDNA suitability for a bacterial host; match supplied fragment lengths to samples; explain DNA → mRNA → protein; distinguish DNA presence from functional expression. 22 direct prerequisites include drift, Hardy–Weinberg, SNP analysis, epigenetics and other unneeded contracts. The two coveredGoalIds clusters expand to 36 ordinary atoms, far beyond the four assessed tasks.
    Colour vision Use a supplied cone model to interpret a possible effect of M-cone dysfunction and explain redundant signal design using contrast, position and other information. 16 direct prerequisites include hearing, pain, Hebbian learning, ENG/ECG and other unneeded contracts. The two coverage clusters expand to 28 ordinary atoms; organ anatomy, complete visual pathways and detailed neural transduction are not tested.

    For Insulin, the existing atom 374e6de5-0747-57cb-99e3-e50ccb371124 (compatible conditions for recombinant protein expression) is a close contract candidate, with protein biosynthesis already among its transitive prerequisites. The cDNA/intron knowledge required by task 1 is not supplied in the material; the existing alternative-splicing goal also demands protein diversity and evolutionary interpretation and is too broad a substitute. Restriction and gel goals are possible preparation candidates, not automatically assessed coverage: expected fragment lengths are supplied, with no independent recognition-sequence or size-marker task. Their complete atomic promises therefore cannot be copied into the coverage list. The expression atom's current raw applicability is only DE-HE, with an explicit mapping-inheritance boundary; an ST mapping needs its own evidence.

    For colour vision, 9499943f-89b7-54e3-9fe2-e90404beaa4a (structure and function of selected sense organs) is a possible preparation candidate, but its full promise is not tested. No exact existing atom for the narrow qualitative cone/contrast performance was established. The listed sensitivity maxima do not determine response curves or unique responses to the two wavelengths. The present task supports a qualitative possible loss of information, not a quantitative discrimination claim. A revised task must state the intended inference and provide the necessary model/data; extra prerequisite edges cannot supply missing task evidence.

    Both tasks use a total of 30 points and a passing threshold of 18. Insulin can pass with tasks 1–3 scoring 22 points and task 4 scoring zero; colour vision can pass with tasks 1, 3 and 4 scoring 22 and the dysfunction analysis in task 2 scoring zero. Thus passing alone does not demonstrate every claimed atomic competence. Preparation, assessed coverage and globally recognized mastery remain separate; neither requires nor visible placement writes mastery. These counterexamples do not claim that existing learners have already received an incorrect result.

    The next subject-authoring step is a reviewed narrow Insulin eAN contract that closes the cDNA preparation/material gap, followed by a precise colour- vision target and matching task/rubric. The review must bind actual atomic coverage, course-specific applicability, necessary preparation and the meaning of a passing result. Both released identities also carry a separate assessmentApplicabilityBasis referencing the old clusters; changing only coveredGoalIds would leave that basis untouched. Before changing an existing identity, check result/history and selection semantics explicitly and decide whether a reviewed correction/migration or a distinct successor identity is needed. No effect on existing learner records is asserted without that check. Do not rewrite the frozen V9 evidence or silently reuse it for a changed goal.

    Consequently this is F5's documented evidence-gap outcome, not an approved replacement prerequisite list, new review pass or permission to place both tasks in both ST courses. New assessment, coverage, applicability and result contracts remain subject-authoring follow-up work. The availability of the official PDF proves a source basis, not a finished atomic task contract.

    The inspected inputs are reproducible without the exploratory drafts:

    • Canonical Biology, the two goal IDs above, including examData, requires, assessmentApplicabilityBasis and the referenced ordinary goals. Raw SHA-256: 0de1cfc92fd15d6633575f6f1da9aa78027feec4383e45f50ca738744977fe5e.
    • Retained official PDF at curricula/DE/Gymnasium/input/ST/FLP_Biologie_Gym_01082022_swd.pdf, pp. 46–49 and 57–61, with official provenance recorded in the source registry. Raw SHA-256: 58b106c65a71478a096f31cf8e9743a238a59aec085414ac472ee0ab5da10c38.
    • The preparation/coverage distinction and released-assessment identity boundary in AGENTS.md, sections 2 and 6.3. This audit changes none of those contracts or any curriculum/view bytes.

    In the national GK view, BW, NI, HB, HH and SL still have no applicable configured terminal. Exposing another folder cannot create the missing applicability/assessment evidence. Review existing suitable assessments and their actual coverage first; if none applies, author and independently review an appropriate assessment. Do not broaden an applicability override just to remove these findings.

    Chemistry

    The two endpoints without compiled applicability are eed5eda3-2daf-5d48-b935-23dadd622d9b (chemical knowledge development, with course assignment explicitly unresolved) and d7abdeb3-5b69-5744-b7fd-8149fd92b0d5 (Grignard alcohol synthesis). Source, course and assessment evidence must settle these cases before pinning the whole scope. The report separately lists missing placements in all 101 compiled projections; hiding these two endpoints would not resolve them.

    Physics and Mathematics

    The report names every missing/unexpected endpoint by view, jurisdiction and goal ID. Physics already has deliberate stage-specific and prerequisite-only assessment placements. These cannot be converted wholesale into target endpoints. Mathematics likewise needs its state/course-specific assessment collections checked against authoritative target scope and actual tasks. Restoring Biology's already authored national collections does not authorize adding every canonical examination to every state view.

    Decision after the Biology package

    Matching course/duration filters is a shared evaluator correction. The remaining curriculum gaps have different causes and do not support one bulk repair across all four subjects. Keep compositionVisibilityRequired unchanged until a complete subject scope passes with independent applicability and assessment evidence. CQR-104 therefore remains PARTIAL at eight of twelve scopes. Even twelve passing scopes would not by itself prove the additional semantic route obligation of GD-C22 or the broader assessment/runtime obligations of GD-A18.
