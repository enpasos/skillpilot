    Assessment-Gated Completion and Global Mastery

    Binding product decision, 4 November 2026, with the accepted recursive learning-unit structure; concept, not deployed behaviour. An ordinary goal can be completed through direct understanding or independent application in the relevant passed assessments of its authoritative acquisition unit. Both establish global mastery for future prerequisites and total progress.

    This replaces the 31 October preparation-discharge interpretation. In \(Q\to B\to A\), ordinary mastery of B alone does not release A while the local Q remains open. Passing the relevant local obligations can establish mastery of A and B by application. Full transitivity and transitive minimality remain required. This document claims neither completed implementation/migration nor verified coverage of existing assessments.

    1. One stored result, equivalent global credit

    The sole persisted result per atomic goal remains binary:

    \[ m(a)\in\{0,1\},\qquad M=\{a\mid m(a)=1\}. \]

    The derived set \(M_{\mathrm{eff}}\) combines explicit results and valid recognition. There is no second stored outcome, partial tier, waiver grant, per-part result registry or additional examination pass.

    An ordinary direct result demonstrates understanding; implicit mastery demonstrates independent application. Both satisfy future prerequisites and count toward total progress wherever the same canonical goal is a target. A changed Personal Curriculum, wider focus or later open assessment cannot withdraw valid recognition. Correction of an invalid source result or contract is a separate explicit integrity process.

    Assessment and memorization goals are never implicitly mastered. Exams need their normal positive outcome; memory goals need M1 verified recall. A due card is not a failed recall, and self-rated practice is not verified recall. Orientation retains its motivational-marker meaning; its completion does not certify subject competence or fabricate an interaction.

    2. Authoritative acquisition ownership in recursive units

    Resolve a finite, versioned unit U from existing program/placement or reviewed composition structure. Let \(Own(U)\) be its directly assigned actual atomic targets and \(Children(U)\) its required child units after authored choices. Its recursive target scope is

    \[ T(U)=Own(U)\cup\bigcup_{V\in Children(U)}T(V). \]

    Each atom has one direct acquisition home within a resolved learning path. A parent aggregates child targets without claiming their acquisition again. Assign recognition to the smallest authoritative unit that owns that outcome. A focus, display filter, dated plan or arbitrary scope visit is not such a unit or a new contract. Synthetic tree markers are not atomic acquisition goals. A valid unit has a nonempty resolved T(U); an empty or unresolved structure is not a completed unit. Pure aggregates contain actual required children.

    Own targets include actual subject, orientation, exam and memory atoms. Hidden prerequisite-only support is validated without becoming a target or increasing the denominator. Resolve applicable choices, direct entries and projection roles before computing ownership.

    Let Q denote assessment atoms and K memorization atoms; K here does not denote the cluster set in the graph specification. A unit's own protected duties are

    \[ P_U=Own(U)\cap(Q\cup K). \]

    Every ordinary subject target in Own(U) needs an own local assessment consumer in the full normalized relation:

    \[ \forall a\in Own(U):\quad a\text{ is an ordinary subject target}\Rightarrow \exists q\in Own(U)\cap Q:\ (q,a)\in R^+. \]

    This structural condition is necessary, not task-content proof. A memory endpoint alone does not establish application. An uncovered ordinary terminal is a missing contract, never zero-work mastery.

    Pure aggregates may have no own assessment. An integrative parent may own a synthesis assessment solely of already acquired child competencies, without a new ordinary target. Neither case violates the own-target coverage rule. A parent assessment cannot substitute for child exams/cards or create a new recognition contract over all descendants.

    Required recall belongs with its actual first acquisition. Do not place a broad existing memory goal at the parent merely to declare its children complete without their required cards. Keep a fitting larger boundary or review a genuine goal/card-selection split with stable meanings and explicit evidence migration. A support reference does not satisfy a missing recall obligation.

    3. Full transitive requirements independent of mastery

    Normalize direct/inherited requirements and expand required clusters into atomic obligations under the authoritative graph/composition contract. R points from consumer to prerequisite. Missing references, identity conflicts and containment, requirement or expanded atomic cycles make the calculation unavailable, not an empty completed unit.

    Full transitive reach is independent of M:

    \[ Reach_R^*(S)=S\cup\{v\mid\exists u\in S:(u,v)\in R^+\}. \]

    Never stop at a mastered intermediate. Traverse outside support as needed for structural checks, without granting its mastery through the local unit. All own protected consumers count. Future exams outside this acquisition contract, including a parent's synthesis assessment, do not reopen its valid recognition.

    Transitive minimality tests whether an authored requirement remains reachable after removing that edge and recomputing the effective relation, including inherited alternatives. A redundant shortcut does not retain a separate preparation obligation. See Graph Definition §7.3.

    4. Own local recognition and durable global mastery

    Using explicit M only, define

    \[ N_U=Reach_R^*(P_U\setminus M),\qquad I_U=Own(U)\setminus(M\cup P_U\cup N_U). \]

    Unpassed own protected roots retain their full transitive preparation, including through mastered intermediates. Only own ordinary targets outside that demand can enter \(I_U\), under a valid immutable recognition contract. An unrelated open card does not retain every ordinary target automatically. Orientation, where derivatively completed, remains an entry marker without an invented motivational interaction.

    Do not substitute T(U) for Own(U). Child recognition comes from the child's own acquisition contract. An open parent synthesis does not revoke child achievement; a passed parent synthesis does not pass an open child Q/K.

    Global effective mastery is

    \[ M_{\mathrm{eff}}=M\cup\bigcup_{U\in\mathcal D}I_U, \]

    where \(\mathcal D\) contains durably applicable authoritative unit contracts, including valid earlier contracts after a focus, curriculum or Personal-Curriculum change. Retain immutable versions, stable identities and original result attribution. Arbitrary scope visits cannot grant applicability or mastery.

    Implicit results are never fed back into \(I_U\). All Q/K outcomes are explicit; there is no recognition fixed point or second pass state. Direct adjacency traversal may avoid materializing all transitive pairs.

    A stable assessment ID binds stable recognition meaning. Task variants may preserve it. Expanded claims need a new assessment identity or a reviewed meaning-preserving migration; a new parent or graph revision must not reinterpret an old pass as evidence of additional competencies. Ambiguous historical meaning preserves the explicit result without granting expanded recognition. This requires no new per-learner version-eligibility outcome.

    5. What the assessments must establish

    A requires path proves reachability, not content coverage. Independent review inspects actual tasks and the normal pass rubric. For each own subject target a, derive its releasing protected set

    \[ D_U(a)=\{p\in P_U\mid(p,a)\in R^+\}. \]

    Targets with equal sets may share review of that release condition. Review each distinct releasing set and authorized task variant, rather than blindly all binary result subsets. The relevant assessments together must require independent application of each claimed essential competence. Cards are not application proof.

    The counterexample is concrete: can a learner pass the releasing assessment combination while entirely lacking a competence it would recognize? If yes, the contract fails. Points elsewhere must not compensate for a fully missing essential competence while still granting it. Revise tasks, normal rubric, claim or ownership boundary. This is static content QA, not a per-part stored pass or additional runtime qualification.

    Existing positive exam mastery may count without another pass if its legacy meaning supports this claim. A previous ordinary understanding result does not become an application-exam pass through a type conversion. Preserve original identities/results or introduce the stronger assessment identity. Existing curricula are not declared reviewed for that claim by this concept.

    6. Recursive completion and partial achievement

    \[ Complete(U)\iff Own(U)\subseteq M_{\mathrm{eff}} \ \land\ \bigwedge_{V\in Children(U)}Complete(V). \]

    Own Q/K need explicit outcomes and every required child must complete its own contract. Completion is derived, not a stored unit result or cluster mastery. A pure aggregate may complete through children alone. A parent with own synthesis also needs that explicit pass, even if it introduces no ordinary competence.

    Case Result
    Own \(Q\to B\to A\); M empty or only B mastered Q and its full transitive demand remain open.
    Same valid acquisition contract; Q passed A and B may be globally recognized by application.
    Local Q and S both require A; only Q passed S retains A unless valid direct/earlier recognition already establishes it.
    An unrelated required card stays open Unit completion stays open; independently established recognition remains valid.
    Child completed; parent synthesis open Child recognition stays globally valid; parent is incomplete.
    Parent synthesis passed; child exam/card open Child and parent remain incomplete; child obligations were not passed.
    Parent has no own exam and all required children complete A valid aggregate may complete without inventing another exam.

    Whole-unit completion is not a precondition for every individually valid recognition. Nor is completion itself a decision about technical entry. Learning-Unit Progression keeps regular order, any proposed access lock and direct assessment policy distinct.

    7. Assessment access and ordinary continuation

    Existing applicable assessments remain deliberately addressable without first mastering preparation. Authentication, learner revision, session ownership, task availability and normal grading remain required. Discovery is read-only; paging must not permanently remove assessments. An attempt is not a pass.

    Whether authored whole-unit prerequisites later enforce technical locks, and how such a mode treats direct exams, remains undecided. The conditional gated example is not a new universal restriction on existing assessment access.

    Ordinary prerequisites use \(M_{\mathrm{eff}}\) and full reachability before focus restriction. A mastered or outside-focus intermediate cannot hide an in-focus requirement. Normal mode ignores a wholly outside-focus valid prerequisite; strict mode checks globally. Invalid authored Level-2 references remain configuration errors. Voluntary repetition remains available without revoking credit or mutating authored requirements.

    8. Progress, plans and evidence boundaries

    For a fixed deduplicated target set G, total achievement and default plan IST use

    \[ IST(G)=|G\cap M_{\mathrm{eff}}|. \]

    The existing explicit-only plan-counting preference may use \(|G\cap M|\) for plan IST only; it changes neither global mastery nor prerequisite satisfaction or general progress. Weighted progress uses the same effective indicators. Current targets determine the denominator; outside support contributes nothing. Multiple placements and recursive child scopes count each canonical atom once.

    Daily/weekly histories and period quotas use reliably dated explicit outcome events only. Implicit recognition fabricates no date, threshold event or that day's performed work. A dated plan or calendar year is not another acquisition unit. Time-budget claims remain a separate optional planning profile.

    Acceptance must cover own/child ownership, all Q/K, full transitivity, invalid coverage, individual recognition before whole completion, parent synthesis/aggregation, stable global credit after scope changes, legacy meaning, normal/strict boundaries, direct exam access, card ownership, target deduplication and explicit-only event histories. Independent task/rubric review is separate from machine tests, real-host acceptance, usability and learning effectiveness.
